Numbers and Counting

Goal: By the end you can count to 100, ask and say someone's age, and read out a phone number.

Watch the lesson

This video has no sound: watch it, then say every sound out loud yourself.

Dialogue: Age and phone number

  • Alex
    ミカさんは なんさいですか。
    Mika-san wa nansai desu ka.
    How old are you, Mika?
  • Mika
    はたちです。アレックスさんは？
    Hatachi desu. Arekkusu-san wa?
    I'm twenty. And you, Alex?
  • Alex
    わたしも はたちです。
    Watashi mo hatachi desu.
    I'm twenty too.
  • Mika
    おなじですね！
    Onaji desu ne!
    We're the same age!
  • Alex
    でんわばんごうを おしえて ください。
    Denwa bangou o oshiete kudasai.
    Please tell me your phone number.
  • Mika
    はい。ゼロ はち ゼロ の いち に さん よん の ご ろく なな はち です。
    Hai. Zero hachi zero no ichi ni san yon no go roku nana hachi desu.
    Sure. It's 080-1234-5678.
  • Alex
    ありがとう ございます！
    Arigatou gozaimasu!
    Thank you!

Numbers 0 to 10

  • 0 (ゼロ / れい)zero / reizero
  • 1 (いち)ichione
  • 2 (に)nitwo
  • 3 (さん)santhree
  • 4 (よん / し)yon / shifour
  • 5 (ご)gofive
  • 6 (ろく)rokusix
  • 7 (なな / しち)nana / shichiseven
  • 8 (はち)hachieight
  • 9 (きゅう / く)kyuu / kunine
  • 10 (じゅう)juuten

Making bigger numbers

11 to 99 are built from 10

じゅういち (11), じゅうに (12), … じゅうきゅう (19). Then にじゅう (20 = 2 × 10), さんじゅう (30), よんじゅう (40), … きゅうじゅう (90). So 25 is にじゅうご (“twenty-five”) and 99 is きゅうじゅうきゅう.

Hundreds and thousands

ひゃく (100), せん (1,000), いちまん (10,000). Watch the exceptions: さんびゃく (300), ろっぴゃく (600), はっぴゃく (800), さんぜん (3,000), はっせん (8,000).

よん and なな are the everyday choices

Both 4 and 7 have two readings. In phone numbers and most counting, よん and なな are clearest. し and しち appear in set phrases such as しがつ (April) and しちじ (seven o'clock).

Age: 〜さい

Add さい after the number

ごさい (5), ろくさい (6), ななさい (7), きゅうさい (9). A few change: いっさい (1), はっさい (8), じゅっさい (10).

Twenty is special

20 years old is はたち, not にじゅっさい. After that, it's regular: にじゅういっさい (21), にじゅうにさい (22).

Asking age

なんさいですか (“How old are you?”) is fine with a friend. With an adult you've just met, some people prefer to avoid it, so use it with friends of a similar age.

Phone numbers

Say each digit and use の for the dash

080-1234-5678 is ゼロ はち ゼロ の いち に さん よん の ご ろく なな はち. Say the digits one by one, and link the groups with の. Zero is often said ゼロ in phone numbers (れい is also common).

ね at the end of a sentence

おなじですね means “it's the same, isn't it?”. ね seeks agreement or shows a shared feeling, like “right?” or “isn't it?” in English.
