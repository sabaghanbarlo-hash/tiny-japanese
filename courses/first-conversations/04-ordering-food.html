Ordering Food and Drinks

Goal: By the end you can order food and drink politely, count items, and finish a meal with the right phrases.

Grammar

X を ください — “Please give me X”

を marks the thing you're asking for. ラーメンを ください is direct and polite. You can add a count after: ラーメンを ふたつ ください (“Two ramen, please.”).

X を おねがいします — “X, please”

This is a softer, slightly more formal way to ask. In a restaurant, either one works; おねがいします is a safe all-purpose choice.

Counting with ひとつ, ふたつ…

This set of numbers counts general objects up to ten. Say the item first (を), then the number, then ください.

いただきます and ごちそうさまでした

Say いただきます before you eat and ごちそうさまでした when you finish. They are small thank-you rituals rather than word-for-word translations, and they are said at nearly every meal.

おいしいですね

とても おいしいです means “It's very delicious.” Adding ね (“isn't it?”) invites your friend to agree and makes the meal feel shared.
