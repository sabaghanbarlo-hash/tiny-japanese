Telling the Time and Days

Goal: By the end you can ask and tell the time, say today and tomorrow, and name the days of the week.

Dialogue: Time and days

  • Alex
    いま なんじですか。
    Ima nanji desu ka.
    What time is it now?
  • Mika
    いま さんじ はんです。
    Ima sanji han desu.
    It's half past three.
  • Alex
    えいがは なんじからですか。
    Eiga wa nanji kara desu ka.
    What time does the movie start?
  • Mika
    ごじ じゅっぷんからです。
    Goji juppun kara desu.
    It starts at 5:10.
  • Alex
    きょうは なんようびですか。
    Kyou wa nan'youbi desu ka.
    What day of the week is it today?
  • Mika
    きょうは すいようびです。
    Kyou wa suiyoubi desu.
    Today is Wednesday.
  • Alex
    あしたは？
    Ashita wa?
    And tomorrow?
  • Mika
    あしたは もくようびです。
    Ashita wa mokuyoubi desu.
    Tomorrow is Thursday.

Grammar

Hours: number + じ

Most hours are regular: ごじ (5), ろくじ (6). Three are special: よじ (4), しちじ (7), くじ (9).

Minutes: ふん or ぷん

Minutes change with the number. 2, 5, 7, 9 use ふん (にふん, ごふん, ななふん, きゅうふん). 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10 use ぷん (いっぷん, さんぷん, よんぷん, ろっぷん, はっぷん, じゅっぷん). Don't worry about memorising all of it at once: start with はん (“half past”) and じゅっぷん (“10 minutes”).

Half past: はん

さんじ はん means 3:30. Japanese often says はん instead of さんじゅっぷん.

a.m. and p.m. come first

ごぜん くじ is 9 a.m. and ごご さんじ is 3 p.m. Put the a.m./p.m. word in front of the time.

から means “starting from”

ごじからです means “It starts at 5.” In lesson 2 から meant “from a place”; here it means “from a time”.

Days of the week

Each day is named after a natural element: にち (sun), げつ (moon), か (fire), すい (water), もく (wood), きん (gold), ど (earth), plus ようび (“day of the week”).
