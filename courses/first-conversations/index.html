Course · Beginner

First Conversations

Ten everyday situations, from greetings to asking for help.

  • 10 lessons
  • about 4.5 hours
  • Speaking & listening

Ten dialogues with Mika and Alex that cover the situations you actually meet: greetings, introductions, numbers, ordering food, shopping, directions, time, likes, plans and asking for help. Each lesson has vocabulary, grammar, notes and a quiz.

Start lesson 1

What you'll learn

  • Greet people, introduce yourself and keep a short conversation going
  • Order food, shop and ask how much things cost
  • Ask for directions and understand the answer
  • Tell the time, make plans and invite a friend
  • Say when you don't understand and ask for help

Who it's for: Beginners who can read hiragana (take Hiragana Starter first if not).

Lessons

  1. 1Greetings Through the DaySay good morning, hello, good evening, thank you, sorry and goodbye.
  2. 2Introducing YourselfSay your name, where you're from, whether you're a student, and ask the same about someone else.
  3. 3Numbers and CountingCount from zero to a thousand, give your age and read out a phone number.
  4. 4Ordering Food and DrinksOrder a meal, ask for water, praise the food, and ask for the bill.
  5. 5Shopping and PricesAsk what something costs, point at things, compare prices and decide.
  6. 6Asking for DirectionsFind the station, the restroom or a shop, and understand “straight”, “left” and “right”.
  7. 7Telling the Time and DaysSay what time it is, when something starts, and the days of the week.
  8. 8Likes and DislikesTalk about what you like, love, and don't really like.
  9. 9Making Plans TogetherInvite someone, accept, agree on a time and place, and look forward to it.
  10. 10Asking for HelpSay you don't understand, ask people to repeat or slow down, and handle emergencies.
