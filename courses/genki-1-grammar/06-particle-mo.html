も: Also, Too

Goal: By the end you can say “me too” and link two similar sentences.

The lesson, step by step

What も means

も means “also” or “too”. It is a tiny word that saves you from repeating yourself. “Mika is a student. Alex is a student too.”

In Japanese you do not need to say “too” as a separate word. も sits right after the topic and does the job.

  • ミカさんは がくせい です。
    Mika-san wa gakusei desu.
    Mika is a student.
  • アレックスさんも がくせい です。
    Arekkusu-san mo gakusei desu.
    Alex is a student too.

も replaces は

When the new sentence has a new topic but the same comment, swap は for も. You never write both together. も takes the place of は.

The pattern becomes: topic, も, comment. The comment is the same as in the sentence before.

  • わたしは がくせい です。
    Watashi wa gakusei desu.
    I'm a student.
  • わたしも がくせい です。
    Watashi mo gakusei desu.
    I'm a student too.

The comment must be the same

も only works when what you say about the new topic is the same as before. If Mika is a student and Alex is a teacher, you cannot say “Alex too”, because the comments are different.

In that case, use は again, and the contrast is clear.

  • ミカさんは がくせい です。
    Mika-san wa gakusei desu.
    Mika is a student.
  • アレックスさんは せんせい です。
    Arekkusu-san wa sensei desu.
    Alex is a teacher. (different, so は)
  • ✗ アレックスさんも せんせい です。
    (wrong here)
    Wrong here: Mika is a student, not a teacher, so も does not fit.

“Me too”

A short way to say “me too” is わたしも です, or just わたしも. In natural speech you will often hear only the topic and も.

It is a very friendly thing to say when you meet someone with the same job, hobby or school.

  • わたしも です。
    Watashi mo desu.
    Me too.
  • わたしも。
    Watashi mo.
    Me too. (shorter)

も in questions

You can ask “is it also ...?” too. さくらさんも こうこうせい ですか asks whether Sakura is also a high school student. Just add か as usual.

The answer can repeat も: yes, Sakura is also one.

  • さくらさんも こうこうせい ですか。
    Sakura-san mo koukousei desu ka.
    Is Sakura a high school student too?
  • はい、さくらさんも こうこうせい です。
    Hai, Sakura-san mo koukousei desu.
    Yes, Sakura is a high school student too.

も takes the place of other particles too

も also replaces を: パンを たべます becomes パンも たべます, I also eat bread. It replaces が the same way.

With に and で it works differently: you add も after them, にも and でも. You will meet that later. For now, remember that も takes the place of は.

  • パンを たべます。
    Pan o tabemasu.
    I eat bread.
  • パンも たべます。
    Pan mo tabemasu.
    I also eat bread.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is writing both は and も after the same word. They do the same job, so only one can be there.

Do not write わたしは も or わたしも は. Just write わたしも.

  • ✗ わたしは も がくせい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: は and も cannot appear together.
  • ✗ わたしも は がくせい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: only one particle after the topic.
  • わたしも がくせい です。
    Watashi mo gakusei desu.
    Right: I'm a student too.
Try it: Say: “The teacher is Japanese too.”

せんせいも にほんじん です。

Sensei mo nihonjin desu.: The teacher is Japanese too.

Remember

  • も takes the place of は. Do not write ミカさんは も.
