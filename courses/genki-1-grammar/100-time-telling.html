Telling the Time

Goal: By the end you can say “It is 4:05 p.m.” and “The movie starts at half past five”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

Hours

Say the hour with the number plus じ: いちじ, にじ, さんじ. Three hours are special: よじ (4), しちじ (7) and くじ (9).

Do not say よんじ or きゅうじ.

  • いま さんじ です。
    Ima sanji desu.
    It is three o'clock now.
  • よじ、しちじ、くじ
    yoji, shichiji, kuji
    4:00, 7:00, 9:00

Minutes

Minutes use ふん or ぷん. The common ones are いっぷん (1), にふん (2), さんぷん (3), よんぷん (4), ごふん (5).

ろっぷん (6), ななふん (7), はっぷん (8), きゅうふん (9) and じゅっぷん (10) finish the list.

  • いま ごじ ごふん です。
    Ima goji gofun desu.
    It is 5:05 now.
  • ろくじ じゅっぷん です。
    Rokuji juppun desu.
    It is 6:10.

Half past

For “half past”, say はん after the hour. しちじ はん means “7:30”.

You do not need to say thirty minutes.

  • しちじ はん です。
    Shichiji han desu.
    It is 7:30.
  • じゅういちじ はんに ねます。
    Juuichiji han ni nemasu.
    I go to bed at 11:30.

Morning and afternoon

Put ごぜん before the time for morning (a.m.) and ごご for afternoon or evening (p.m.). ごぜん くじ is “9 a.m.”.

They go before the number, the opposite of English.

  • ごぜん くじに おきます。
    Gozen kuji ni okimasu.
    I get up at 9 a.m.
  • ごご よじ ごふん です。
    Gogo yoji gofun desu.
    It is 4:05 p.m.

Asking

Ask the hour with なんじ and the minutes with なんぷん. いま なんじ ですか means “What time is it now?”.

Use に when something happens at that time: ごじに かえります.

  • いま なんじ ですか。
    Ima nanji desu ka.
    What time is it now?
  • えいがは なんじから ですか。
    Eiga wa nanji kara desu ka.
    What time does the movie start?

Common mistakes

Do not say よんじ. Say よじ. Do not say ろくふん. Say ろっぷん.

Check the three special hours: 4, 7 and 9.

  • ✗ よんじ です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: say よじ.
  • よじ です。
    Yoji desu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “It is 4:30 p.m.”

ごご よじ はん です。

Gogo yoji han desu.: It is 4:30 p.m.

Remember

  • Four, seven and nine o'clock are yoji, shichiji and kuji.
  • Minutes change: ippun, sanpun, roppun, happun and juppun use pun.
  • Gozen and gogo go before the time.
