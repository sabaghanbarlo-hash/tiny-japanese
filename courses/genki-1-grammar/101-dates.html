Dates and Days of the Week

Goal: By the end you can say “My birthday is April 20th” and “It is Saturday”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

Months

Months are the number plus がつ: いちがつ (January), にがつ, さんがつ and so on up to じゅうにがつ.

Three are special: しがつ (April), しちがつ (July) and くがつ (September).

  • たんじょうびは しがつ です。
    Tanjoubi wa shigatsu desu.
    My birthday is in April.
  • しちがつ、くがつ
    shichigatsu, kugatsu
    July, September

Days 1 to 10

The first ten days have their own words: ついたち (1st), ふつか (2nd), みっか (3rd), よっか (4th), いつか (5th).

Then むいか (6th), なのか (7th), ようか (8th), ここのか (9th) and とおか (10th).

  • くがつ ついたち です。
    Kugatsu tsuitachi desu.
    It is September 1st.
  • じゅうがつ とおか です。
    Juugatsu tooka desu.
    It is October 10th.

Days after 10

After the 10th, most days are the number plus にち: じゅういちにち (11th), にじゅうさんにち (23rd).

Special ones: じゅうよっか (14th), はつか (20th) and にじゅうよっか (24th).

  • はつか です。
    Hatsuka desu.
    It is the 20th.
  • じゅうよっか です。
    Juuyokka desu.
    It is the 14th.

Days of the week

Days of the week end in ようび: にちようび (Sunday), げつようび, かようび, すいようび, もくようび, きんようび, どようび (Saturday).

To ask, say なんようび.

  • きょうは もくようび です。
    Kyou wa mokuyoubi desu.
    Today is Thursday.
  • あしたは なんようび ですか。
    Ashita wa nanyoubi desu ka.
    What day is tomorrow?

Asking and answering

Ask the date with いつ, なんがつ and なんにち. たんじょうびは いつ ですか means “When is your birthday?”.

Say month first, then day, then the day of the week: しがつ はつか、どようび.

  • たんじょうびは いつ ですか。
    Tanjoubi wa itsu desu ka.
    When is your birthday?
  • しがつ はつか です。
    Shigatsu hatsuka desu.
    It is April 20th.

Common mistakes

Do not say よんがつ. April is しがつ. Do not say やっか. The eighth is ようか.

Learn the special days one by one.

  • ✗ よんがつ です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: April is しがつ.
  • しがつ です。
    Shigatsu desu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “My birthday is March 2nd.”

たんじょうびは さんがつ ふつか です。

Tanjoubi wa sangatsu futsuka desu.: My birthday is March 2nd.

Story: Birthdays

  • Mika
    アレックスさんの たんじょうびは いつ ですか。
    Arekkusu-san no tanjoubi wa itsu desu ka.
    Alex, when is your birthday?
  • Alex
    くがつ とおか です。ミカさんは?
    Kugatsu tooka desu. Mika-san wa?
    September 10th. And yours, Mika?
  • Mika
    わたしは しがつ はつか です。
    Watashi wa shigatsu hatsuka desu.
    Mine is April 20th.
  • Alex
    なんようび ですか。
    Nanyoubi desu ka.
    What day of the week is it?
  • Mika
    どようび です。
    Doyoubi desu.
    It is a Saturday.
  • Alex
    じゃ、どようびに パーティーを しましょう。
    Ja, doyoubi ni paatii o shimashou.
    Then let's have a party on Saturday.

Remember

  • April, July and September are shigatsu, shichigatsu and kugatsu.
  • The first ten days and the 14th, 20th and 24th are special words.
  • Say month, then day, then day of the week.
