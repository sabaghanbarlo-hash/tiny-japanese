Verbs, Part 3: する and くる

Goal: By the end you know the two irregular verbs and can turn many nouns into verbs.

The lesson, step by step

Only two

Ichidan and godan cover almost every verb. Only two verbs follow their own rules: する, to do, and くる, to come. You simply memorise them.

They are among the most common verbs in the language, so you will not forget them for long.

  • する
    suru
    to do
  • くる
    kuru
    to come

する: to do

する means “to do”. You can use it by itself, as in しごとを する, to do work. But its most important use is attaching to nouns, which is the next step.

In the polite form, it becomes します.

  • しごとを する。
    Shigoto o suru.
    (I) do work.
  • テニスを する。
    Tenisu o suru.
    (I) play tennis.

Noun + する

This is a huge pattern. Take a noun that names an action and add する, and you get a verb. べんきょう, study, plus する is べんきょうする, to study.

Hundreds of words work this way, so once you learn one new noun, you often get a verb for free.

  • べんきょうする
    benkyou suru
    to study
  • さんぽする
    sanpo suru
    to take a walk
  • れんしゅうする
    renshuu suru
    to practise
  • でんわする
    denwa suru
    to make a phone call

くる: to come

くる means “to come”. It is irregular because the first sound changes: sometimes it is く, sometimes き, and sometimes こ. You will see each form as you learn more verb endings.

In the polite form it becomes きます.

  • ともだちが くる。
    Tomodachi ga kuru.
    A friend comes. (casual)
  • にほんに くる。
    Nihon ni kuru.
    (I) come to Japan. (casual)

What about いく?

いく, to go, is a regular godan verb in the forms you need now. It becomes いきます. It has one irregular form later, but you can ignore that for now.

So the only truly irregular verbs are する and くる.

  • いく
    iku
    to go
  • がっこうに いく。
    Gakkou ni iku.
    (I) go to school. (casual)

Story: A busy day

  • Alex
    きょう、なにを しますか。
    Kyou, nani o shimasu ka.
    What are you doing today?
  • Mika
    ともだちが きます。いっしょに べんきょうします。
    Tomodachi ga kimasu. Issho ni benkyou shimasu.
    A friend is coming. We'll study together.
  • Alex
    いいですね。わたしは さんぽします。
    Ii desu ne. Watashi wa sanpo shimasu.
    Nice. I'll take a walk.
  • Mika
    そのあと、れんしゅうも しますか。
    Sono ato, renshuu mo shimasu ka.
    Will you practise afterward too?
  • Alex
    はい、にほんごを れんしゅうします。
    Hai, nihongo o renshuu shimasu.
    Yes, I'll practise Japanese.

Remember

  • Only する and くる are irregular. Everything else is ichidan or godan.
  • Many nouns become verbs with する: べんきょう → べんきょうする.
  • いく is a regular godan verb for now.
