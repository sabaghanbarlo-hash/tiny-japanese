ます Form, Part 1: Ichidan Verbs

Goal: By the end you can make the polite form of any ichidan verb.

The lesson, step by step

What the ます form is

The ます form is the polite form of a verb. You use it with teachers, strangers, shop staff, and anyone you do not know well. It is the form to use in nearly every beginner conversation.

It covers the present and the future: たべます means “I eat” and “I will eat”.

  • たべます。
    Tabemasu.
    I eat. / I will eat.
  • みます。
    Mimasu.
    I watch. / I will watch.

The rule

For ichidan verbs the rule is the easiest of all. Take the dictionary form, drop the final る, and add ます.

たべる becomes たべ, plus ます: たべます. Nothing else changes.

  • たべる → たべます
    taberu → tabemasu
    to eat → eat (polite)
  • みる → みます
    miru → mimasu
    to watch → watch (polite)
  • おきる → おきます
    okiru → okimasu
    to wake up → wake up (polite)
  • ねる → ねます
    neru → nemasu
    to sleep → sleep (polite)

No change for the person

Just like です, the ます form never changes for I, you, he, she, we or they. わたしは たべます and ミカさんは たべます use the same verb.

Context tells you who is eating.

  • わたしは たべます。
    Watashi wa tabemasu.
    I eat.
  • ミカさんは たべます。
    Mika-san wa tabemasu.
    Mika eats.

In real sentences

Here are the verbs in full sentences. Notice how the verb always comes last. You already know を marks the object and に marks a time.

しちじに おきます means “I get up at seven”.

  • しちじに おきます。
    Shichiji ni okimasu.
    I get up at seven.
  • ごはんを たべます。
    Gohan o tabemasu.
    I eat a meal.
  • テレビを みます。
    Terebi o mimasu.
    I watch TV.
  • じゅういちじに ねます。
    Juuichiji ni nemasu.
    I go to sleep at eleven.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is keeping the る: たべるます is wrong. Drop る first, then add ます.

Do not add です after ます either. ます already makes the sentence polite.

  • ✗ たべるます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: drop る before adding ます.
  • ✗ たべます です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ます already makes it polite.
  • たべます。
    Tabemasu.
    Right: I eat.
Try it: What is the polite form of ねる (to sleep)?

ねます。

Nemasu.: I sleep.

Vocabulary

  • たべますtabemasueat
  • みますmimasusee, watch
  • おきますokimasuwake up
  • ねますnemasusleep, go to bed
  • あさごはんasagohanbreakfast
  • ニュースnyuusunews
  • なんじnanjiwhat time

Story: Mika's morning

  • Alex
    ミカさんは なんじに おきますか。
    Mika-san wa nanji ni okimasu ka.
    What time do you get up, Mika?
  • Mika
    しちじに おきます。
    Shichiji ni okimasu.
    I get up at seven.
  • Alex
    あさごはんを たべますか。
    Asagohan o tabemasu ka.
    Do you eat breakfast?
  • Mika
    はい、パンを たべます。
    Hai, pan o tabemasu.
    Yes, I eat bread.
  • Alex
    テレビも みますか。
    Terebi mo mimasu ka.
    Do you watch TV too?
  • Mika
    はい、ニュースを みます。
    Hai, nyuusu o mimasu.
    Yes, I watch the news.

Remember

  • Ichidan polite form: drop る, add ます.
  • ます is the polite present and future form.
  • ます is the same for every person.
