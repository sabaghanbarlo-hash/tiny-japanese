ます Form, Part 2: Godan Verbs

Goal: By the end you can make the polite form of any godan verb.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The rule

For godan verbs, take the dictionary form and change the last sound to its matching い-row sound. Then add ます.

のむ ends in む. The い-row partner of む is み. So you get のみ, plus ます: のみます.

  • のむ → のみます
    nomu → nomimasu
    to drink → drink (polite)
  • かく → かきます
    kaku → kakimasu
    to write → write (polite)
  • あそぶ → あそびます
    asobu → asobimasu
    to play → play (polite)
  • いく → いきます
    iku → ikimasu
    to go → go (polite)

The full table

Here are all the changes. う becomes い. く becomes き. ぐ becomes ぎ. す becomes し. つ becomes ち. ぬ becomes に. ぶ becomes び. む becomes み. る becomes り.

You do not need to memorise a table. Say the dictionary form, then say the vowel row slowly from u to i.

  • かう → かいます
    kau → kaimasu
    to buy → buy (polite)
  • およぐ → およぎます
    oyogu → oyogimasu
    to swim → swim (polite)
  • まつ → まちます
    matsu → machimasu
    to wait → wait (polite)
  • のる → のります
    noru → norimasu
    to ride → ride (polite)

Watch す and つ

Two changes look different from the others. す becomes し, so はなす becomes はなします. And つ becomes ち, so まつ becomes まちます.

Do not say はなすます or まつます.

  • はなす → はなします
    hanasu → hanashimasu
    to speak → speak (polite)
  • まつ → まちます
    matsu → machimasu
    to wait → wait (polite)

る verbs in this group

Godan verbs that end in る change it to り. のる becomes のります. わかる becomes わかります. And the exception かえる becomes かえります, not かえます.

That is why it matters to know which group a verb belongs to.

  • のる → のります
    noru → norimasu
    to ride → ride (polite)
  • わかる → わかります
    wakaru → wakarimasu
    to understand → understand (polite)
  • かえる → かえります
    kaeru → kaerimasu
    to return → return (polite)

In real sentences

Now put the verbs into sentences. Remember that the verb is last, and each noun carries its own particle.

コーヒーを のみます, I drink coffee. ともだちに あいます, I meet a friend.

  • コーヒーを のみます。
    Koohii o nomimasu.
    I drink coffee.
  • ともだちに あいます。
    Tomodachi ni aimasu.
    I meet a friend.
  • ほんを よみます。
    Hon o yomimasu.
    I read a book.
  • バスに のります。
    Basu ni norimasu.
    I ride the bus.

Common mistakes

Do not just add ます to the dictionary form. のむます is wrong. And do not treat かえる as ichidan: かえます is wrong, because it is godan.

Always change the last sound first.

  • ✗ のむます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: change む to み first.
  • ✗ かえます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: かえる is godan, so かえります.
  • のみます。
    Nomimasu.
    Right: I drink.
Try it: What is the polite form of はなす (to speak)?

はなします。

Hanashimasu.: I speak.

Vocabulary

  • のみますnomimasudrink
  • まちますmachimasuwait
  • のりますnorimasuride
  • よみますyomimasuread
  • あいますaimasumeet
  • はなしますhanashimasuspeak, talk
  • あるいて きますaruite kimasucome on foot

Story: At the cafe

  • Alex
    なにを のみますか。
    Nani o nomimasu ka.
    What will you drink?
  • Mika
    コーヒーを のみます。
    Koohii o nomimasu.
    I'll drink coffee.
  • Alex
    ここで ともだちを まちますか。
    Koko de tomodachi o machimasu ka.
    Will you wait for your friend here?
  • Mika
    はい、ここで まちます。
    Hai, koko de machimasu.
    Yes, I'll wait here.
  • Alex
    ともだちは バスに のりますか。
    Tomodachi wa basu ni norimasu ka.
    Does your friend take the bus?
  • Mika
    いいえ、あるいて きます。
    Iie, aruite kimasu.
    No, they come on foot.

Remember

  • Godan polite form: change the last u-sound to its i-sound, then add ます.
  • す → し and つ → ち are the changes to watch.
  • かえる is godan, so it is かえります.
