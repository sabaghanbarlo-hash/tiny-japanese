Present Tense: ます and ません

Goal: By the end you can say what you do, will do and do not do.

The lesson, step by step

One form, present and future

Japanese has no separate future tense. The ます form covers habits, general facts and the near future. あした いきます is “I will go tomorrow”, and まいにち いきます is “I go every day”.

Context, such as a time word, tells you which one is meant.

  • まいにち ほんを よみます。
    Mainichi hon o yomimasu.
    I read a book every day.
  • あした ほんを よみます。
    Ashita hon o yomimasu.
    I will read a book tomorrow.

Habits

For habits, add a word such as まいにち, every day, or まいあさ, every morning. The verb stays in the ます form.

This is how you describe your daily routine.

  • まいあさ コーヒーを のみます。
    Maiasa koohii o nomimasu.
    I drink coffee every morning.
  • まいにち べんきょうします。
    Mainichi benkyou shimasu.
    I study every day.

The near future

For the near future, add a time word like あした, tomorrow, or らいしゅう, next week. The verb is exactly the same.

If the time is not clear, the listener will assume a habit or a plan from the situation.

  • あした うちに かえります。
    Ashita uchi ni kaerimasu.
    I'll go home tomorrow.
  • らいしゅう きょうとへ いきます。
    Raishuu Kyouto e ikimasu.
    I'll go to Kyoto next week.

The negative: ません

To say “do not” or “will not”, replace ます with ません. たべます becomes たべません. The rest of the sentence stays the same.

Be careful with the spelling: it is ません, with the last sound ん.

  • たべます → たべません
    tabemasu → tabemasen
    eat → do not eat
  • コーヒーを のみません。
    Koohii o nomimasen.
    I don't drink coffee.
  • テレビを みません。
    Terebi o mimasen.
    I don't watch TV.

Answering yes or no

Answer a ます question by repeating the verb. For yes, say はい and the ます form. For no, say いいえ and the ません form.

You can drop the object if it is obvious.

  • コーヒーを のみますか。
    Koohii o nomimasu ka.
    Do you drink coffee?
  • はい、のみます。
    Hai, nomimasu.
    Yes, I do.
  • いいえ、のみません。
    Iie, nomimasen.
    No, I don't.

Not “right now”

Be careful: the ます form does not describe what is happening right now. たべます is not “I am eating at this moment”. That meaning uses a different form that you will learn later, the ている form.

For now, think of ます as habits and plans.

  • まいにち たべます。
    Mainichi tabemasu.
    I eat every day. (a habit)

Common mistakes

Do not add ない and ます together. たべないます is wrong. And do not put ません after ます: たべます ません is wrong.

The negative polite form replaces ます.

  • ✗ たべないます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use ません.
  • ✗ たべます ません。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ません replaces ます.
  • たべません。
    Tabemasen.
    Right: I don't eat.
Try it: Say: “I don't watch TV.”

テレビを みません。

Terebi o mimasen.: I don't watch TV.

Remember

  • ます covers habits, general facts and the near future.
  • Negative: replace ます with ません.
  • ます does not mean “right now”; that comes later.
