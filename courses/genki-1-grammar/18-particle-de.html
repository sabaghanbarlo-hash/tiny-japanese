で: Place of Action and Means

Goal: By the end you can say where you study and what you go by.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea: where and how

で answers two questions. Where does an action happen? And how, or with what, do you do it? Both are about the setting of an action.

Think of で as describing the stage and the tools of whatever the verb is doing.

  • としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    I study at the library. (where)
  • バスで いきます。
    Basu de ikimasu.
    I go by bus. (how)

Place of action

Put で after the place where an action happens: studying, eating, working, meeting. The place is the stage, and the verb is the performance.

The action is something you do there, not a movement toward the place.

  • としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    I study at the library.
  • レストランで たべます。
    Resutoran de tabemasu.
    I eat at a restaurant.
  • うちで テレビを みます。
    Uchi de terebi o mimasu.
    I watch TV at home.

Means: transport

で also marks what you travel by. バスで is by bus, でんしゃで is by train, and じてんしゃで is by bicycle.

Walking uses a different pattern, which you will learn later with the te-form.

  • バスで いきます。
    Basu de ikimasu.
    I go by bus.
  • でんしゃで いきます。
    Densha de ikimasu.
    I go by train.
  • じてんしゃで いきます。
    Jitensha de ikimasu.
    I go by bicycle.

Means: tools and languages

The same で marks tools and languages. You write with a pen, and you speak in Japanese. “With” in the sense of “using”.

Think of it as answering “how?”: by what means, with which tool, in which language.

  • にほんごで はなします。
    Nihongo de hanashimasu.
    I speak in Japanese.
  • ペンで かきます。
    Pen de kakimasu.
    I write with a pen.

で vs に

This is the classic mix-up. に marks the point you go to. で marks the place where an action happens. Going to the library is に. Studying in the library is で.

A sentence can use both: you meet a person, so the person takes に, and the meeting place takes で.

  • としょかんに いきます。
    Toshokan ni ikimasu.
    I go to the library.
  • としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    I study at the library.
  • としょかんで ともだちに あいます。
    Toshokan de tomodachi ni aimasu.
    I meet a friend at the library.

Questions: どこで and なにで

Ask where with どこで: where do you study? Ask how, or by what means, with なにで: how do you go?

The answer replaces the question word. どこで gets a place, and なにで gets a vehicle or a tool.

  • どこで べんきょうしますか。
    Doko de benkyou shimasu ka.
    Where do you study?
  • なにで いきますか。
    Nani de ikimasu ka.
    How do you go? (By what?)
  • バスで いきます。
    Basu de ikimasu.
    I go by bus.

Common mistakes

Do not use に for the place where you study or eat: that is で. And do not use を for the vehicle. You do not “go the bus”; you go バスで.

When you are unsure, ask: is this the place where the action happens, or the means of doing it? Then it is で.

  • ✗ としょかんに べんきょうします。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: studying is an action, so use で.
  • ✗ バスを いきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: the means takes で.
  • としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    Right: I study at the library.
Try it: Say: “I go to school by bicycle.”

じてんしゃで がっこうに いきます。

Jitensha de gakkou ni ikimasu.: I go to school by bicycle.

Remember

  • で marks where an action happens, and に marks where you go.
  • で also marks the means: バスで, じてんしゃで, にほんごで.
