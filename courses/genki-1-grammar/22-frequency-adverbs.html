Frequency Adverbs

Goal: By the end you can say how often you do something, and you know which words need a negative.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

How often?

Frequency adverbs say how often you do something. From most often to least often, you can use いつも, always. よく, often. ときどき, sometimes. あまり, not often. ぜんぜん, not at all.

They go right before the verb.

  • いつも
    itsumo
    always
  • よく
    yoku
    often
  • ときどき
    tokidoki
    sometimes
  • あまり
    amari
    not very often (needs a negative)
  • ぜんぜん
    zenzen
    not at all (needs a negative)

The positive ones

The first three are used with a normal ます verb. いつも コーヒーを のみます means “I always drink coffee”.

You can put them after the topic or the time word, right before the verb.

  • いつも コーヒーを のみます。
    Itsumo koohii o nomimasu.
    I always drink coffee.
  • よく えいがを みます。
    Yoku eiga o mimasu.
    I often watch movies.
  • ときどき りょうりを します。
    Tokidoki ryouri o shimasu.
    I sometimes cook.

The negative ones

あまり and ぜんぜん must be used with a negative verb. あまり のみません means “I don't drink much”. ぜんぜん のみません means “I never drink”, or “I don't drink at all”.

If you use them with a positive verb, the sentence is wrong.

  • あまり テレビを みません。
    Amari terebi o mimasen.
    I don't watch TV much.
  • ぜんぜん おさけを のみません。
    Zenzen osake o nomimasen.
    I never drink alcohol.

Where to put them

The usual position is right before the verb, after the other parts of the sentence. わたしは いつも バスで いきます means “I always go by bus”.

The topic, time, place and object come first. The adverb and the verb finish the sentence.

  • わたしは いつも バスで いきます。
    Watashi wa itsumo basu de ikimasu.
    I always go by bus.
  • ミカさんは よく としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Mika-san wa yoku toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    Mika often studies at the library.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is using あまり or ぜんぜん with a positive verb. あまり のみます is wrong. Use あまり のみません.

Also remember that they go before the verb, not at the end of the sentence.

  • ✗ あまり のみます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: あまり needs ません.
  • ✗ ぜんぜん みます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ぜんぜん needs ません.
  • あまり のみません。
    Amari nomimasen.
    Right: I don't drink much.
Try it: Say: “I don't watch TV much.”

あまり テレビを みません。

Amari terebi o mimasen.: I don't watch TV much.

Story: How often do you cook?

  • Alex
    ミカさんは よく りょうりを しますか。
    Mika-san wa yoku ryouri o shimasu ka.
    Do you cook often, Mika?
  • Mika
    いいえ、あまり しません。ときどき します。
    Iie, amari shimasen. Tokidoki shimasu.
    No, not much. I cook sometimes.
  • Alex
    いつも なにを たべますか。
    Itsumo nani o tabemasu ka.
    What do you always eat?
  • Mika
    いつも ごはんを たべます。
    Itsumo gohan o tabemasu.
    I always eat rice.
  • Alex
    パンは？
    Pan wa?
    And bread?
  • Mika
    パンは ぜんぜん たべません。
    Pan wa zenzen tabemasen.
    I never eat bread.

Remember

  • いつも, よく, ときどき go with a positive verb.
  • あまり and ぜんぜん must go with a negative verb.
  • The adverb goes right before the verb.
