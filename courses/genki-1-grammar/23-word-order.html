Word Order

Goal: By the end you can build a full sentence with time, place, object and verb.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The one rule

Japanese word order has one firm rule: the verb goes at the end of the sentence. Everything else can move a little, because each part carries a particle that marks its job.

English puts the verb in the middle. Japanese ends with it.

  • わたしは パンを たべます。
    Watashi wa pan o tabemasu.
    I eat bread.

The standard order

A common order is: topic, time, place, object, verb. わたしは, topic. まいあさ, time. うちで, place. コーヒーを, object. のみます, verb.

You do not need all the parts. Use the ones you need.

  • わたしは まいあさ うちで コーヒーを のみます。
    Watashi wa maiasa uchi de koohii o nomimasu.
    I drink coffee at home every morning.

Particles keep it clear

Because each part has a particle, the order is flexible. You can say the time first, or the object earlier. The listener still knows which word is the object, which is the place, and which is the time.

The verb still stays last.

  • まいあさ わたしは うちで コーヒーを のみます。
    Maiasa watashi wa uchi de koohii o nomimasu.
    Every morning I drink coffee at home.
  • コーヒーを まいあさ うちで のみます。
    Koohii o maiasa uchi de nomimasu.
    Coffee, every morning at home, I drink.

Time usually comes early

Time words usually come near the start of the sentence, often right after the topic. あした is a good example. Place usually comes after the time.

A simple habit to start with: time, place, object, verb.

  • あした がっこうで ともだちに あいます。
    Ashita gakkou de tomodachi ni aimasu.
    Tomorrow I'll meet a friend at school.
  • まいにち としょかんで ほんを よみます。
    Mainichi toshokan de hon o yomimasu.
    I read a book at the library every day.

Dropping what is clear

Japanese drops anything that is clear from context, especially the topic. コーヒーを のみます is a complete, natural sentence. You do not need to say “I”.

A good strategy is to build a sentence from the verb backward.

  • コーヒーを のみます。
    Koohii o nomimasu.
    (I) drink coffee.

Building a long sentence

Build a sentence step by step. Start with the verb: のみます. Add the object: コーヒーを のみます. Add the place: うちで コーヒーを のみます. Add the time: まいあさ うちで コーヒーを のみます.

Each new piece goes in front of the verb, with its own particle.

  • のみます。
    Nomimasu.
    (I) drink.
  • コーヒーを のみます。
    Koohii o nomimasu.
    (I) drink coffee.
  • うちで コーヒーを のみます。
    Uchi de koohii o nomimasu.
    (I) drink coffee at home.
  • まいあさ うちで コーヒーを のみます。
    Maiasa uchi de koohii o nomimasu.
    (I) drink coffee at home every morning.

Common mistakes

Do not put the verb in the middle of the sentence. のみます コーヒーを まいあさ is wrong. And do not forget the particle on each part.

If you feel lost, find the verb first, then check each word before it.

  • ✗ のみます コーヒーを まいあさ。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: the verb must be last.
  • ✗ ともだちを あいます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: あいます needs に.
  • ともだちに あいます。
    Tomodachi ni aimasu.
    Right: I meet a friend.
Try it: Say: “I read a book at the library every day.”

まいにち としょかんで ほんを よみます。

Mainichi toshokan de hon o yomimasu.: I read a book at the library every day.

Story: My weekly routine

  • Alex
    ミカさんの いちにちを おしえて ください。
    Mika-san no ichinichi o oshiete kudasai.
    Tell me about your day, Mika.
  • Mika
    まいあさ しちじに おきます。
    Maiasa shichiji ni okimasu.
    Every morning I get up at seven.
  • Mika
    うちで あさごはんを たべます。
    Uchi de asagohan o tabemasu.
    I eat breakfast at home.
  • Mika
    それから がっこうに いきます。
    Sorekara gakkou ni ikimasu.
    Then I go to school.
  • Alex
    ごごは なにを しますか。
    Gogo wa nani o shimasu ka.
    What do you do in the afternoon?
  • Mika
    としょかんで ともだちと べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de tomodachi to benkyou shimasu.
    I study with a friend at the library.

Remember

  • The verb always comes last.
  • A handy order: topic, time, place, object, verb.
  • Particles mark each part, so you can drop whatever is clear.
