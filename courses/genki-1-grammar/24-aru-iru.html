ある and いる: Saying What Exists

Goal: By the end you can say what there is in a place and choose between あります and います.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

Japanese has two verbs for “there is” and “exists”. あります is for things. います is for people and animals. The choice depends on whether the subject is alive and can move by itself.

These two verbs are the foundation of every sentence that says what is in a place.

  • ほんが あります。
    Hon ga arimasu.
    There is a book.
  • ねこが います。
    Neko ga imasu.
    There is a cat.

ある: things

Use あります for objects, and also for plants, events and ideas: anything that does not move by its own will. A book, a desk, a tree, a test.

The dictionary form is ある. The polite form is あります, which is an irregular-looking godan verb you can simply memorise.

  • へやに テレビが あります。
    Heya ni terebi ga arimasu.
    There is a TV in the room.
  • あした テストが あります。
    Ashita tesuto ga arimasu.
    There is a test tomorrow.

いる: people and animals

Use います for people and animals. The dictionary form is いる, which is an ichidan verb, so the polite form is います.

A robot is a thing, so you would use あります. But a person in a photograph is still a person, so います.

  • ミカさんが います。
    Mika-san ga imasu.
    Mika is here.
  • いぬが います。
    Inu ga imasu.
    There is a dog.

The pattern

To say what exists in a place, the pattern is: place, に, thing, が, verb. としょかんに ひとが います means “there are people in the library”.

に marks the place where something exists, and が marks what exists.

  • としょかんに ひとが います。
    Toshokan ni hito ga imasu.
    There are people in the library.
  • へやに つくえが あります。
    Heya ni tsukue ga arimasu.
    There is a desk in the room.

The negative

The negative of あります is ありません. The negative of います is いません. Both follow the normal ません rule.

These are the same ません endings you already know.

  • ほんが ありません。
    Hon ga arimasen.
    There is no book.
  • ねこが いません。
    Neko ga imasen.
    There is no cat.

Questions

To ask what is in a place, use なにが ありますか. To ask who is there, use だれが いますか. The question word takes が, just as you learned.

The answer replaces the question word.

  • ここに なにが ありますか。
    Koko ni nani ga arimasu ka.
    What is there here?
  • へやに だれが いますか。
    Heya ni dare ga imasu ka.
    Who is in the room?
  • ミカさんが います。
    Mika-san ga imasu.
    Mika is there.

に, not で

The place of existence takes に, not で. Remember, で is for where an action happens. Existing is not an action.

So you say へやに ミカさんが います, never へやで ミカさんが います.

  • ✗ へやで ミカさんが います。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: existence takes に.
  • へやに ミカさんが います。
    Heya ni Mika-san ga imasu.
    Right: Mika is in the room.

Common mistakes

Do not use あります for a person or an animal, and do not use います for an object. ねこが あります and ほんが います are both wrong.

If you are unsure, ask: is it alive? Then います.

  • ✗ ねこが あります。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: a cat is alive, so います.
  • ✗ ほんが います。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: a book is a thing, so あります.
  • ほんが あります。
    Hon ga arimasu.
    Right: There is a book.
Try it: Say: “There is a cat in the room.”

へやに ねこが います。

Heya ni neko ga imasu.: There is a cat in the room.

Story: What's in the room?

  • Alex
    ミカさんの へやに なにが ありますか。
    Mika-san no heya ni nani ga arimasu ka.
    What is in your room, Mika?
  • Mika
    つくえと ベッドが あります。
    Tsukue to beddo ga arimasu.
    There is a desk and a bed.
  • Alex
    テレビは ありますか。
    Terebi wa arimasu ka.
    Is there a TV?
  • Mika
    いいえ、テレビは ありません。
    Iie, terebi wa arimasen.
    No, there is no TV.
  • Alex
    ねこは いますか。
    Neko wa imasu ka.
    Is there a cat?
  • Mika
    はい、ねこが います。なまえは ミケです。
    Hai, neko ga imasu. Namae wa Mike desu.
    Yes, there is a cat. Its name is Mike.

Remember

  • あります is for things; います is for people and animals.
  • Place に Thing が あります/います.
  • Existence uses に, not で.
