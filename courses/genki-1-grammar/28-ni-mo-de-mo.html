にも and でも: Adding も to Place Particles

Goal: By the end you can say “also” and “not anywhere” correctly with place particles.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

You learned も as “also”. It can replace は, が and を. But it cannot replace に or で. Those stay, and も is added after them.

So に becomes にも, and で becomes でも.

  • ミカさんも いきます。
    Mika-san mo ikimasu.
    Mika goes too. (replaces は)
  • おんがくも ききます。
    Ongaku mo kikimasu.
    (I) listen to music too. (replaces を)

に + も

After a place or time marked with に, add も: にも. としょかんにも いきます means “I go to the library too”.

With existence, にも says there is something there as well.

  • としょかんにも いきます。
    Toshokan ni mo ikimasu.
    (I) go to the library too.
  • へやにも ほんが あります。
    Heya ni mo hon ga arimasu.
    There is a book in the room too.

で + も

After a place of action marked with で, add も: でも. うちでも べんきょうします means “I study at home too”.

The same works for a tool or a means.

  • うちでも べんきょうします。
    Uchi de mo benkyou shimasu.
    (I) study at home too.
  • カフェでも ほんを よみます。
    Kafe de mo hon o yomimasu.
    (I) read at a cafe too.

Negative: anywhere, anyone

With a question word plus も and a negative verb, you say “not anywhere”, “nothing” and “nobody”. どこにも いきません means “I am not going anywhere”.

なにも たべません means “I eat nothing”. だれも いません means “there is nobody”.

  • どこにも いきません。
    Doko ni mo ikimasen.
    (I) am not going anywhere.
  • なにも たべません。
    Nani mo tabemasen.
    (I) eat nothing.
  • だれも いません。
    Dare mo imasen.
    There is nobody.

Note: で and も together

でも here is で plus も. Do not confuse it with another でも that means “but”, or “even”, which you may meet later. Context tells you which.

Here we only use で plus も.

  • がっこうでも あそびます。
    Gakkou de mo asobimasu.
    (I) play at school too.
  • ここでも のみます。
    Koko de mo nomimasu.
    (I) drink here too.

Common mistakes

Do not replace に or で with も. としょかんも いきます sounds like “the library goes too”, which is wrong. Say としょかんにも.

Also do not use a positive verb with a question word plus も: it needs the negative.

  • ✗ としょかんも いきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: keep に: としょかんにも.
  • ✗ なにも たべます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: なにも needs a negative verb.
  • としょかんにも いきます。
    Toshokan ni mo ikimasu.
    Right: (I) go to the library too.
Try it: Say: “I am not going anywhere.”

どこにも いきません。

Doko ni mo ikimasen.: I am not going anywhere.

Vocabulary

  • にもnimoalso (place/time)
  • でもdemoalso (place of action)
  • どこにもdoko ni monowhere (with negative)
  • なにもnani monothing (with negative)
  • だれもdare monobody (with negative)
  • しゅうまつshuumatsuweekend
  • いっしょにissho nitogether
  • カフェkafecafe

Story: A quiet weekend

  • Mika
    しゅうまつ、どこかに いきましたか。
    Shuumatsu, doko ka ni ikimashita ka.
    Did you go anywhere at the weekend?
  • Alex
    いいえ、どこにも いきませんでした。
    Iie, doko ni mo ikimasen deshita.
    No, I did not go anywhere.
  • Mika
    うちで なにを しましたか。
    Uchi de nani o shimashita ka.
    What did you do at home?
  • Alex
    べんきょうしました。きょうも うちで べんきょうします。
    Benkyou shimashita. Kyou mo uchi de benkyou shimasu.
    I studied. Today too I will study at home.
  • Mika
    わたしも うちで べんきょうします。としょかんにも いきます。
    Watashi mo uchi de benkyou shimasu. Toshokan ni mo ikimasu.
    I will study at home too. I will go to the library as well.
  • Alex
    いっしょに いきましょう。
    Issho ni ikimashou.
    Let's go together.

Remember

  • も replaces は, が and を, but is added after に and で.
  • Question word + も + negative verb = nothing, nobody, nowhere.
  • Keep に and で: write にも and でも.
