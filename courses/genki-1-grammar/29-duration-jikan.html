～じかん: How Long

Goal: By the end you can say how long you do something and ask “how long?”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say how long something lasts, put the amount of time directly before the verb. Do not use a particle like に or を after it.

にじかん べんきょうしました means “I studied for two hours”.

  • にじかん べんきょうしました。
    Nijikan benkyou shimashita.
    (I) studied for two hours.
  • さんじかん ねました。
    Sanjikan nemashita.
    (I) slept for three hours.

Hours: じかん

For hours, add じかん to a number: いちじかん, にじかん, さんじかん, よじかん, ごじかん.

Be careful: four hours is よじかん, not よんじかん. And do not confuse じかん, a length of time, with じ, a clock time such as さんじ, three o'clock.

  • いちじかん
    ichijikan
    one hour
  • よじかん
    yojikan
    four hours
  • さんじ に あいます。
    Sanji ni aimasu.
    (We) meet at three o'clock. (clock time)

Minutes, days, weeks

Minutes use ふん or ぷん, for example じゅっぷん, ten minutes. Days use にち: ふつか is two days and みっか is three days. Weeks use しゅうかん: にしゅうかん is two weeks.

These counters have irregular sounds, so learn them as words and treat them as vocabulary.

  • にしゅうかん にほんに いました。
    Nishuukan Nihon ni imashita.
    (I) was in Japan for two weeks.
  • みっか やすみました。
    Mikka yasumimashita.
    (I) rested for three days.
  • じゅっぷん まちました。
    Juppun machimashita.
    (I) waited ten minutes.

Months and years

Months use かげつ: さんかげつ is three months. Years use ねん: にねん is two years.

Again, no particle before the verb.

  • さんかげつ にほんごを べんきょうしました。
    Sankagetsu nihongo o benkyou shimashita.
    (I) studied Japanese for three months.
  • にねん ここに いました。
    Ninen koko ni imashita.
    (I) was here for two years.

Asking: どのくらい

To ask how long, use どのくらい. どのくらい べんきょうしましたか means “how long did you study?”. You can also say なんじかん, how many hours.

Answer with an amount of time.

  • どのくらい べんきょうしましたか。
    Dono kurai benkyou shimashita ka.
    How long did you study?
  • なんじかん ねましたか。
    Nanjikan nemashita ka.
    How many hours did you sleep?
  • ごじかん ねました。
    Gojikan nemashita.
    (I) slept five hours.

Approximately: くらい

Add くらい after an amount to say “about”. さんじかんくらい means “about three hours”.

It is common in daily conversation because we rarely give exact times.

  • さんじかんくらい かかります。
    Sanjikan kurai kakarimasu.
    It takes about three hours.
  • ごふんくらい まちました。
    Gofun kurai machimashita.
    (I) waited about five minutes.

Common mistakes

Do not add を or に after an amount of time. にじかんを べんきょうしました is wrong. And say よじかん, not よんじかん.

Do not mix up じ for clock time and じかん for length.

  • ✗ にじかんに べんきょうしました。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: no particle after the amount.
  • ✗ よんじかん ねました。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: four hours is よじかん.
  • にじかん べんきょうしました。
    Nijikan benkyou shimashita.
    Right: (I) studied for two hours.
Try it: Say: “I slept for six hours.”

ろくじかん ねました。

Rokujikan nemashita.: I slept for six hours.

Vocabulary

  • じかんjikanhours (length of time)
  • ふん / ぷんfun / punminutes
  • しゅうかんshuukanweeks
  • かげつkagetsumonths
  • ねんnenyears
  • どのくらいdono kuraihow long, how much
  • くらいkuraiabout

    How long did you study yesterday?

    Rokujikan nemashita. Mika-san wa?
    Six hours. And you?

    Watashi wa hachijikan nemashita.

    Lucky you.

Remember

  • An amount of time goes straight before the verb, with no particle.
  • Four hours is よじかん.
  • Ask with どのくらい or なんじかん; add くらい for “about”.
