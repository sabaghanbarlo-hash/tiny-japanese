たくさん: Saying “a Lot”

Goal: By the end you can use たくさん, すこし and ぜんぜん in sentences.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

たくさん means “a lot” or “many”. It works with both things you can count and amounts of things, and with how much someone does something.

It is an adverb, so it goes before the verb and needs no particle.

  • ほんが たくさん あります。
    Hon ga takusan arimasu.
    There are a lot of books.
  • たくさん たべました。
    Takusan tabemashita.
    (I) ate a lot.

Position in the sentence

Put たくさん right before the verb. ほんを たくさん よみます means “I read a lot of books”.

You do not say の between the noun and たくさん, as in English “a lot of”. The adverb stands on its own.

  • ほんを たくさん よみます。
    Hon o takusan yomimasu.
    (I) read a lot of books.
  • ひとが たくさん います。
    Hito ga takusan imasu.
    There are a lot of people.

The opposite: すこし

すこし means “a little”. すこし のみました means “I drank a little”. It also goes before the verb.

ちょっと is a casual way to say “a little” and is very common in speech.

  • すこし たべました。
    Sukoshi tabemashita.
    (I) ate a little.
  • ちょっと まって ください。
    Chotto matte kudasai.
    Please wait a moment.

Not at all: ぜんぜん

ぜんぜん means “not at all” and goes with a negative verb. ぜんぜん たべません means “I do not eat at all”.

あまり, “not very”, also goes with a negative verb. あまり たべません means “I do not eat much”.

  • ぜんぜん のみません。
    Zenzen nomimasen.
    (I) do not drink at all.
  • あまり たべません。
    Amari tabemasen.
    (I) do not eat much.

A scale

Here is the scale. たくさん, a lot. すこし, a little. あまり and negative, not much. ぜんぜん and negative, not at all.

Remember that the last two need a negative verb.

  • たくさん たべます。
    Takusan tabemasu.
    (I) eat a lot.
  • すこし たべます。
    Sukoshi tabemasu.
    (I) eat a little.
  • あまり たべません。
    Amari tabemasen.
    (I) do not eat much.
  • ぜんぜん たべません。
    Zenzen tabemasen.
    (I) do not eat at all.

Common mistakes

Do not say たくさんの ほん here when you are learning the basic pattern; ほんが たくさん is simpler and always safe. And do not use ぜんぜん with a positive verb.

Do not use たくさん for a person's age or for exact numbers.

  • ✗ ぜんぜん たべます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ぜんぜん needs a negative.
  • ほんが たくさん あります。
    Hon ga takusan arimasu.
    Right: There are a lot of books.
Try it: Say: “I do not drink coffee at all.”

コーヒーを ぜんぜん のみません。

Koohii o zenzen nomimasen.: I do not drink coffee at all.

Story: At the market

  • Alex
    ミカさん、やさいを たくさん かいましたね。
    Mika-san, yasai o takusan kaimashita ne.
    Mika, you bought a lot of vegetables.
  • Mika
    はい。わたしは やさいが すきです。
    Hai. Watashi wa yasai ga suki desu.
    Yes. I like vegetables.
  • Alex
    にくも かいましたか。
    Niku mo kaimashita ka.
    Did you buy meat too?
  • Mika
    いいえ、すこしだけ。あまり たべません。
    Iie, sukoshi dake. Amari tabemasen.
    No, only a little. I do not eat much.
  • Alex
    わたしは さかなを ぜんぜん たべません。
    Watashi wa sakana o zenzen tabemasen.
    I do not eat fish at all.
  • Mika
    そうですか。
    Sou desu ka.
    Is that so?

Remember

  • たくさん goes before the verb, with no particle.
  • ぜんぜん and あまり need a negative verb.
  • Scale: たくさん, すこし, あまり ～ません, ぜんぜん ～ません.
