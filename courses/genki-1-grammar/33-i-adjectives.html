い-Adjectives: Describing Things

Goal: By the end you can recognise an い-adjective and say that something is big, small, new, old and more.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

An adjective is a word that describes a noun, such as big, small or delicious. Japanese has two kinds of adjectives, and this lesson covers the first kind: the い-adjectives.

You can spot an い-adjective because it ends in the sound い when written in hiragana.

  • おおきい
    ookii
    big
  • ちいさい
    chiisai
    small
  • あたらしい
    atarashii
    new

Say it with です

To say that something is big, put です after the adjective. これは おおきい です means “this is big”.

The です makes the sentence polite. With an い-adjective it does not change the meaning, it only adds politeness.

  • これは おおきい です。
    Kore wa ookii desu.
    This is big.
  • へやは ひろい です。
    Heya wa hiroi desu.
    The room is spacious.
  • ラーメンは おいしい です。
    Raamen wa oishii desu.
    The ramen is delicious.

Common い-adjectives

Learn adjectives in pairs. おおきい, big, and ちいさい, small. あたらしい, new, and ふるい, old. たかい, expensive or tall, and やすい, cheap.

More pairs: あつい, hot, and さむい, cold. おもしろい, interesting, and つまらない, boring.

  • たかい
    takai
    expensive, tall
  • やすい
    yasui
    cheap
  • あつい
    atsui
    hot (weather)
  • さむい
    samui
    cold (weather)

Questions

To ask what something is like, use どう. このラーメンは どうですか means “how is this ramen?”.

Answer with an adjective: おいしい です.

  • このラーメンは どうですか。
    Kono raamen wa dou desu ka.
    How is this ramen?
  • おいしい です。
    Oishii desu.
    It's delicious.
  • すこし たかい です。
    Sukoshi takai desu.
    It's a little expensive.

Degree words

Put とても, very, or すこし, a little, before the adjective. とても おおきい です means “it is very big”.

Degree words always go directly before the adjective.

  • とても おおきい です。
    Totemo ookii desu.
    It is very big.
  • すこし さむい です。
    Sukoshi samui desu.
    It is a little cold.
  • とても おもしろい です。
    Totemo omoshiroi desu.
    It is very interesting.

The tricky ones

いい, good, is an い-adjective. Later you will see that it changes in a special way. きれい, pretty or clean, ends in い but belongs to the other group, the な-adjectives, so be careful.

That is why you must learn each adjective together with its group.

  • いい てんき です。
    Ii tenki desu.
    It's good weather.
  • きれい です。
    Kirei desu.
    It's pretty.

Common mistakes

Do not put な or の between an い-adjective and the noun or です. おおきい な です is wrong. Also do not drop the final い: おおき です is wrong.

The adjective is complete as it is.

  • ✗ おおきい な です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: no な after い-adjectives.
  • ✗ おおき です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: keep the final い.
  • おおきい です。
    Ookii desu.
    Right: It is big.
Try it: Say: “This is very cheap.”

これは とても やすい です。

Kore wa totemo yasui desu.: This is very cheap.

Remember

  • い-adjectives end in い and can be followed by です.
  • Never add な or の after an い-adjective.
  • Learn each adjective with its group: きれい looks like い but is not.
