な-Adjectives: The Other Group

Goal: By the end you can recognise a な-adjective and say that something is quiet, pretty, famous or easy.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

The second group is the な-adjectives. They do not end in い. They behave much like nouns: they take です directly.

しずか means quiet. げんき means healthy and energetic. ゆうめい means famous. かんたん means easy.

  • しずか
    shizuka
    quiet
  • ゆうめい
    yuumei
    famous
  • かんたん
    kantan
    easy, simple

Say it with です

With です, a な-adjective looks just like an い-adjective. ここは しずか です means “it is quiet here”.

The difference only shows up when the adjective changes, for example in the negative or before a noun.

  • ここは しずか です。
    Koko wa shizuka desu.
    It is quiet here.
  • この まちは にぎやか です。
    Kono machi wa nigiyaka desu.
    This town is lively.
  • しけんは かんたん です。
    Shiken wa kantan desu.
    The test is easy.

More な-adjectives

きれい, pretty or clean. すき, liked. きらい, disliked. じょうず, skilled. へた, unskilled. べんり, convenient. ひま, free, not busy.

Many of these come from Chinese and are close to nouns in grammar.

  • きれい
    kirei
    pretty, clean
  • べんり
    benri
    convenient
  • ひま
    hima
    free (time)
  • じょうず
    jouzu
    skilled

Tell the groups apart

If an adjective does not end in い, it is a な-adjective. If it ends in い, it is usually an い-adjective.

But a few な-adjectives do end in い. The most important are きれい, pretty, and きらい, disliked. Treat them as な-adjectives.

  • きれい
    kirei
    pretty (な-adjective!)
  • きらい
    kirai
    disliked (な-adjective!)
  • おおきい
    ookii
    big (い-adjective)

Questions

To ask what kind of thing it is, use どんな. どんな まちですか means “what kind of town is it?”. To ask how it is, use どう.

Answer with any adjective.

  • どんな まちですか。
    Donna machi desu ka.
    What kind of town is it?
  • しずかな まちです。
    Shizuka na machi desu.
    It is a quiet town.
  • まちは どうですか。
    Machi wa dou desu ka.
    How is the town?

Common mistakes

Do not add い to a な-adjective. しずかい です is wrong. And do not treat きれい as an い-adjective just because it ends in い.

Remember this group when we change adjectives in the next lessons.

  • ✗ しずかい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: しずか has no い.
  • ✗ ゆうめい い です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: no い to add.
  • しずか です。
    Shizuka desu.
    Right: It is quiet.
Try it: Say: “This town is lively.”

この まちは にぎやか です。

Kono machi wa nigiyaka desu.: This town is lively.

Story: A quiet town

  • Alex
    ミカさんの まちは どんな まちですか。
    Mika-san no machi wa donna machi desu ka.
    What kind of town is yours, Mika?
  • Mika
    しずかで きれいな まちです。
    Shizuka de kirei na machi desu.
    It is a quiet, pretty town.
  • Alex
    べんりですか。
    Benri desu ka.
    Is it convenient?
  • Mika
    はい、とても べんりです。
    Hai, totemo benri desu.
    Yes, very convenient.
  • Alex
    ゆうめいな ところが ありますか。
    Yuumei na tokoro ga arimasu ka.
    Are there famous places?
  • Mika
    ゆうめいな おてらが あります。
    Yuumei na otera ga arimasu.
    There is a famous temple.

Remember

  • な-adjectives do not end in い (except a few like きれい).
  • With です they look like い-adjectives; they differ elsewhere.
  • Never add い to a な-adjective.
