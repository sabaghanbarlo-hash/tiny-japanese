Adjectives Before Nouns

Goal: By the end you can describe a noun with either kind of adjective.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

Until now an adjective came before です. Now you will put it in front of a noun, as in English: “a big house”, “a quiet town”.

Japanese does this the same way English does: the describing word comes first, then the noun.

  • おおきい いえ
    ookii ie
    a big house
  • しずかな まち
    shizuka na machi
    a quiet town

い-adjectives: nothing to add

With an い-adjective, just place it directly before the noun. It does not change at all. あたらしい くるま means “a new car”.

That is why they are the easy group.

  • あたらしい くるま
    atarashii kuruma
    a new car
  • やすい ほん
    yasui hon
    a cheap book
  • おいしい ラーメン
    oishii raamen
    delicious ramen

な-adjectives: add な

With a な-adjective you must add な between the adjective and the noun. That is exactly where the name of the group comes from. しずかな まち means “a quiet town”.

Without the な, the sentence is wrong.

  • しずかな まち
    shizuka na machi
    a quiet town
  • ゆうめいな ひと
    yuumei na hito
    a famous person
  • べんりな みせ
    benri na mise
    a convenient shop

In a sentence

Describing phrases fit into any sentence you already know. おおきい いえが あります means “there is a big house”. しずかな まちに すんでいます would be “I live in a quiet town”, but we will study that verb form later.

For now, practice with です and あります.

  • おおきい いえが あります。
    Ookii ie ga arimasu.
    There is a big house.
  • きれいな ともだちが います。
    Kirei na tomodachi ga imasu.
    (I) have a pretty friend.
  • これは ふるい ほん です。
    Kore wa furui hon desu.
    This is an old book.

Two adjectives

You can use more than one adjective. Put them one after another before the noun. おおきくて is a form you will learn later, so for now just keep to one adjective per noun.

A simple way to add detail is a degree word: とても おおきい いえ, a very big house.

  • とても おおきい いえ
    totemo ookii ie
    a very big house
  • とても きれいな へや
    totemo kirei na heya
    a very pretty room

Common mistakes

Do not leave out な with a な-adjective: しずか まち is wrong. And do not add な to an い-adjective: おおきい な いえ is wrong.

Remember きれい: it is a な-adjective, so you say きれいな.

  • ✗ しずか まち
    (wrong)
    Wrong: add な.
  • ✗ おおきい な いえ
    (wrong)
    Wrong: no な after い-adjectives.
  • しずかな まち
    shizuka na machi
    Right: a quiet town.
Try it: Say: “a pretty room.”

きれいな へや

kirei na heya: a pretty room

Story: Looking for a flat

  • Alex
    どんな へやが すきですか。
    Donna heya ga suki desu ka.
    What kind of room do you like?
  • Mika
    あかるい へやが すきです。
    Akarui heya ga suki desu.
    I like a bright room.
  • Alex
    この へやは どうですか。
    Kono heya wa dou desu ka.
    How about this room?
  • Mika
    しずかな へやですね。
    Shizuka na heya desu ne.
    It's a quiet room.
  • Alex
    あたらしい ベッドも あります。
    Atarashii beddo mo arimasu.
    There is a new bed too.
  • Mika
    この へやに します。
    Kono heya ni shimasu.
    I will take this room.

Remember

  • An い-adjective goes straight before the noun, unchanged.
  • A na-adjective needs na between it and the noun: shizuka na machi.
  • Never add na to an i-adjective.
