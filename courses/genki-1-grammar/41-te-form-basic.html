て-Form: Ichidan and Irregular Verbs

Goal: By the end you can make the て-form of every ichidan verb and the two irregular verbs.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

The て-form is a verb form that works like a connector. On its own it does not tell the time or the politeness. It appears inside many patterns: asking someone to do something, giving permission, describing what you are doing right now, and joining actions together.

That is why this form is so important: dozens of grammar points are built on it.

  • たべます → たべて
    tabemasu → tabete
    eat → eating, and
  • します → して
    shimasu → shite
    do → doing, and

Ichidan verbs

For an ichidan verb, the rule is the simplest of all: drop the final る of the dictionary form and add て. たべる becomes たべて. みる becomes みて. おきる becomes おきて.

Remember how to spot an ichidan verb: it ends in える or いる, like たべる or みる.

  • たべる → たべて
    taberu → tabete
    eat
  • みる → みて
    miru → mite
    see, watch
  • おきる → おきて
    okiru → okite
    wake up
  • ねる → ねて
    neru → nete
    sleep

More ichidan

あける, open, becomes あけて. しめる, close, becomes しめて. かける, hang or make a call, becomes かけて. います, from いる, becomes いて.

The rule never changes for this group.

  • あける → あけて
    akeru → akete
    open
  • しめる → しめて
    shimeru → shimete
    close
  • いる → いて
    iru → ite
    be (alive)

Irregular verbs

There are only two irregular verbs and each has its own form. する becomes して. くる becomes きて.

Memorise these two; they are used constantly. べんきょうする becomes べんきょうして.

  • する → して
    suru → shite
    do
  • くる → きて
    kuru → kite
    come
  • べんきょうする → べんきょうして
    benkyou suru → benkyou shite
    study

Ending with て

Whatever the verb, the form ends in て or で. For this group it is always て. That sound is the hallmark of this form.

In the next lesson you will meet the godan verbs, where the ending can also be で.

  • みて
    mite
    watch (te-form)
  • たべて
    tabete
    eat (te-form)
  • きて
    kite
    come (te-form)

Common mistakes

Do not forget to drop る: たべるて is wrong. And do not mix up the two irregular verbs: する is して, くる is きて.

Do not use the ます stem form by mistake: たべます て is wrong.

  • ✗ たべるて
    (wrong)
    Wrong: drop る first.
  • ✗ くる → くて
    (wrong)
    Wrong: くる becomes きて.
  • たべる → たべて
    taberu → tabete
    Right: eat → tabete
Try it: Make the て-form of みる (see).

mite: mite

Vocabulary

  • たべてtabeteeat (て-form)
  • みてmitesee (て-form)
  • おきてokitewake up (て-form)
  • ねてnetesleep (て-form)
  • してshitedo (て-form)
  • きてkitecome (て-form)
  • あさごはんasagohanbreakfast
  • いそがしいisogashiibusy

Story: A busy morning

  • Mika
    アレックスさん、きのうは なんじに おきましたか。
    Arekkusu-san, kinou wa nanji ni okimashita ka.
    Alex, what time did you get up yesterday?
  • Alex
    ろくじに おきて、あさごはんを たべました。
    Rokuji ni okite, asagohan o tabemashita.
    I got up at six and ate breakfast.
  • Mika
    それから なにを しましたか。
    Sorekara nani o shimashita ka.
    Then what did you do?
  • Alex
    テレビを みて、べんきょうして、がっこうへ いきました。
    Terebi o mite, benkyou shite, gakkou e ikimashita.
    I watched TV, studied, and went to school.
  • Mika
    いそがしい あさ ですね。
    Isogashii asa desu ne.
    That is a busy morning.
  • Alex
    はい、とても いそがしかった です。
    Hai, totemo isogashikatta desu.
    Yes, it was very busy.

Remember

  • Ichidan verbs: drop ru and add te.
  • Suru becomes shite; kuru becomes kite.
  • The te-form is the base for many later patterns.
