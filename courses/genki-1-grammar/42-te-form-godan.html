て-Form: Godan Verbs

Goal: By the end you can make the て-form of any godan verb from its dictionary form.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

Godan verbs are the other big group of verbs. To make the て-form of a godan verb, you look at the last sound of the dictionary form, and that sound decides the ending. There are four patterns. Learn them by heart, because they are used all the time.

A quick tip: sing the pattern as a rhyme and repeat it until it feels automatic.

  • かう → かって
    kau → katte
    buy
  • のむ → のんで
    nomu → nonde
    drink
  • かく → かいて
    kaku → kaite
    write
  • はなす → はなして
    hanasu → hanashite
    speak

Pattern 1: う, つ, る

If the verb ends in う, つ or る, replace that ending with って. かう, buy, becomes かって. まつ, wait, becomes まって. のる, ride, becomes のって.

The little っ makes a short pause before the て.

  • かう → かって
    kau → katte
    buy
  • まつ → まって
    matsu → matte
    wait
  • のる → のって
    noru → notte
    ride
  • かえる → かえって
    kaeru → kaette
    return

Pattern 2: む, ぶ, ぬ

If the verb ends in む, ぶ or ぬ, replace it with んで. のむ, drink, becomes のんで. あそぶ, play, becomes あそんで. しぬ, die, becomes しんで.

Notice that this time the ending is で, not て.

  • のむ → のんで
    nomu → nonde
    drink
  • あそぶ → あそんで
    asobu → asonde
    play
  • よむ → よんで
    yomu → yonde
    read
  • よぶ → よんで
    yobu → yonde
    call

Pattern 3: く and ぐ

If the verb ends in く, change it to いて. かく, write, becomes かいて. きく, listen, becomes きいて.

If it ends in ぐ, change it to いで. およぐ, swim, becomes およいで.

  • かく → かいて
    kaku → kaite
    write
  • きく → きいて
    kiku → kiite
    listen
  • およぐ → およいで
    oyogu → oyoide
    swim
  • いそぐ → いそいで
    isogu → isoide
    hurry

The exception: いく

The verb いく, go, is an exception. It should be いいて, but it is いって. Memorise this one because いく is extremely common.

No other verb breaks the rule for く.

  • いく → いって
    iku → itte
    go
  • ✗ いく → いいて
    (wrong)
    Wrong: いく is an exception.

Pattern 4: す

If the verb ends in す, change it to して. はなす, speak, becomes はなして. かす, lend, becomes かして. だす, take out, becomes だして.

This pattern is just like する becoming して.

  • はなす → はなして
    hanasu → hanashite
    speak
  • かす → かして
    kasu → kashite
    lend
  • だす → だして
    dasu → dashite
    take out

Common mistakes

Do not treat a godan verb like an ichidan verb. のむ is のんで, not のめて. Some verbs ending in る are godan, like かえる, return, and はいる, enter. They become かえって and はいって.

If the form ends in る and you are not sure, check the vowel before it and learn the exceptions as you meet them.

  • ✗ のむ → のめて
    (wrong)
    Wrong: む becomes んで.
  • ✗ かえる → かえて
    (wrong)
    Wrong: かえる is godan, so かえって.
  • のむ → のんで
    nomu → nonde
    Right: drink → nonde
Try it: Make the て-form of かく (write).

かいて

kaite: kaite

Vocabulary

  • かってkattebuy (て-form)
  • のんでnondedrink (て-form)
  • かいてkaitewrite (て-form)
  • はなしてhanashitespeak (て-form)
  • いってittego (て-form)
  • およいでoyoideswim (て-form)
  • あそんでasondeplay (て-form)
  • てがみtegamiletter

Story: Weekend plans

  • Mika
    きのうの ばん、なにを しましたか。
    Kinou no ban, nani o shimashita ka.
    What did you do last night?
  • Alex
    ほんを よんで、てがみを かいて、ねました。
    Hon o yonde, tegami o kaite, nemashita.
    I read a book, wrote a letter, and slept.
  • Mika
    しゅうまつは どうしますか。
    Shuumatsu wa dou shimasu ka.
    What will you do at the weekend?
  • Alex
    ともだちと あそんで、ごはんを たべます。
    Tomodachi to asonde, gohan o tabemasu.
    I will play with friends and eat.
  • Mika
    わたしは うみで およいで、はなして、かえります。
    Watashi wa umi de oyoide, hanashite, kaerimasu.
    I will swim at the beach, talk, and go home.
  • Alex
    たのしそうですね。
    Tanoshisou desu ne.
    That sounds fun.

Remember

  • u, tsu, ru become tte. mu, bu, nu become nde.
  • ku becomes ite, gu becomes ide, su becomes shite.
  • Iku is the exception: itte, not iite.
