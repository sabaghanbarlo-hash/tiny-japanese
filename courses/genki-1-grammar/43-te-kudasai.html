～て ください: Asking Politely

Goal: By the end you can make polite requests and know when to add ませんか instead.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

ください means “please give me”. When you add it after the て-form of a verb, it becomes a polite request: “please do it”. みて ください means “please look”.

This is the most common way to ask someone to do something in polite Japanese.

  • みて ください。
    Mite kudasai.
    Please look.
  • たべて ください。
    Tabete kudasai.
    Please eat.

With objects

Put the object before the verb with を. ほんを よんで ください means “please read the book”.

Everything else follows the normal word order you already know.

  • ほんを よんで ください。
    Hon o yonde kudasai.
    Please read the book.
  • なまえを かいて ください。
    Namae o kaite kudasai.
    Please write your name.
  • ドアを あけて ください。
    Doa o akete kudasai.
    Please open the door.

Useful requests

ちょっと まって ください, please wait a moment. ゆっくり はなして ください, please speak slowly. もういちど いって ください, please say it once more.

These three are especially handy when you are learning Japanese.

  • ちょっと まって ください。
    Chotto matte kudasai.
    Please wait a moment.
  • ゆっくり はなして ください。
    Yukkuri hanashite kudasai.
    Please speak slowly.
  • もういちど いって ください。
    Mou ichido itte kudasai.
    Please say it once more.

Ask for a thing

When you want something, not an action, use を ください directly after the noun. みずを ください means “please give me water”. This does not use the て-form.

It is the form used in shops and restaurants.

  • みずを ください。
    Mizu o kudasai.
    Please give me water.
  • これを ください。
    Kore o kudasai.
    I'll take this, please.

Softer requests

て ください is polite but can sound like an instruction. For a softer, more careful request, you can use a different pattern later, such as てくれませんか. For now, remember that adding ちょっと or すみません before the request makes it friendlier.

すみません、ちょっと まって ください.

  • すみません、まって ください。
    Sumimasen, matte kudasai.
    Excuse me, please wait.
  • すみません、おしえて ください。
    Sumimasen, oshiete kudasai.
    Excuse me, please tell me.

Common mistakes

Do not use the ます form before ください: みます ください is wrong. And do not forget the object marker を.

Also remember that ください is for asking others, not for yourself.

  • ✗ みます ください。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the て-form.
  • ✗ ほん よんで ください。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: add を.
  • みて ください。
    Mite kudasai.
    Right: Please look.
Try it: Say: “Please wait a moment.”

ちょっと まって ください。

Chotto matte kudasai.: Please wait a moment.

    Minasan, hon o akete kudasai.
    Everyone, please open your books.

    Sensei, mou ichido itte kudasai.

    Yes. Please look at page three.

    Yukkuri hanashite kudasai.
    Please speak slowly.

    わかりましたか。
    Wakarimashita ka.
    Do you understand?
  • Alex
    はい、わかりました。ありがとうございます。
    Hai, wakarimashita. Arigatou gozaimasu.
    Yes, I understand. Thank you.

Remember

  • Verb te-form plus kudasai means please do it.
  • A thing plus o kudasai means please give me the thing.
  • Add sumimasen or chotto to sound friendlier.
