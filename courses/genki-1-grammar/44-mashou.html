～ましょう: Let's Do It

Goal: By the end you can say “let's go”, “let's eat” and accept an invitation.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To suggest doing something together, change the ます ending of a verb to ましょう. いきます becomes いきましょう, “let's go”.

The meaning is “let us do it”. It is warm and polite, and it works for any verb.

  • いきます → いきましょう
    ikimasu → ikimashou
    go → let's go
  • たべます → たべましょう
    tabemasu → tabemashou
    eat → let's eat

In a sentence

The rest of the sentence is unchanged. いっしょに えいがを みましょう means “let's watch a movie together”. The word いっしょに, together, makes the invitation friendlier.

Do not add か unless you want to ask a question.

  • いっしょに ごはんを たべましょう。
    Issho ni gohan o tabemashou.
    Let's eat together.
  • えきまで あるきましょう。
    Eki made arukimashou.
    Let's walk to the station.
  • コーヒーを のみましょう。
    Koohii o nomimashou.
    Let's drink coffee.

Accepting

To accept an invitation, say はい、いきましょう, “yes, let's go”, or いいですね, “sounds good”. Another common reply is そうしましょう, “let's do that”.

These replies sound natural and keep the conversation moving.

  • いいですね。
    Ii desu ne.
    Sounds good.
  • はい、いきましょう。
    Hai, ikimashou.
    Yes, let's go.
  • そうしましょう。
    Sou shimashou.
    Let's do that.

Declining politely

To decline, do not say a flat “no”. Say すみません、ちょっと..., which leaves the reason unsaid, or すみません、きょうは いそがしい です, “sorry, I'm busy today”.

This soft refusal is how Japanese speakers keep the atmosphere kind.

  • すみません、ちょっと...
    Sumimasen, chotto...
    Sorry, it's a bit difficult...
  • きょうは いそがしい です。
    Kyou wa isogashii desu.
    I'm busy today.

Compare with ませんか

You already know いきませんか, “won't you go?”. いきましょう is more direct: “let's go”. Both are polite. ませんか sounds a little gentler, while ましょう sounds more decided.

Use ませんか with someone you do not know well, and ましょう with friends.

  • いきませんか。
    Ikimasen ka.
    Won't you go? (gentle)
  • いきましょう。
    Ikimashou.
    Let's go. (direct)

Common mistakes

Do not use ましょう for something only you will do: ひとりで いきましょう sounds strange, because “let's” needs at least two people. And do not use the dictionary form: いくましょう is wrong.

Always start from the ます stem: いき plus ましょう.

  • ✗ いくましょう。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the ます stem.
  • ✗ ひとりで たべましょう。
    (wrong)
    Odd: let's needs another person.
  • いきましょう。
    Ikimashou.
    Right: Let's go.
Try it: Say: “Let's eat together.”

いっしょに たべましょう。

Issho ni tabemashou.: Let's eat together.

Remember

  • Change masu to mashou to say let's.
  • Accept with ii desu ne or sou shimashou.
  • Decline softly with sumimasen, chotto.
