～ましょうか: Shall I? Shall We?

Goal: By the end you can offer help and make a suggestion as a question.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

Add か to ましょう and you get ましょうか. It has two uses. First, you offer to do something: “shall I do it for you?”. Second, you ask someone about doing something together: “shall we?”.

Context shows which one is meant.

  • てつだいましょうか。
    Tetsudaimashou ka.
    Shall I help?
  • いきましょうか。
    Ikimashou ka.
    Shall we go?

Offering help

When you see someone who may need help, offer with ましょうか. にもつを もちましょうか means “shall I carry your luggage?”. まどを あけましょうか means “shall I open the window?”.

This is a polite, thoughtful way to offer.

  • にもつを もちましょうか。
    Nimotsu o mochimashou ka.
    Shall I carry your luggage?
  • まどを あけましょうか。
    Mado o akemashou ka.
    Shall I open the window?
  • でんきを つけましょうか。
    Denki o tsukemashou ka.
    Shall I turn on the light?

Answering an offer

To accept an offer, say おねがいします, “please”. Or おねがいできますか, “could you?”. To decline, say だいじょうぶです, “I'm fine”, or けっこうです, “no thank you”.

おねがいします is one of the most useful phrases in Japanese.

  • おねがいします。
    Onegai shimasu.
    Yes, please.
  • だいじょうぶです。
    Daijoubu desu.
    I'm fine, thank you.
  • けっこうです。
    Kekkou desu.
    No, thank you.

Asking “shall we?”

Use いっしょに to make it clearly a suggestion: いっしょに かえりましょうか, “shall we go home together?”. A reply could be はい、かえりましょう.

This is softer than ましょう, because it asks for the listener's opinion.

  • いっしょに かえりましょうか。
    Issho ni kaerimashou ka.
    Shall we go home together?
  • なにを たべましょうか。
    Nani o tabemashou ka.
    What shall we eat?
  • どこへ いきましょうか。
    Doko e ikimashou ka.
    Where shall we go?

Question words

Put a question word before the verb to ask “what shall we...?”. なにを たべましょうか, what shall we eat? いつ あいましょうか, when shall we meet? どこで あいましょうか, where shall we meet?

These questions are perfect for planning something with a friend.

  • いつ あいましょうか。
    Itsu aimashou ka.
    When shall we meet?
  • どこで あいましょうか。
    Doko de aimashou ka.
    Where shall we meet?
  • なんじに あいましょうか。
    Nanji ni aimashou ka.
    What time shall we meet?

Common mistakes

Do not use ましょうか to ask permission: “may I?” is another pattern you will learn soon. And do not mix ましょうか with ますか: たべますか asks about habit, たべましょうか asks about a plan.

Remember the difference: ましょうか is about a plan or an offer.

  • たべますか。
    Tabemasu ka.
    Do you eat? (habit)
  • たべましょうか。
    Tabemashou ka.
    Shall we eat? (plan)
  • ✗ たべるましょうか。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the ます stem.
Try it: Say: “Shall I open the window?”

まどを あけましょうか。

Mado o akemashou ka.: Shall I open the window?

Story: Can I help?

  • Mika
    アレックスさん、にもつが おもそうですね。
    Arekkusu-san, nimotsu ga omosou desu ne.
    Alex, your luggage looks heavy.
  • Mika
    もちましょうか。
    Mochimashou ka.
    Shall I carry it?
  • Alex
    ありがとうございます。おねがいします。
    Arigatou gozaimasu. Onegai shimasu.
    Thank you. Yes, please.
  • Mika
    ドアも あけましょうか。
    Doa mo akemashou ka.
    Shall I open the door too?
  • Alex
    いいえ、だいじょうぶです。
    Iie, daijoubu desu.
    No, I'm fine.
  • Mika
    じゃ、いっしょに いきましょうか。
    Ja, issho ni ikimashou ka.
    Then shall we go together?

Remember

  • Mashou ka offers help (shall I) or suggests (shall we).
  • Accept with onegai shimasu; decline with daijoubu desu.
  • Add a question word for what, where, when.
