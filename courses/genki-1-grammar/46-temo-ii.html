～ても いい です: May I?

Goal: By the end you can ask “may I...?” and answer yes or no politely.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say that something is allowed, put も and いい です after the て-form. たべても いい です means “it is okay to eat”, “you may eat”.

Literally it says: even if you eat, it is good.

  • たべても いい です。
    Tabete mo ii desu.
    You may eat.
  • みても いい です。
    Mite mo ii desu.
    You may look.

Asking permission

Add か to ask. ここで たべても いい ですか means “may I eat here?”. This is the standard way to ask for permission.

A friendly addition is すみません first.

  • ここで たべても いい ですか。
    Koko de tabete mo ii desu ka.
    May I eat here?
  • しゃしんを とっても いい ですか。
    Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka.
    May I take a photo?
  • トイレを かりても いい ですか。
    Toire o karite mo ii desu ka.
    May I use the toilet?

Giving permission

To say yes, answer はい、いい ですよ or どうぞ, “go ahead”. The word よ makes it friendly and reassuring.

You can also repeat the verb: はい、たべても いい です.

  • はい、いい ですよ。
    Hai, ii desu yo.
    Yes, that's fine.
  • どうぞ。
    Douzo.
    Go ahead.
  • はい、とっても いい です。
    Hai, totte mo ii desu.
    Yes, you may take one.

Refusing permission

To say no, you can say すみません、ちょっと..., which is soft. Or say いいえ、いけません, “no, you must not”, which is strong and used for rules. The stronger form belongs to the next lesson.

In everyday life the soft refusal is more common between people.

  • すみません、ちょっと...
    Sumimasen, chotto...
    Sorry, I'd rather you not.
  • いいえ、いけません。
    Iie, ikemasen.
    No, you must not.

With the plain て-form

The pattern always uses the て-form that you learned in the last lessons. かいて, のんで, いって, して. Put も いい です after it.

Practise by saying each verb you know in the pattern.

  • かいても いい です。
    Kaite mo ii desu.
    You may write.
  • のんでも いい です。
    Nonde mo ii desu.
    You may drink.
  • いっても いい です。
    Itte mo ii desu.
    You may go.

Common mistakes

Do not say たべますも いい です. Use the て-form. And do not forget も: たべて いい です is not the same. Without も, it can sound like “eating is good”.

Also いい here means “permitted”, not “tasty”.

  • ✗ たべますも いい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the て-form.
  • ✗ たべて いい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: add も.
  • たべても いい です。
    Tabete mo ii desu.
    Right: You may eat.
Try it: Say: “May I take a photo?”

しゃしんを とっても いい ですか。

Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka.: May I take a photo?

Remember

  • Te-form plus mo ii desu means you may.
  • Add ka to ask: may I...?
  • Answer yes with ii desu yo or douzo; refuse softly with chotto.
