Casual Negative: ～ない

Goal: By the end you can say “don't eat”, “won't go” and “don't do” in casual style.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

The plain negative ends in ない. It is the casual version of ません. たべない means “don't eat” or “won't eat”.

How you make it depends on the verb group.

  • たべます → たべない
    tabemasu → tabenai
    eat → don't eat
    Word by word
    たべますtabemasueat, polite→becomesたべないtabenaidon't eat
  • いきます → いかない
    ikimasu → ikanai
    go → don't go
    Word by word
    いきますikimasugo, polite→becomesいかないikanaidon't go

Ichidan verbs

For an ichidan verb, drop る and add ない. たべる becomes たべない. みる becomes みない. ねる becomes ねない.

This is the easiest group.

  • たべる → たべない
    taberu → tabenai
    eat
    Word by word
    たべるtaberueat→becomesたべないtabenaidon't eat
  • みる → みない
    miru → minai
    see
    Word by word
    みるmirusee; watch→becomesみないminaidon't see; watch
  • おきる → おきない
    okiru → okinai
    wake up
    Word by word
    おきるokiruwake up; get up→becomesおきないokinaidon't wake up

Godan verbs

For a godan verb, change the last sound to its あ row and add ない. いく becomes いかない. のむ becomes のまない. はなす becomes はなさない. かく becomes かかない.

Think of it as: the last kana moves to the あ column.

  • いく → いかない
    iku → ikanai
    go
    Word by word
    いくikugo→becomesいかないikanaidon't go
  • のむ → のまない
    nomu → nomanai
    drink
    Word by word
    のむnomudrink→becomesのまないnomanaidon't drink
  • はなす → はなさない
    hanasu → hanasanai
    speak
    Word by word
    はなすhanasuspeak→becomesはなさないhanasanaidon't speak
  • まつ → またない
    matsu → matanai
    wait
    Word by word
    まつmatsuwait→becomesまたないmatanaidon't wait

The う ending

Verbs ending in う change to わ, not あ. かう becomes かわない. あう becomes あわない. いう becomes いわない.

This is one small exception to the あ-column rule.

  • かう → かわない
    kau → kawanai
    buy
    Word by word
    かうkaubuy→becomesかわないkawanaidon't buy
  • あう → あわない
    au → awanai
    meet
    Word by word
    あうaumeet→becomesあわないawanaidon't meet
  • いう → いわない
    iu → iwanai
    say
    Word by word
    いうiusay→becomesいわないiwanaidon't say

Irregular verbs

する becomes しない. くる becomes こない. And ある, to exist, becomes just ない, not あらない.

These three are worth memorising.

  • する → しない
    suru → shinai
    do
    Word by word
    するsurudo→しないshinaido not
  • くる → こない
    kuru → konai
    come
    Word by word
    くるkurucome→こないkonaido not come
  • ある → ない
    aru → nai
    exist
    Word by word
    あるaruexist→ないnainot exist

Common mistakes

Do not say かあない or あらない. And do not use くない for verbs; くない is for い-adjectives.

Always check whether the verb ends in う.

  • ✗ かう → かあない
    (wrong)
    Wrong: う becomes わ.
    Word by word
    かうkaubuy→かあないkaanaido not buy
  • ✗ ある → あらない
    (wrong)
    Wrong: the negative of ある is ない.
    Word by word
    あるaruexist→あらないaranainot exist
  • かう → かわない
    kau → kawanai
    Right: don't buy.
    Word by word
    かうkaubuy→かわないkawanaido not buy
Try it: Say: “I won't go.”

いかない。

Ikanai.: I won't go.

Remember

  • Ichidan: drop ru, add nai.
  • Godan: change the last sound to the a row, then nai; u becomes wa.
  • Suru is shinai, kuru is konai, aru is nai.
