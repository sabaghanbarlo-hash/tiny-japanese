Casual Past: ～た and ～なかった

Goal: By the end you can say “ate”, “went”, “didn't eat” and “didn't go” casually.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

The plain past, the た-form, is made exactly like the て-form, but with た where the て-form has て, and だ where it has で. たべて becomes たべた. のんで becomes のんだ.

So you do not need to learn a new set of rules. Just change the ending.

  • たべて → たべた
    tabete → tabeta
    ate
  • のんで → のんだ
    nonde → nonda
    drank

Ichidan and irregular

Ichidan: drop る, add た. たべる becomes たべた. Irregular: する becomes した, and くる becomes きた.

These are the same as the て-form patterns.

  • たべる → たべた
    taberu → tabeta
    ate
  • みる → みた
    miru → mita
    saw
  • する → した
    suru → shita
    did
  • くる → きた
    kuru → kita
    came

Godan verbs

Godan verbs follow the same rule as the て-form. かう becomes かった. のむ becomes のんだ. かく becomes かいた. はなす becomes はなした. いく becomes いった.

Just replace the last て or で with た or だ.

  • かう → かった
    kau → katta
    bought
  • のむ → のんだ
    nomu → nonda
    drank
  • かく → かいた
    kaku → kaita
    wrote
  • いく → いった
    iku → itta
    went

The negative past

For the negative past, take the plain negative ない and change it to なかった. たべない becomes たべなかった. いかない becomes いかなかった.

This is just like the い-adjective rule you learned in the adjective lessons.

  • たべなかった。
    Tabenakatta.
    (I) didn't eat.
  • いかなかった。
    Ikanakatta.
    (I) didn't go.
  • しなかった。
    Shinakatta.
    (I) didn't do it.

Questions

Ask with rising tone: たべた？ means “did you eat?”. Answer with うん、たべた or ううん、たべなかった.

In casual speech, うん is yes and ううん is no.

  • きのう なに した？
    Kinou nani shita?
    What did you do yesterday?
  • ごはん たべた？
    Gohan tabeta?
    Did you eat?
  • ううん、たべなかった。
    Uun, tabenakatta.
    No, I didn't eat.

Common mistakes

Do not use た with ます: たべますた is wrong. And do not say たべなかった です in careful speech; add です only to adjectives in polite style.

Keep casual forms casual.

  • ✗ たべますた。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use たべた.
  • ✗ かうた。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: かう becomes かった.
  • たべた。
    Tabeta.
    Right: (I) ate.
Try it: Say: “I didn't go.”

いかなかった。

Ikanakatta.: I didn't go.

Remember

  • The ta-form is the te-form with ta or da.
  • Negative past: nai becomes nakatta.
  • Un means yes and uun means no in casual speech.
