Casual Nouns and な-Adjectives: だ

Goal: By the end you can say “it is”, “it isn't”, “it was” and “it wasn't” casually with nouns and {な|na}-adjectives.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

In casual speech, です becomes だ. がくせい です becomes がくせい だ, “(I) am a student”. The same works for な-adjectives: しずか です becomes しずか だ.

In real conversation, だ is often dropped completely in questions and sounds blunt when used by itself.

  • がくせい です → がくせい だ
    gakusei desu → gakusei da
    I am a student
  • しずか です → しずか だ
    shizuka desu → shizuka da
    It is quiet

Questions

In a casual question, you usually just drop だ and use a rising tone. がくせい？ means “are you a student?”. しずか？ means “is it quiet?”.

Do not say だ？ at the end of a question. It sounds odd.

  • がくせい？
    Gakusei?
    Are you a student?
  • あした ひま？
    Ashita hima?
    Are you free tomorrow?
  • きれい？
    Kirei?
    Is it pretty?

The negative

The casual negative is じゃ ない. It is what you already met in the lesson on じゃ ない with nouns. がくせい じゃ ない means “am not a student”. しずか じゃ ない means “is not quiet”.

Remember: in casual speech, you can often add よ or ね after it.

  • がくせい じゃ ない。
    Gakusei ja nai.
    (I) am not a student.
  • しずか じゃ ない。
    Shizuka ja nai.
    It is not quiet.
  • ひま じゃ ない よ。
    Hima ja nai yo.
    I'm not free, you know.

The past

The casual past is だった. がくせい だった means “was a student”. しずか だった means “was quiet”.

Think of だった as the past of だ.

  • がくせい だった。
    Gakusei datta.
    (I) was a student.
  • しずか だった。
    Shizuka datta.
    It was quiet.
  • きのうは あめ だった。
    Kinou wa ame datta.
    Yesterday was rainy.

The negative past

The casual negative past is じゃ なかった. がくせい じゃ なかった means “was not a student”. しずか じゃ なかった means “was not quiet”.

It is the plain negative with the past ending かった.

  • がくせい じゃ なかった。
    Gakusei ja nakatta.
    (I) was not a student.
  • しずか じゃ なかった。
    Shizuka ja nakatta.
    It was not quiet.
  • あめ じゃ なかった。
    Ame ja nakatta.
    It wasn't rainy.

All four forms

Polite: です, でした, じゃ ありません, じゃ ありませんでした. Casual: だ, だった, じゃ ない, じゃ なかった.

Keep this table in your head; it lets you switch between styles for nouns and な-adjectives.

  • がくせい だ。
    Gakusei da.
    is (casual)
  • がくせい だった。
    Gakusei datta.
    was (casual)
  • がくせい じゃ ない。
    Gakusei ja nai.
    is not (casual)
  • がくせい じゃ なかった。
    Gakusei ja nakatta.
    was not (casual)

Common mistakes

Do not say がくせい だ ない. The negative is じゃ ない. Also do not use だ after an い-adjective: おいしい だ is not normal in standard speech.

And do not use だ when talking to someone you should be polite to.

  • ✗ がくせい だ ない。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use じゃ ない.
  • ✗ おいしい だ。
    (wrong)
    Odd: い-adjectives stand alone.
  • がくせい じゃ ない。
    Gakusei ja nai.
    Right: am not a student.
Try it: Say casually: “It was quiet.”

しずか だった。

Shizuka datta.: It was quiet.

Vocabulary

Story: Old friends

  • Mika
    ひさしぶり！げんき？
    Hisashiburi! Genki?
    Long time no see! How are you?
  • Alex
    うん、げんき。ミカは？
    Un, genki. Mika wa?
    Good. And you?
  • Mika
    わたしも げんき。きのうは ひま だった。
    Watashi mo genki. Kinou wa hima datta.
    I'm good too. I was free yesterday.
  • Alex
    いま ひま？
    Ima hima?
    Are you free now?
  • Mika
    ううん、ひま じゃ ない。しごと だ。
    Uun, hima ja nai. Shigoto da.
    No, I'm not free. I have work.
  • Alex
    そう。がんばって！
    Sou. Ganbatte!
    I see. Good luck!

Remember

  • Desu becomes da; deshita becomes datta.
  • Ja arimasen becomes ja nai; ja arimasen deshita becomes ja nakatta.
  • Drop da in casual questions and use a rising tone.
