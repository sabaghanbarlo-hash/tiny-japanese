Casual い-Adjectives

Goal: By the end you can say “it's delicious”, “it wasn't expensive” and so on, in casual style.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

An い-adjective is already plain. In casual speech you simply drop です. おいしい です becomes おいしい. Do not add だ.

That is why い-adjectives are the simplest in casual speech.

  • おいしい です → おいしい
    oishii desu → oishii
    It is delicious
  • あつい です → あつい
    atsui desu → atsui
    It is hot

Questions

For a question, use a rising tone: おいしい？ means “is it delicious?”. Friends often add の: おいしいの？ is softer and more curious.

Answer with うん、おいしい or ううん、おいしくない.

  • おいしい？
    Oishii?
    Is it delicious?
  • さむい？
    Samui?
    Are you cold?
  • たかい？
    Takai?
    Is it expensive?

The negative

The casual negative is くない, just like the polite version without です. おいしくない means “it's not delicious”.

Remember the exception: いい becomes よくない.

  • おいしくない。
    Oishikunai.
    It's not delicious.
  • たかくない。
    Takakunai.
    It's not expensive.
  • よくない。
    Yokunai.
    It's not good.

The past

The casual past is かった. おいしかった means “it was delicious”. よかった means “it was good”, and also “I'm glad”.

This is the same かった you learned for polite speech, only without です.

  • おいしかった。
    Oishikatta.
    It was delicious.
  • たのしかった。
    Tanoshikatta.
    It was fun.
  • よかった。
    Yokatta.
    It was good. / I'm glad.

The negative past

The casual negative past is くなかった. おいしくなかった means “it wasn't delicious”. よくなかった means “it wasn't good”.

Add よ or ね at the end for a natural feel.

  • おいしくなかった。
    Oishikunakatta.
    It wasn't delicious.
  • たかくなかった。
    Takakunakatta.
    It wasn't expensive.
  • よくなかった ね。
    Yokunakatta ne.
    It wasn't good, was it.

Common mistakes

Do not add だ after an い-adjective: おいしい だ is wrong for a normal statement. And do not use じゃ ない: おいしい じゃ ない is wrong; use おいしくない.

Keep the two groups of adjectives separate.

  • ✗ おいしい だ。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: no だ after い-adjectives.
  • ✗ おいしい じゃ ない。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use くない.
  • おいしくない。
    Oishikunai.
    Right: it's not delicious.
Try it: Say casually: “It was fun.”

たのしかった。

Tanoshikatta.: It was fun.

Story: After the festival

  • Mika
    おまつり、どうだった？
    Omatsuri, dou datta?
    How was the festival?
  • Alex
    すごく たのしかった！
    Sugoku tanoshikatta!
    It was so much fun!
  • Mika
    ひと、おおかった？
    Hito, ookatta?
    Were there lots of people?
  • Alex
    うん、すごく おおかった。
    Un, sugoku ookatta.
    Yeah, so many.
  • Mika
    たべもの、おいしかった？
    Tabemono, oishikatta?
    Was the food good?
  • Alex
    おいしかった けど、たかかった。
    Oishikatta kedo, takakatta.
    It was delicious, but it was expensive.

Remember

  • I-adjectives drop desu and stand alone in casual speech.
  • Negative: kunai. Past: katta. Negative past: kunakatta.
  • Never add da to an i-adjective.
