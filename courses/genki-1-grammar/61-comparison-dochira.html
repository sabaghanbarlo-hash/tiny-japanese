どちらが ～ですか: Which One?

Goal: By the end you can ask “which is bigger, A or B?” and answer “A is bigger”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To ask which of two things is more, use: A, と, B, と, どちらが, adjective, ですか. ねこと いぬと どちらが かわいい ですか means “which is cuter, cats or dogs?”.

どちら means “which one (of two)”.

  • ねこと いぬと どちらが かわいい ですか。
    Neko to inu to dochira ga kawaii desu ka.
    Which is cuter, cats or dogs?
  • バスと でんしゃと どちらが はやい ですか。
    Basu to densha to dochira ga hayai desu ka.
    Which is faster, the bus or the train?

Answering

Answer with A の ほうが adjective です. ねこの ほうが かわいい です means “cats are cuter”. The word ほう means “side” and points to the winner.

You can also answer just ねこ です, but the full pattern is clearer.

  • ねこの ほうが かわいい です。
    Neko no hou ga kawaii desu.
    Cats are cuter.
  • でんしゃの ほうが はやい です。
    Densha no hou ga hayai desu.
    The train is faster.
  • コーヒーの ほうが すき です。
    Koohii no hou ga suki desu.
    I like coffee better.

Both or neither

If you think both are the same, say どちらも, “both”. どちらも すき です means “I like both”. With a negative verb, どちらも means “neither”: どちらも すき じゃ ありません.

Another useful answer is おなじ くらい です, “about the same”.

  • どちらも すき です。
    Dochira mo suki desu.
    I like both.
  • どちらも すき じゃ ありません。
    Dochira mo suki ja arimasen.
    I like neither.
  • おなじ くらい です。
    Onaji kurai desu.
    They are about the same.

Casual speech

In casual speech, どちら becomes どっち. ねこと いぬと どっちが すき？ means “cats or dogs, which do you like?”.

Use it with friends.

  • ねこと いぬと どっちが すき？
    Neko to inu to docchi ga suki?
    Cats or dogs, which do you like?
  • ねこの ほうが すき。
    Neko no hou ga suki.
    I like cats better.

Other question words

どちら also asks about choices of any kind. コーヒーと おちゃと どちらが いい ですか means “coffee or tea, which would you like?”. In a shop, どちらに しますか means “which will you take?”.

It is also a polite way to say “where” in formal speech, such as おくには どちらですか, “where are you from?”.

  • コーヒーと おちゃと どちらが いい ですか。
    Koohii to ocha to dochira ga ii desu ka.
    Coffee or tea, which do you prefer?
  • どちらに しますか。
    Dochira ni shimasu ka.
    Which will you take?
  • おくには どちら ですか。
    Okuni wa dochira desu ka.
    Where are you from?

Common mistakes

Use どちら only when there are exactly two options. For three or more, you will use なにが いちばん, in the next lesson. And do not forget the second と: ねこと いぬ どちらが is incomplete.

Also do not answer with より alone; use ほう.

  • ✗ ねこと いぬ どちらが かわいい ですか。
    (wrong)
    Incomplete: add the second と.
  • ✗ ねこより かわいい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong answer shape: use の ほうが.
  • ねこの ほうが かわいい です。
    Neko no hou ga kawaii desu.
    Right: Cats are cuter.
Try it: Say: “Which is faster, the bus or the train?”

バスと でんしゃと どちらが はやい ですか。

Basu to densha to dochira ga hayai desu ka.: Which is faster, the bus or the train?

Remember

  • A to B to dochira ga adjective desu ka asks which of two.
  • Answer with X no hou ga adjective desu.
  • Dochira mo means both, or neither with a negative.
