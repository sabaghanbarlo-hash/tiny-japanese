～でしょう: Probably, Right?

Goal: By the end you can say “it will probably rain” and ask “right?” in a polite way.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

でしょう has two uses. First, it expresses a guess: “it will probably...”. Second, with a rising tone, it asks for agreement: “...right?”.

Both uses attach to the plain form.

  • あした あめが ふる でしょう。
    Ashita ame ga furu deshou.
    It will probably rain tomorrow.
  • おいしい でしょう？
    Oishii deshou?
    It's delicious, right?

A guess

For a guess, put でしょう after the plain form. ミカさんは くる でしょう means “Mika will probably come”. The word たぶん, probably, goes well with it.

Use a falling tone for a guess.

  • たぶん あした あめが ふる でしょう。
    Tabun ashita ame ga furu deshou.
    It will probably rain tomorrow.
  • たぶん ミカさんは こない でしょう。
    Tabun Mika-san wa konai deshou.
    Mika probably won't come.
  • きのうは ひまだった でしょう。
    Kinou wa hima datta deshou.
    You were probably free yesterday.

Nouns and な-adjectives

With nouns and な-adjectives, use no だ. がくせい でしょう, “probably a student”. しずか でしょう, “probably quiet”.

い-adjectives stay as they are: たかい でしょう.

  • がくせい でしょう。
    Gakusei deshou.
    (He) is probably a student.
  • ここは しずか でしょう。
    Koko wa shizuka deshou.
    It is probably quiet here.
  • たかい でしょう。
    Takai deshou.
    It is probably expensive.

Asking for agreement

With a rising tone, でしょう becomes “…right?”. きれい でしょう？ means “pretty, isn't it?”. It expects the listener to say yes.

A common answer is そうですね, “that's right”.

  • きれい でしょう？
    Kirei deshou?
    Pretty, isn't it?
  • そう でしょう？
    Sou deshou?
    Right?
  • そうですね。
    Sou desu ne.
    Yes, indeed.

Casual form

In casual speech, でしょう becomes でしょ with rising tone: きれい でしょ？. Men also use だろう for a guess. For now, learn でしょう well.

でしょ is very common among friends.

  • きれい でしょ？
    Kirei desho?
    Pretty, right?
  • あした くる でしょ？
    Ashita kuru desho?
    You're coming tomorrow, right?

Common mistakes

Do not say がくせい だ でしょう. And do not use ます before: いきます でしょう is not natural. Also do not use でしょう for things you know for sure: use です.

It is for guesses and confirmation only.

  • ✗ がくせい だ でしょう。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: drop だ.
  • ✗ いきます でしょう。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the plain form.
  • いく でしょう。
    Iku deshou.
    Right: (He) will probably go.
Try it: Say: “It will probably rain tomorrow.”

あした あめが ふる でしょう。

Ashita ame ga furu deshou.: It will probably rain tomorrow.

Story: Weather forecast

  • Alex
    あしたの てんきは どうでしょう。
    Ashita no tenki wa dou deshou.
    What will tomorrow's weather be like?
  • Mika
    たぶん あめが ふる でしょう。
    Tabun ame ga furu deshou.
    It will probably rain.
  • Alex
    さむい でしょうか。
    Samui deshou ka.
    Will it be cold?
  • Mika
    ええ、さむい でしょう。
    Ee, samui deshou.
    Yes, probably.
  • Alex
    かさを もって いきます。
    Kasa o motte ikimasu.
    I'll take an umbrella.
  • Mika
    そうですね。それが いい でしょう。
    Sou desu ne. Sore ga ii deshou.
    Yes. That would probably be good.

Remember

  • Plain form plus deshou means probably.
  • Rising tone makes it right? and asks for agreement.
  • Drop da before deshou after nouns and na-adjectives.
