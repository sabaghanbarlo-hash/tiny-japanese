～なくても いい です: Don't Have To

Goal: By the end you can say “you don't have to come” and ask “do I have to...?”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say “you don't have to”, take the plain negative ない, change い to くて, add も, then いい です. たべなくても いい です means “you don't have to eat”.

Literally: “even if you don't eat, it's fine”.

  • たべなくても いい です。
    Tabenakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to eat.
  • こなくても いい です。
    Konakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to come.

Examples

いかなくても いい です. かわなくても いい です. しなくても いい です. みなくても いい です. Always start from the plain negative.

It is the opposite of なければ なりません.

  • いかなくても いい です。
    Ikanakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to go.
  • しなくても いい です。
    Shinakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to do it.
  • かわなくても いい です。
    Kawanakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to buy it.

Asking

Ask いかなくても いい ですか for “do I have to go?”, or more exactly “may I not go?”. It is a polite way to check if something is required.

A yes answer means it is not required: はい、いい です.

  • あした こなくても いい ですか。
    Ashita konakute mo ii desu ka.
    May I not come tomorrow?
  • はい、いい ですよ。
    Hai, ii desu yo.
    Yes, that's fine.
  • いいえ、こなければ なりません。
    Iie, konakereba narimasen.
    No, you must come.

Four patterns together

Now you know four related patterns. ても いい, may. ては いけません, must not. なければ なりません, must. なくても いい, don't have to.

Together they cover permission and obligation.

  • たべても いい です。
    Tabete mo ii desu.
    You may eat.
  • たべては いけません。
    Tabete wa ikemasen.
    You must not eat.
  • たべなければ なりません。
    Tabenakereba narimasen.
    You must eat.
  • たべなくても いい です。
    Tabenakute mo ii desu.
    You don't have to eat.

Casual speech

Casual: こなくても いい, or shorter こなくて いい. Among friends, いいよ is a common answer.

Do not use it with someone above you without です.

  • こなくても いい。
    Konakute mo ii.
    You don't have to come.
  • いかなくて いい よ。
    Ikanakute ii yo.
    You don't have to go.
  • むりしなくても いい よ。
    Muri shinakute mo ii yo.
    You don't have to push yourself.

Common mistakes

Do not confuse なくても いい with ないで ください. One says it is unnecessary, the other asks someone not to. And do not use なくても without いい to mean “don't have to”.

Check each ending carefully.

  • ✗ たべなくて ください。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ないで ください is please don't.
  • ✗ いかなくても です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: add いい.
  • いかなくても いい です。
    Ikanakute mo ii desu.
    Right: You don't have to go.
Try it: Say: “You don't have to come.”

こなくても いい です。

Konakute mo ii desu.: You don't have to come.

Story: Do I have to?

  • Alex
    せんせい、あした がっこうへ こなければ なりませんか。
    Sensei, ashita gakkou e konakereba narimasen ka.
    Teacher, do I have to come to school tomorrow?
  • Sensei
    いいえ、こなくても いい です。
    Iie, konakute mo ii desu.
    No, you don't have to come.
  • Alex
    しゅくだいは しなければ なりませんか。
    Shukudai wa shinakereba narimasen ka.
    Do I have to do the homework?
  • Sensei
    はい、しなければ なりません。
    Hai, shinakereba narimasen.
    Yes, you must.
  • Alex
    レポートは かかなくても いい ですか。
    Repooto wa kakanakute mo ii desu ka.
    Don't I have to write the report?
  • Sensei
    はい、かかなくても いい です。
    Hai, kakanakute mo ii desu.
    No, you don't have to write it.

Remember

  • Plain negative: change nai to nakute mo ii for don't have to.
  • Four patterns: may, must not, must, don't have to.
  • Do not confuse with nai de kudasai.
