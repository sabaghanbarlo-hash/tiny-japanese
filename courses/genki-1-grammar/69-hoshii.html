ほしい: I Want Something

Goal: By the end you can say “I want a new phone” and ask “What do you want?”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say you want a thing, use ほしい. It is an い-adjective, so it behaves like たかい. The thing you want is marked with が.

くるまが ほしい means “I want a car”. Add です to be polite.

  • くるまが ほしい です。
    Kuruma ga hoshii desu.
    I want a car.
    Word by word
    くるまkurumacarがgasubject markerほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copula。
  • スマホが ほしい です。
    Sumaho ga hoshii desu.
    I want a smartphone.
    Word by word

Asking

To ask what someone wants, put なに in front. なにが ほしい ですか means “What do you want?”.

You answer with the thing and が ほしい です.

  • なにが ほしい ですか。
    Nani ga hoshii desu ka.
    What do you want?
    Word by word
    なにnaniwhatがgasubject markerほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copulaかkaquestion marker。
  • じかんが ほしい です。
    Jikan ga hoshii desu.
    I want time.
    Word by word
    じかんjikantimeがgasubject markerほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copula。

Negative and past

Because ほしい is an い-adjective, drop the last い to change it. ほしくない means “I don't want it”, and ほしかった means “I wanted it”.

Add です to be polite: ほしくない です, ほしかった です.

  • くるまは ほしくない です。
    Kuruma wa hoshikunai desu.
    I don't want a car.
    Word by word
    くるまkurumacarはwatopic markerほしくないhoshikunaido not wantですdesupolite copula。
  • ふくが ほしかった です。
    Fuku ga hoshikatta desu.
    I wanted clothes.
    Word by word
    ふくfukuclothesがgasubject markerほしかったhoshikattawantedですdesupolite copula。

Things, not actions

ほしい is for things. To say you want to do something, use たい from the earlier lesson.

ほんが ほしい is “I want a book”. ほんを よみたい is “I want to read a book”.

  • ほんが ほしい です。
    Hon ga hoshii desu.
    I want a book.
    Word by word
    ほんhonbookがgasubject markerほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copula。
  • ほんを よみたい です。
    Hon o yomitai desu.
    I want to read a book.
    Word by word
    ほんhonbookをoobject markerよみたいyomitaiwant to readですdesupolite copula。

Common mistakes

Do not attach ほしい to a verb stem. よみほしい is wrong; use よみたい. Also, ほしい talks about your own feeling, just like たい. To say what another person wants, you will need a different form later, so for now use it for yourself, or to ask “you”.

The standard pattern for a beginner is the thing plus が.

  • ✗ ほんを よみほしい です。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use よみたい.
    Word by word
    ほんhonbookをoobject markerよみyomiread (stem)ほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copula。
  • かばんが ほしい です。
    Kaban ga hoshii desu.
    Right: I want a bag.
    Word by word
    かばんkabanbagがgasubject markerほしいhoshiiwantですdesupolite copula。

Remember

  • Hoshii is an i-adjective: hoshikunai means don't want, hoshikatta means wanted.
  • The thing you want takes ga.
  • Use hoshii for things and tai for actions.
