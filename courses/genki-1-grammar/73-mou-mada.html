もう と まだ: Already and Not Yet

Goal: By the end you can say “I already ate” and “I haven't done it yet”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

もう before a past verb means “already”. もう たべました means “I already ate”.

It says the thing is finished.

  • もう ひるごはんを たべました。
    Mou hirugohan o tabemashita.
    I already ate lunch.

    Mou shukudai o shimashita.

Asking

Use もう in a question to ask “Have you ... yet?”. もう たべましたか means “Have you eaten yet?”.

It is the normal way to ask if something is done.

  • もう レポートを かきましたか。
    Mou repooto o kakimashita ka.
    Have you written the report yet?

Answering

To say yes, answer はい、もう ～ました. To say “not yet”, answer いいえ、まだ です.

まだ means “still” or “not yet”.

  • はい、もう たべました。
    Hai, mou tabemashita.
    Yes, I already ate.
    Word by word
    はいhaiyes、もうmōalreadyたべましたtabemashitaate。
  • いいえ、まだ です。
    Iie, mada desu.
    No, not yet.
    Word by word

Not yet, in a full sentence

For a full sentence, use まだ, the て-form, and いません. まだ たべて いません means “I haven't eaten yet”.

This uses the て いる pattern from the earlier lesson, in the negative.

  • まだ レポートを かいて いません。
    Mada repooto o kaite imasen.
    I haven't written the report yet.
    Word by word
    まだmadanot yetレポートrepōtoreportをoobject markerかいてkaitewritingいませんimasenhave not。
  • まだ ひるごはんを たべて いません。
    Mada hirugohan o tabete imasen.
    I haven't eaten lunch yet.

Common mistakes

For “I haven't done it yet”, do not use the plain past negative. まだ たべませんでした is not what you want here. Use まだ たべて いません.

Also, もう with a present negative has a different meaning: もう たべません means “I won't eat any more”.

  • ✗ まだ たべませんでした。
    (wrong here)
    Not what you want: use て いません.
    Word by word
    まだmadanot yetたべませんでしたtabemasendeshitadid not eat。
  • ✗ もう たべません。
    Mou tabemasen.
    Different meaning: I won't eat any more.
    Word by word
    もうmōno longerたべませんtabemasenwill not eat。
  • まだ たべて いません。
    Mada tabete imasen.
    Right: I haven't eaten yet.
    Word by word
    まだmadanot yetたべてtabeteeatenいませんimasenhave not。

Remember

  • Mou plus past means already.
  • Answer not yet with mada desu, or mada plus te imasen.
  • Mou with a negative can mean not any more, so be careful.
