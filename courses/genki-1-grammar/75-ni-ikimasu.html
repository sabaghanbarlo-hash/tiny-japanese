～に いきます: Go to Do

Goal: By the end you can say “I go to buy a book” and “Let's go to see a movie”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say you go in order to do something, take the ます form, remove ます, and add に, then いきます. かいます becomes かい, so かいに いきます means “I go to buy”.

The little に marks the purpose.

  • ほんを かいに いきます。
    Hon o kai ni ikimasu.
    I go to buy a book.
  • ごはんを たべに いきます。
    Gohan o tabe ni ikimasu.
    I go to eat.

With action nouns

Some nouns already mean an action, like かいもの for “shopping” and りょこう for “travel”. Add に directly: かいものに いきます means “I go shopping”.

You do not need a verb in front.

  • デパートへ かいものに いきます。
    Depaato e kaimono ni ikimasu.
    I go shopping at the department store.
  • きょうとへ りょこうに いきました。
    Kyouto e ryokou ni ikimashita.
    I went to Kyoto to travel.

Adding the place

Put the place first with へ, then the purpose with に. がっこうへ べんきょうしに いきます means “I go to school to study”.

A verb like およぐ becomes およぎ before に.

  • がっこうへ べんきょうしに いきます。
    Gakkou e benkyou shi ni ikimasu.
    I go to school to study.
  • うみへ およぎに いきます。
    Umi e oyogi ni ikimasu.
    I go to the sea to swim.

Coming and returning

You can replace いきます with きます “come” or かえります “return”. The pattern stays the same.

あそびに きます means “come to play”.

  • ともだちが あそびに きます。
    Tomodachi ga asobi ni kimasu.
    A friend comes to hang out.
  • うちへ ひるごはんを たべに かえります。
    Uchi e hirugohan o tabe ni kaerimasu.
    I go back home to eat lunch.

Inviting

This pattern is perfect for invitations. のみに いきませんか means “Shall we go for a drink?”, and みに いきましょう means “Let's go to see it”.

You learned both endings in the earlier lessons.

  • いっしょに のみに いきませんか。
    Issho ni nomi ni ikimasen ka.
    Shall we go for a drink together?
  • えいがを みに いきましょう。
    Eiga o mi ni ikimashou.
    Let's go to see a movie.

Common mistakes

Do not keep the ます ending: たべますに いきます is wrong. Do not use the dictionary form either: たべるに いきます is wrong.

Use the stem only: たべに いきます.

  • ✗ たべますに いきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: remove ます.
  • ✗ たべるに いきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the stem.
  • たべに いきます。
    Tabe ni ikimasu.
    Right: I go to eat.
Try it: Say: “I go to see a movie.”

えいがを みに いきます。

Eiga o mi ni ikimasu.: I go to see a movie.

Remember

  • Remove masu, add ni, then ikimasu: tabe ni ikimasu.
  • An action noun like kaimono takes ni directly.
  • Never keep masu before ni.
