ので: Because (Softer)

Goal: By the end you can say “I have a fever, so I'll rest” and explain yourself politely.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

ので gives a reason, like から, but it sounds softer and more like an explanation of a fact. Put the plain form before it.

ねつが ある ので、やすみます means “I have a fever, so I'll rest”.

  • ねつが ある ので、やすみます。
    Netsu ga aru node, yasumimasu.
    I have a fever, so I'll rest.
  • さむい ので、コートを きます。
    Samui node, kooto o kimasu.
    It's cold, so I'll wear a coat.

Which forms

With verbs and い-adjectives, use the plain form: たかい ので, いそがしい ので, いく ので. The ending of the whole sentence can stay polite.

Plain before ので, polite at the end.

  • たかい ので、かいません。
    Takai node, kaimasen.
    It's expensive, so I won't buy it.
  • いそがしい ので、いきません。
    Isogashii node, ikimasen.
    I'm busy, so I won't go.

Nouns and な-adjectives

After a noun or a な-adjective, use な instead of だ. あめ なので means “because it's rain”. しずか なので means “because it's quiet”.

This is the main difference from から.

  • あめ なので、うちに います。
    Ame na node, uchi ni imasu.
    It's raining, so I'm staying home.
  • しずか なので、すきです。
    Shizuka na node, suki desu.
    It's quiet, so I like it.

Why choose it

から can sound like your own strong reason. ので sounds like an explanation of the situation, so it is common when you excuse yourself, or explain to a teacher or a boss.

Both are correct. This is a matter of feeling.

  • すみません、ねつが ある ので、やすみます。
    Sumimasen, netsu ga aru node, yasumimasu.
    Sorry, I have a fever, so I'll rest.
  • あした テストが ある ので、べんきょうします。
    Ashita tesuto ga aru node, benkyou shimasu.
    I have a test tomorrow, so I'll study.

Common mistakes

Do not use だ before ので. あめ だので is wrong; say あめ なので. しずか だので is wrong; say しずか なので.

Remember: noun or な-adjective, then な.

  • ✗ あめ だので、うちに います。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use なので.
  • ✗ しずか だので、すきです。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use なので.
  • あめ なので、うちに います。
    Ame na node, uchi ni imasu.
    Right: It's raining, so I'm home.
Try it: Say: “It's cold, so I'll wear a coat.”

さむい ので、コートを きます。

Samui node, kooto o kimasu.: It's cold, so I'll wear a coat.

Story: Calling in sick

  • Alex
    せんせい、すみません。ねつが ある ので、きょうは やすみます。
    Sensei, sumimasen. Netsu ga aru node, kyou wa yasumimasu.
    Teacher, sorry. I have a fever, so I'll be absent today.
  • Teacher
    だいじょうぶ ですか。
    Daijoubu desu ka.
    Are you all right?
  • Alex
    はい、すこし あたまが いたい ので、ねます。
    Hai, sukoshi atama ga itai node, nemasu.
    Yes, my head hurts a little, so I'll sleep.
  • Teacher
    おだいじに。くすりを のんだ ほうが いい ですよ。
    Odaijini. Kusuri o nonda hou ga ii desu yo.
    Take care. You should take medicine.
  • Alex
    はい、ありがとう ございます。
    Hai, arigatou gozaimasu.
    Yes, thank you.

Remember

  • Plain form plus node gives a soft reason.
  • After a noun or na-adjective, use na node, never da node.
  • Node is good for excuses and explanations.
