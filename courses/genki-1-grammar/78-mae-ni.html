まえに: Before

Goal: By the end you can say “I brush my teeth before sleeping” and “I wash my hands before eating”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say “before doing something”, put the dictionary form of the verb before まえに. ねる まえに means “before sleeping”.

Then say what you do. ねる まえに、はを みがきます means “Before sleeping, I brush my teeth”.

  • ねる まえに、はを みがきます。
    Neru mae ni, ha o migakimasu.
    Before sleeping, I brush my teeth.
  • でかける まえに、でんわを します。
    Dekakeru mae ni, denwa o shimasu.
    Before going out, I make a phone call.

Always the dictionary form

The verb before まえに always stays in the dictionary form, even when the main verb is in the past. The main verb at the end shows the time.

ねる まえに、ほんを よみました means “Before sleeping, I read a book”.

  • ねる まえに、ほんを よみました。
    Neru mae ni, hon o yomimashita.
    Before sleeping, I read a book.
  • たべる まえに、てを あらいました。
    Taberu mae ni, te o araimashita.
    Before eating, I washed my hands.

With nouns

With a noun, add の before まえに. しょくじの まえに means “before the meal”. テストの まえに means “before the test”.

Same idea, a small の for the noun.

  • しょくじの まえに、てを あらいます。
    Shokuji no mae ni, te o araimasu.
    Before the meal, I wash my hands.
  • テストの まえに、べんきょうしました。
    Tesuto no mae ni, benkyou shimashita.
    Before the test, I studied.

Compare with てから

The same two events can be said with まえに or with てから from the earlier lesson. ねる まえに はを みがきます and はを みがいて から ねます say the same order.

With まえに you start from the later event. With てから you start from the first.

  • ねる まえに、はを みがきます。
    Neru mae ni, ha o migakimasu.
    Before sleeping, I brush my teeth.
  • はを みがいて から、ねます。
    Ha o migaite kara, nemasu.
    After brushing my teeth, I sleep.

Common mistakes

Do not use the ます form: ねます まえに is wrong. Do not use the た-form: ねた まえに is wrong, because the event has not happened yet when you are describing “before”.

Dictionary form, always.

  • ✗ ねます まえに、はを みがきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use ねる.
  • ✗ ねた まえに、はを みがきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use ねる.
  • ねる まえに、はを みがきます。
    Neru mae ni, ha o migakimasu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “Before eating, I wash my hands.”

たべる まえに、てを あらいます。

Taberu mae ni, te o araimasu.: Before eating, I wash my hands.

Remember

  • Dictionary form plus mae ni means before doing.
  • The verb before mae ni stays in the dictionary form, even for the past.
  • With a noun, add no: shokuji no mae ni.
