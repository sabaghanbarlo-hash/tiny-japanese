あとで: After

Goal: By the end you can say “After eating, I take a walk” and “I'll call you later”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say “after doing something”, put the た-form of the verb before あとで. たべた あとで means “after eating”.

たべた あとで、さんぽします means “After eating, I take a walk”.

  • ごはんを たべた あとで、さんぽします。
    Gohan o tabeta ato de, sanpo shimasu.
    After eating, I take a walk.
  • しゅくだいを した あとで、ねます。
    Shukudai o shita ato de, nemasu.
    After doing homework, I sleep.

The main verb shows the time

The verb before あとで is always in the た-form, whatever the real time is. The main verb at the end tells you if it is past or future.

みた あとで、ねました is past. おわった あとで、いきます is future.

  • テレビを みた あとで、ねました。
    Terebi o mita ato de, nemashita.
    After watching TV, I slept.
  • しごとが おわった あとで、のみに いきます。
    Shigoto ga owatta ato de, nomi ni ikimasu.
    After work ends, I'll go for a drink.

With nouns

With a noun, add の: しょくじの あとで means “after the meal”.

It works the same way as まえに.

  • しょくじの あとで、コーヒーを のみます。
    Shokuji no ato de, koohii o nomimasu.
    After the meal, I drink coffee.
  • テストの あとで、ねます。
    Tesuto no ato de, nemasu.
    After the test, I will sleep.

あとで alone

By itself, あとで means “later”. あとで でんわします means “I'll call later”.

It is a handy word for putting something off politely.

  • あとで でんわします。
    Ato de denwa shimasu.
    I'll call you later.
  • あとで いきます。
    Ato de ikimasu.
    I'll go later.

Compare with てから

てから, from the earlier lesson, stresses the order: do this first, then that. あとで simply says “after”. In many sentences you can use either.

たべて から、ねます and たべた あとで、ねます both mean “I sleep after eating”.

  • たべて から、ねます。
    Tabete kara, nemasu.
    I eat, and then I sleep.
  • たべた あとで、ねます。
    Tabeta ato de, nemasu.
    After eating, I sleep.

Common mistakes

Do not use the dictionary form before あとで: たべる あとで is wrong. Do not use the ます form: たべます あとで is wrong. This is the opposite of まえに.

Remember: まえに takes the dictionary form, and あとで takes the た-form.

  • ✗ たべる あとで、ねます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use たべた.
  • ✗ たべます あとで、ねます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use たべた.
  • たべた あとで、ねます。
    Tabeta ato de, nemasu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “After class, let's talk.”

じゅぎょうの あとで、はなしましょう。

Jugyou no ato de, hanashimashou.: After class, let's talk.

Remember

  • Ta-form plus ato de means after doing.
  • The main verb shows the time, not the first verb.
  • Mae ni takes the dictionary form; ato de takes the ta-form.
