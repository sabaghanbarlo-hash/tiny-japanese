なにか、どこか、だれか: Something, Somewhere, Someone

Goal: By the end you can say “Let's eat something” and “Shall we go somewhere?”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

Add か to a question word to make it vague. なに becomes なにか, “something”. どこ becomes どこか, “somewhere”. だれ becomes だれか, “someone”.

You are not asking which one. You just mean there is one.

  • なにか たべましょう。
    Nanika tabemashou.
    Let's eat something.
  • だれか いますか。
    Dareka imasu ka.
    Is anyone there?

Yes-or-no questions

なにを のみますか asks “What will you drink?”. なにか のみますか asks “Will you drink something?”, and you answer yes or no.

So the か word turns a “what” question into a yes-or-no question.

  • なにか のみますか。
    Nanika nomimasu ka.
    Will you drink something?
  • なにを のみますか。
    Nani o nomimasu ka.
    What will you drink?

With particles

The particles を and が are usually dropped after these words. なにか たべます sounds natural. But keep へ and に: どこかへ いきます, だれかに あいます.

A small rule that helps a lot.

  • どこかへ いきました。
    Dokoka e ikimashita.
    I went somewhere.
  • だれかに あいました。
    Dareka ni aimashita.
    I met someone.

The opposite

With a negative ending, も instead of か means “nothing” or “nobody”. なにも たべませんでした means “I ate nothing”. だれも いません means “nobody is here”.

So answer “no” to a なにか question with なにも.

  • なにも たべませんでした。
    Nani mo tabemasen deshita.
    I didn't eat anything.
  • だれも いません。
    Daremo imasen.
    Nobody is here.

Inviting

These words are great for invitations. なにか のみに いきませんか means “Shall we go and drink something?”. どこか いきましょう means “Let's go somewhere”.

It leaves the choice open and friendly.

  • なにか のみに いきませんか。
    Nanika nomi ni ikimasen ka.
    Shall we go drink something?
  • どこか いきましょう。
    Dokoka ikimashou.
    Let's go somewhere.

Common mistakes

Do not use なにか in a negative answer when you mean “nothing”. なにか たべませんでした is not what you mean; say なにも たべませんでした.

Also remember that なにを asks “what”, but なにか asks “something?”.

  • ✗ なにか たべませんでした。
    (not what you mean)
    Not “I ate nothing”: use なにも.
  • なにも たべませんでした。
    Nani mo tabemasen deshita.
    Right: I ate nothing.
Try it: Say: “Shall we go somewhere?”

どこかへ いきませんか。

Dokoka e ikimasen ka.: Shall we go somewhere?

Story: A free afternoon

  • Mika
    アレックスさん、なにか たべませんか。
    Arekkusu-san, nanika tabemasen ka.
    Alex, shall we eat something?
  • Alex
    いい ですね。どこかへ いきましょう。
    Ii desu ne. Dokoka e ikimashou.
    Sounds good. Let's go somewhere.
  • Mika
    だれか いますか。いっしょに いきたい です。
    Dareka imasu ka. Issho ni ikitai desu.
    Is anyone around? I'd like to go together.
  • Alex
    いいえ、きょうは だれも いません。
    Iie, kyou wa daremo imasen.
    No, nobody is here today.
  • Mika
    じゃあ、ふたりで なにか のみに いきませんか。
    Jaa, futari de nanika nomi ni ikimasen ka.
    Then shall we two go drink something?
  • Alex
    はい、いきましょう。
    Hai, ikimashou.
    Yes, let's go.

Remember

  • Add ka to a question word: nanika means something.
  • Nanika makes a yes or no question; nani o asks what.
  • Nani mo with a negative means nothing.
