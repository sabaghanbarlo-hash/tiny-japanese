～ながら: While Doing

Goal: By the end you can say “I study while listening to music”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

The idea

ながら means “while”. Take the ます form, remove ます, and add ながら. ききます becomes ききながら, “while listening”.

It joins two actions done at the same time.

  • うたを ききながら、べんきょうします。
    Uta o kikinagara, benkyou shimasu.
    I study while listening to a song.
  • テレビを みながら、ごはんを たべます。
    Terebi o minagara, gohan o tabemasu.
    I eat while watching TV.

The main action

The verb at the end is the main action. The ながら verb is the side action. あるきながら はなします means “I talk while walking”, and talking is the main thing.

So think about which action is more important, and put it last.

  • あるきながら、はなします。
    Aruki nagara, hanashimasu.
    I talk while walking.
  • コーヒーを のみながら、しんぶんを よみます。
    Koohii o nominagara, shinbun o yomimasu.
    I read the newspaper while drinking coffee.

Same person

Both actions must be done by the same person. You cannot use ながら when two different people do the actions.

うたいながら あるきます means “I walk while singing”.

  • うたいながら、あるきます。
    Utai nagara, arukimasu.
    I walk while singing.
  • わらいながら、はなしました。
    Warai nagara, hanashimashita.
    I talked while laughing.

Tense

The ながら verb never changes for tense. Only the verb at the end shows past or present.

ききながら そうじしました means “I cleaned while listening”.

  • おんがくを ききながら、そうじしました。
    Ongaku o kikinagara, souji shimashita.
    I cleaned while listening to music.
  • おんがくを ききながら、そうじします。
    Ongaku o kikinagara, souji shimasu.
    I clean while listening to music.

Compare with て

The て-form joins actions one after another. ながら joins actions at the same time. たべて、ねます means “eat, then sleep”. たべながら、はなします means “talk while eating”.

Order, or at once.

  • ごはんを たべて、ねます。
    Gohan o tabete, nemasu.
    I eat, then sleep.
  • ごはんを たべながら、はなします。
    Gohan o tabenagara, hanashimasu.
    I talk while eating.

Common mistakes

Do not keep ます: ききますながら is wrong. Do not use the て-form: きいて ながら is wrong.

Stem plus ながら, nothing else.

  • ✗ ききますながら、べんきょうします。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the stem.
  • ✗ きいて ながら、べんきょうします。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use the stem.
  • ききながら、べんきょうします。
    Kikinagara, benkyou shimasu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “I study while listening to music.”

おんがくを ききながら、べんきょうします。

Ongaku o kikinagara, benkyou shimasu.: I study while listening to music.

Remember

  • Remove masu and add nagara to say while doing.
  • The last verb is the main action.
  • Nagara is for one person doing two things at once.
