あげます と もらいます: Give and Receive

Goal: By the end you can say “I gave a flower to a friend” and “I received a book from Mika”.

Watch the lesson

The lesson, step by step

Giving

あげます means “give”. The pattern is: giver は, receiver に, thing を, あげます. わたしは いもうとに ケーキを あげます means “I give my sister a cake”.

The receiver is marked with に.

  • わたしは いもうとに ケーキを あげます。
    Watashi wa imouto ni keeki o agemasu.
    I give my younger sister a cake.
  • ミカさんは アレックスさんに ほんを あげました。
    Mika-san wa Arekkusu-san ni hon o agemashita.
    Mika gave Alex a book.

Receiving

もらいます means “receive”. Now the receiver is the topic. The pattern is: receiver は, giver に or から, thing を, もらいます.

わたしは ともだちに はなを もらいました means “I received a flower from a friend”.

  • わたしは ともだちに はなを もらいました。
    Watashi wa tomodachi ni hana o moraimashita.
    I received a flower from a friend.
  • アレックスさんは ミカさんから ほんを もらいました。
    Arekkusu-san wa Mika-san kara hon o moraimashita.
    Alex received a book from Mika.

Two sides of one event

The same event can be said both ways. ミカさんは アレックスさんに ほんを あげました starts from Mika. アレックスさんは ミカさんに ほんを もらいました starts from Alex.

Choose the view of the person you are talking about.

  • ミカさんは アレックスさんに ほんを あげました。
    Mika-san wa Arekkusu-san ni hon o agemashita.
    Mika gave Alex a book.
  • アレックスさんは ミカさんに ほんを もらいました。
    Arekkusu-san wa Mika-san ni hon o moraimashita.
    Alex received a book from Mika.

に or から

With もらいます, the giver can be marked with に or から. から is also natural when the giver is a company or a group. With あげます, the receiver is always に.

Both are common for people.

  • ははに プレゼントを もらいました。
    Haha ni purezento o moraimashita.
    I received a present from my mother.
  • ともだちから てがみを もらいました。
    Tomodachi kara tegami o moraimashita.
    I received a letter from a friend.

Be careful with tone

あげます can sound as if you are doing a favour. With a teacher or a boss, it can sound a little too direct, and Japanese has a humbler verb for that, which you will meet later. With friends and family it is perfect.

For now, use it for friends, family and younger people.

  • いもうとに おかしを あげました。
    Imouto ni okashi o agemashita.
    I gave my younger sister some sweets.
  • ともだちに プレゼントを あげます。
    Tomodachi ni purezento o agemasu.
    I give a friend a present.

Common mistakes

Do not use あげます when someone gives something to you. ミカさんは わたしに ほんを あげました is wrong. You need a different verb, くれます, which is the next lesson.

You can always use もらいます to say you received it.

  • ✗ ミカさんは わたしに ほんを あげました。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use くれました.
  • わたしは ミカさんに ほんを もらいました。
    Watashi wa Mika-san ni hon o moraimashita.
    Right: I received a book from Mika.
Try it: Say: “I gave a flower to my friend.”

ともだちに はなを あげました。

Tomodachi ni hana o agemashita.: I gave a flower to my friend.

Story: Birthday

  • Mika
    きのうは たんじょうびでした。
    Kinou wa tanjoubi deshita.
    Yesterday was my birthday.
  • Alex
    おめでとう ございます。わたしは ミカさんに ほんを あげます。
    Omedetou gozaimasu. Watashi wa Mika-san ni hon o agemasu.
    Happy birthday. I'll give you a book.
  • Mika
    ありがとう。ははに ケーキを もらいました。
    Arigatou. Haha ni keeki o moraimashita.
    Thank you. I received a cake from my mother.
  • Alex
    いい ですね。
    Ii desu ne.
    That's nice.
  • Mika
    ともだちから てがみも もらいました。
    Tomodachi kara tegami mo moraimashita.
    I also received a letter from a friend.

Remember

  • Agemasu: giver wa, receiver ni, thing o.
  • Moraimasu: receiver wa, giver ni or kara, thing o.
  • Do not use agemasu when someone gives to you.
