かぞえかた: Counting

Goal: By the end you can say “three apples”, “two people” and “two notebooks”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

In Japanese you use a different counting word depending on what you count. For many things you can use the general series: ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ, よっつ, いつつ.

These mean one, two, three, four and five things.

  • りんごを みっつ かいました。
    Ringo o mittsu kaimashita.
    I bought three apples.
  • ケーキを ふたつ ください。
    Keeki o futatsu kudasai.
    Two cakes, please.

People

For people, one person is ひとり, two people are ふたり. From three on, use にん: さんにん, よにん, ごにん. Note that four people is よにん, not よんにん.

The first two are special words to learn by heart.

  • ともだちが さんにん きます。
    Tomodachi ga sannin kimasu.
    Three friends are coming.
  • ふたりで いきます。
    Futari de ikimasu.
    We two will go.

Flat things

For flat, thin things such as paper, tickets and plates, use まい after the number. いちまい is one sheet, にまい is two, さんまい is three.

It is easy: number plus まい.

  • きっぷを にまい ください。
    Kippu o nimai kudasai.
    Two tickets, please.
  • かみを さんまい かいました。
    Kami o sanmai kaimashita.
    I bought three sheets of paper.

Books

For books, notebooks and magazines, use さつ. One is いっさつ, two is にさつ, three is さんさつ.

The first one has a little pause: いっさつ.

  • ノートを にさつ かいました。
    Nooto o nisatsu kaimashita.
    I bought two notebooks.
  • ほんを いっさつ よみました。
    Hon o issatsu yomimashita.
    I read one book.

Where it goes

The count goes right after the thing and its particle, before the verb. りんごを みっつ かいます. The count does not take a particle of its own.

To ask “how many?”, use いくつ, なんにん, なんまい or なんさつ.

  • りんごを いくつ かいましたか。
    Ringo o ikutsu kaimashita ka.
    How many apples did you buy?
  • ともだちが なんにん きますか。
    Tomodachi ga nannin kimasu ka.
    How many friends are coming?

Common mistakes

Do not use ひとり for things: りんごを ひとり かいました is wrong. Use ひとつ. Do not say よんにん; say よにん.

Choose the counter by what you count: things, people, flat things, books.

  • ✗ りんごを ふたり かいました。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ふたり is for people.
  • りんごを ふたつ かいました。
    Ringo o futatsu kaimashita.
    Right: I bought two apples.
Try it: Say: “I bought two notebooks.”

ノートを にさつ かいました。

Nooto o nisatsu kaimashita.: I bought two notebooks.

Vocabulary

  • ひとつhitotsuone (thing)
  • ふたつfutatsutwo (things)
  • みっつmittsuthree (things)
  • ひとりhitorione person
  • ふたりfutaritwo people
  • まいmaicounter for flat things
  • さつsatsucounter for books
  • いくつikutsuhow many

Remember

  • Use hitotsu, futatsu, mittsu for most things.
  • People: hitori, futari, then sannin, yonin.
  • Put the count after the thing and before the verb.
