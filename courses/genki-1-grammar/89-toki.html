とき: When

Goal: By the end you can say “When I'm free, I read” and “When I went to Japan, I bought a souvenir”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

とき means “when” or “at the time of”. Put the plain form before it. ひまな とき means “when I'm free”.

Then say what happens: ひまな とき、ほんを よみます.

  • ひまな とき、ほんを よみます。
    Hima na toki, hon o yomimasu.
    When I'm free, I read.
  • さむい とき、うちに います。
    Samui toki, uchi ni imasu.
    When it's cold, I stay home.

Which form

After an い-adjective, use the plain form: さむい とき. After a な-adjective, add な: しずかな とき. After a noun, add の: あめの とき. After a verb, use the plain form: いく とき.

It is the same system you used before nouns.

  • あめの とき、バスで いきます。
    Ame no toki, basu de ikimasu.
    When it rains, I go by bus.
  • しずかな とき、ほんを よみます。
    Shizuka na toki, hon o yomimasu.
    When it's quiet, I read.

Dictionary or た-form

With verbs, the tense shows the order. The dictionary form means the action is not finished when the main action happens. にほんへ いく とき means “when I'm going to Japan”. The た-form means it is finished. にほんへ いった とき means “when I went to Japan” or “once I was in Japan”.

Think: not finished yet, or already done.

  • にほんへ いく とき、かばんを かいました。
    Nihon e iku toki, kaban o kaimashita.
    When I was going to Japan, I bought a bag.
  • にほんへ いった とき、おみやげを かいました。
    Nihon e itta toki, omiyage o kaimashita.
    When I went to Japan, I bought souvenirs.

With past and more

The main sentence can be past or present. ちいさい とき means “when I was little”. こどもの とき means “when I was a child”.

The とき clause comes first.

  • こどもの とき、うみへ よく いきました。
    Kodomo no toki, umi e yoku ikimashita.
    When I was a child, I often went to the sea.
  • ちいさい とき、ピアノを ひきました。
    Chiisai toki, piano o hikimashita.
    When I was little, I played the piano.

Common mistakes

Do not use だ before とき: あめだ とき is wrong; say あめの とき. Do not use the ます form: いきます とき is wrong; say いく とき.

Noun: の. な-adjective: な. Verb: plain form.

  • ✗ あめだ とき、バスで いきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use あめの とき.
  • ✗ いきます とき、でんわします。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: use いく とき.
  • いく とき、でんわします。
    Iku toki, denwa shimasu.
    Right: When I go, I call.
Try it: Say: “When I'm free, I read.”

ひまな とき、ほんを よみます。

Hima na toki, hon o yomimasu.: When I'm free, I read.

Remember

  • Plain form plus toki means when.
  • Nouns take no, na-adjectives take na: ame no toki, hima na toki.
  • Dictionary form is not finished yet; ta-form is finished.
