ずつ: Each, at a Time

Goal: By the end you can say “one each” and “I study a little every day”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

ずつ after an amount means “each” or “at a time”. ひとつずつ means “one each” or “one at a time”.

It spreads the amount evenly.

  • ひとつずつ たべます。
    Hitotsu zutsu tabemasu.
    I eat them one at a time.
  • ふたつずつ かいました。
    Futatsu zutsu kaimashita.
    I bought two of each.

With people

With giving, it says what each person gets. ひとりに ひとつずつ あげます means “I give one to each person”.

You learned あげます earlier.

  • ひとりに ひとつずつ あげます。
    Hitori ni hitotsu zutsu agemasu.
    I give one to each person.
  • ふたりに ケーキを ひとつずつ あげました。
    Futari ni keeki o hitotsu zutsu agemashita.
    I gave each of the two people a cake.

Little by little

すこしずつ means “little by little”. It is great for talking about slow progress.

まいにち すこしずつ べんきょうします means “I study a little every day”.

  • まいにち すこしずつ べんきょうします。
    Mainichi sukoshi zutsu benkyou shimasu.
    I study a little every day.
  • すこしずつ じょうずに なりました。
    Sukoshi zutsu jouzu ni narimashita.
    I got better little by little.

Compare with だけ

ずつ means each or at a time. だけ means only. ひとつずつ かいました means “I bought one of each”. ひとつだけ かいました means “I bought only one”.

Be careful, the two are very different.

  • ひとつずつ かいました。
    Hitotsu zutsu kaimashita.
    I bought one of each.
  • ひとつだけ かいました。
    Hitotsu dake kaimashita.
    I bought only one.

Common mistakes

Put ずつ after the amount, not before. ずつ ひとつ is wrong. Also do not use it with a plain thing without an amount: りんごずつ is wrong.

Amount first, then ずつ.

  • ✗ ずつ ひとつ たべます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: ひとつずつ.
  • ひとつずつ たべます。
    Hitotsu zutsu tabemasu.
    Right: one at a time.
Try it: Say: “I study a little every day.”

まいにち すこしずつ べんきょうします。

Mainichi sukoshi zutsu benkyou shimasu.: I study a little every day.

Remember

  • Amount plus zutsu means each or at a time.
  • Sukoshi zutsu means little by little.
  • Zutsu means each; dake means only.
