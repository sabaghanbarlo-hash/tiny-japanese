～に します: I'll Have That

Goal: By the end you can say “I'll have coffee” and ask “Which one will you take?”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

To say what you choose, put the thing before に します. コーヒーに します means “I'll have coffee” or “I'll go with coffee”.

します here means “decide on”.

  • コーヒーに します。
    Koohii ni shimasu.
    I'll have coffee.
  • わたしは ラーメンに します。
    Watashi wa raamen ni shimasu.
    I'll have ramen.

Asking

To ask someone to choose, use どれ, “which one”. どれに しますか means “Which one will you take?”. Or なにに しますか, “What will you have?”.

Waiters use it a lot.

  • どれに しますか。
    Dore ni shimasu ka.
    Which one will you take?
  • なにに しますか。
    Nani ni shimasu ka.
    What will you have?

Ordering

It is the natural way to order. わたしは カレーに します means “I'll have curry”. わたしも おなじ ものに します means “I'll have the same”.

It is polite and simple.

  • わたしは カレーに します。
    Watashi wa karee ni shimasu.
    I'll have curry.
  • わたしも おなじ ものに します。
    Watashi mo onaji mono ni shimasu.
    I'll have the same.

します or なります

に します is a choice you make. に なります, from the earlier lesson, is a change that happens. コーヒーに します is “I choose coffee”. げんきに なります is “I become well”.

Remember who is in control. If you decide, use します.

  • コーヒーに します。
    Koohii ni shimasu.
    I choose coffee.
  • げんきに なります。
    Genki ni narimasu.
    I become well.

Common mistakes

Do not use を to choose: コーヒーを します means something else, since します then means “do”. Use に: コーヒーに します.

Also do not use に なります for your own choice.

  • ✗ コーヒーを します。
    (wrong here)
    Not “I'll have coffee”: use に します.
  • コーヒーに します。
    Koohii ni shimasu.
    Right: I'll have coffee.
Try it: Say: “I'll have tea.”

おちゃに します。

Ocha ni shimasu.: I'll have tea.

Remember

  • Thing plus ni shimasu means I'll have that.
  • Dore ni shimasu ka asks which one will you take.
  • Shimasu is a choice; narimasu is a change that happens.
