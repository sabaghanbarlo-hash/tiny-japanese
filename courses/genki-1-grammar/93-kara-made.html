から / まで: From and Until

Goal: By the end you can say “I work from nine to five” and “I walk from home to the station”.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

から means “from”. まで means “until” or “up to”. Put them right after a time or a place.

くじから ごじまで はたらきます means “I work from nine to five”.

  • くじから ごじまで はたらきます。
    Kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu.
    I work from nine to five.
  • ひるごはんは じゅうにじから です。
    Hirugohan wa juuniji kara desu.
    Lunch is from twelve.

Places

The same two words work for places. うちから えきまで あるきます means “I walk from home to the station”.

から marks the starting point. まで marks the end point.

  • うちから えきまで あるきます。
    Uchi kara eki made arukimasu.
    I walk from home to the station.
  • ここから あそこまで いきます。
    Koko kara asoko made ikimasu.
    I go from here to there.

Only one side

You do not need both. くじから says only the start. ごじまで says only the end.

Add です or a verb as you like.

  • がっこうは くじから です。
    Gakkou wa kuji kara desu.
    School is from nine.
  • ごじまで まって ください。
    Goji made matte kudasai.
    Please wait until five.

まで or に

まで says an action continues up to a point. に says one moment. Compare the two.

ごじまで はたらきます means “I work until five”. ごじに かえります means “I go home at five”.

  • ごじまで はたらきます。
    Goji made hatarakimasu.
    I work until five.
  • ごじに かえります。
    Goji ni kaerimasu.
    I go home at five.

Not the reason から

You already know から for “because”, after a full sentence. After a noun it means “from”. The position tells you which one it is.

あつい から、みずを のみます is “Because it is hot, I drink water”.

  • あつい から、みずを のみます。
    Atsui kara, mizu o nomimasu.
    Because it is hot, I drink water.
  • くじから はたらきます。
    Kuji kara hatarakimasu.
    I work from nine.

Common mistakes

Put から after the time, not before it. から くじ is wrong. Say くじから.

Do not use に when you mean “until”. Use まで.

  • ✗ から くじ ごじまで はたらきます。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: から goes after the time.
  • くじから ごじまで はたらきます。
    Kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “I work from nine to five.”

くじから ごじまで はたらきます。

Kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu.: I work from nine to five.

Remember

  • Kara means from and made means until. Both come after the word.
  • Made lasts up to a point. Ni is for one moment.
  • After a noun, kara means from. After a sentence, it means because.
