の as “One”: The Red One

Goal: By the end you can say “Please give me the red one” and “This is mine”.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech); teacher narration: neural text-to-speech.

The lesson, step by step

The idea

When both people know the thing you mean, you do not need to say the noun again. Put の where the noun would be. あかい の means “the red one”.

It is like “one” in English: the red one, the big one.

  • あかい の を ください。
    Akai no o kudasai.
    Please give me the red one.
  • おおきい のが いい です。
    Ookii no ga ii desu.
    The big one is good.

Mine

Use の after a person to say it belongs to them. これは わたしの です means “This is mine”.

The thing itself is left out, because it is clear.

  • これは わたしの です。
    Kore wa watashi no desu.
    This is mine.
  • これは ミカさんの です。
    Kore wa Mika-san no desu.
    This is Mika's.

な-adjectives

After a な-adjective, you need な before の. きれいな の means “the pretty one”.

い-adjectives go straight to の, as in あかい の.

  • きれいな の を ください。
    Kirei na no o kudasai.
    Please give me the pretty one.
  • しずかな の が いい です。
    Shizuka na no ga ii desu.
    I prefer the quiet one.

Choosing

You can compare two things. あかい のと くろい の、どちらが やすい ですか means “Which is cheaper, the red one or the black one?”.

Use の twice, for each side.

  • あかい のと くろい の、どちらが やすい ですか。
    Akai no to kuroi no, dochira ga yasui desu ka.
    Which is cheaper, the red one or the black one?
  • あかい のが やすい です。
    Akai no ga yasui desu.
    The red one is cheaper.

About people

の stands for things, not for people. To say “the tall person”, keep the noun ひと: せが たかい ひと.

Do not say たかい の when you mean a person.

  • せが たかい ひとは アレックスさん です。
    Se ga takai hito wa Arekkusu-san desu.
    The tall person is Alex.
  • あかい のを ください。
    Akai no o kudasai.
    Please give me the red one.

Common mistakes

After a な-adjective, you cannot skip な. きれい の is wrong. Say きれいな の.

Say the owner, then の. Do not add の twice, like わたしの の.

  • ✗ きれい の を ください。
    (wrong)
    Wrong: add な before の.
  • きれいな の を ください。
    Kirei na no o kudasai.
    Right.
Try it: Say: “Please give me the red one.”

あかい の を ください。

Akai no o kudasai.: Please give me the red one.

Story: Choosing a bag

  • Staff
    いらっしゃいませ。
    Irasshaimase.
    Welcome.
  • Mika
    あの あかい かばんを みせて ください。
    Ano akai kaban o misete kudasai.
    Please show me that red bag.
  • Staff
    どうぞ。くろい のも ありますよ。
    Douzo. Kuroi no mo arimasu yo.
    Here you are. We also have a black one.
  • Mika
    あかい のと くろい の、どちらが やすい ですか。
    Akai no to kuroi no, dochira ga yasui desu ka.
    Which is cheaper, the red one or the black one?
  • Staff
    あかい のが やすい です。
    Akai no ga yasui desu.
    The red one is cheaper.
  • Mika
    じゃ、あかい のを ください。
    Ja, akai no o kudasai.
    Then I will take the red one.

Remember

  • No can stand for a noun that is already clear: akai no is the red one.
  • Add na before no after a na-adjective: kirei na no.
  • After a name, no alone means that person's: Mika-san no.
