Course · Beginner

Genki I Grammar, Point by Point

Every Genki I grammar point gets its own lesson: a deep explanation, examples, common mistakes and a story.

  • 32 lessons
  • about 13.5 hours
  • Textbook companion

An independent study companion for the Genki I textbook, not affiliated with its publisher. Each lesson teaches one grammar point from start to finish: what it does, how it works, examples, the mistakes beginners make, a short story with Mika and Alex, vocabulary and a quiz. New chapters are added as they are finished, in the same order as the book.

Free while we launch

Everything in this course is open to read right now, no account needed.

Start lesson 1

What you'll learn

  • Understand each basic grammar point on its own, not just memorise it
  • Make polite sentences with は, です, か, の, も and the negative
  • Point at things, ask questions and sound natural with ね and よ
  • Build sentences with verbs, the ます form, particles and the right word order

Who it's for: Complete beginners who can read hiragana and want every grammar point explained in depth.

Lessons

  1. 1は: The Topic MarkerLearn what は marks, why it is read “wa”, and how to talk about someone or something.
  2. 2です: The Polite “Is / Am / Are”Learn what です does, why it never changes, and how it finishes a polite sentence.
  3. 3か: The Question ParticleTurn a sentence into a yes/no question, and ask with question words.
  4. 4の: Linking NounsSay “my teacher”, “a Japanese teacher” and “the school's festival” with の.
  5. 5これ, それ, あれ: This and ThatLearn the this / that / that-over-there words and the question word どれ.
  6. 6も: Also, TooUse も to say “too” and “also”.
  7. 7じゃ ありません: Saying “Is Not”Learn the polite negative of です and how to answer “no”.
  8. 8ね: Looking for AgreementUse ね to say “right?” or “isn't it”, and to share feelings.
  9. 9よ: Telling Something NewUse よ to give information the listener may not know, and see how it differs from ね.
  10. 10Verbs, Part 1: Ichidan (る) VerbsMeet Japanese verbs and the first verb group, called ichidan or る-verbs.
  11. 11Verbs, Part 2: Godan (う) VerbsMeet the second verb group, godan or う-verbs, and learn how to tell it from ichidan.
  12. 12Verbs, Part 3: する and くるLearn the two irregular verbs, する and くる, and the huge noun + する pattern.
  13. 13ます Form, Part 1: Ichidan VerbsMake the polite ます form of ichidan verbs by dropping る and adding ます.
  14. 14ます Form, Part 2: Godan VerbsMake the polite ます form of godan verbs by changing the last sound to its い-sound.
  15. 15ます Form, Part 3: します and きますLearn the polite forms of the two irregular verbs, する and くる.
  16. 16Present Tense: ます and ませんUse the polite present for habits and the future, and make it negative with ません.
  17. 17を: The Object MarkerMark what the action is done to: eat bread, read a book, watch TV.
  18. 18で: Place of Action and MeansSay where you do things and how you get there.
  19. 19に: Time, Goal and TargetUse に for clock times, destinations and the person you meet.
  20. 20へ: DirectionSay where you are heading with へ, read “e”.
  21. 21ませんか: Inviting SomeoneUse ませんか to invite people politely, and learn how to accept or decline.
  22. 22Frequency AdverbsSay how often you do things with いつも, よく, ときどき, あまり and ぜんぜん.
  23. 23Word OrderLearn where each part of a Japanese sentence goes, and why particles make it flexible.
  24. 24ある and いる: Saying What ExistsSay that something or someone exists or is somewhere, with あります and います.
  25. 25Describing Where Things AreUse うえ, した, なか, まえ, うしろ, となり and friends to say exactly where something is.
  26. 26でした: The Past of ですSay what something was, and was not, with でした and じゃ ありませんでした.
  27. 27ました: The Past of VerbsSay what you did, and did not do, with ました and ませんでした.
  28. 28にも and でも: Adding も to Place ParticlesCombine も with に and で, and know when も replaces を, は and が.
  29. 29～じかん: How LongSay how many hours, days, weeks and months something takes, and ask with どのくらい.
  30. 30たくさん: Saying “a Lot”Say that there are many things or that you do something a lot, and know the other quantity words.
  31. 31と: And, WithJoin nouns with と and say who you do things with.
  32. 32が: The Subject MarkerSee what が marks, and when it replaces は.
