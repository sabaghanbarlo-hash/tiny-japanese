Genki I Grammar, Point by Point

Every Genki I grammar point gets its own lesson: a deep explanation, examples, common mistakes and a story.

  • 9 lessons
  • about 4 hours
  • Textbook companion

An independent study companion for the Genki I textbook, not affiliated with its publisher. Each lesson teaches one grammar point from start to finish: what it does, how it works, examples, the mistakes beginners make, a short story with Mika and Alex, vocabulary and a quiz. New chapters are added as they are finished, in the same order as the book.

Start lesson 1

What you'll learn

  • Understand each basic grammar point on its own, not just memorise it
  • Make polite sentences with は, です, か, の, も and the negative
  • Point at things, ask questions and sound natural with ね and よ

Who it's for: Complete beginners who can read hiragana and want every grammar point explained in depth.

Lessons

  1. 1は: The Topic MarkerLearn what は marks, why it is read “wa”, and how to talk about someone or something.
  2. 2です: The Polite “Is / Am / Are”Learn what です does, why it never changes, and how it finishes a polite sentence.
  3. 3か: The Question ParticleTurn a sentence into a yes/no question, and ask with question words.
  4. 4の: Linking NounsSay “my teacher”, “a Japanese teacher” and “the school's festival” with の.
  5. 5これ, それ, あれ: This and ThatLearn the this / that / that-over-there words and the question word どれ.
  6. 6も: Also, TooUse も to say “too” and “also”.
  7. 7じゃ ありません: Saying “Is Not”Learn the polite negative of です and how to answer “no”.
  8. 8ね: Looking for AgreementUse ね to say “right?” or “isn't it”, and to share feelings.
  9. 9よ: Telling Something NewUse よ to give information the listener may not know, and see how it differs from ね.
