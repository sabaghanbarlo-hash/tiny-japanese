は: The Topic Marker

Goal: By the end you can say who you are and ask about someone with は.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

は points at the topic: the thing you are about to talk about. Think of it as “As for ...”.

It goes right after the topic word. Whatever comes after is the comment about that topic.

How it works, with examples

Topic は comment

ミカさんは こうこうせい です。 means “As for Mika, she is a high school student”. The topic is ミカさん, and こうこうせい です is what we say about her.

Written は, read “wa”

As a particle, は is pronounced “wa”. You write わたしは, but you say “watashi wa”. Only the particle は does this; はい is still “hai”.

Asking back with は

Leave the comment off to ask “And what about ...?”: アレックスさんは？ means “And you, Alex?” or “What about Alex?”. It is the easiest way to bounce a question back.

は can also contrast

は can say “this one, but not that one”. コーヒーは のみます。 おちゃは のみません。 means “Coffee I drink, but tea I don't”. You will meet this contrast later, so just notice it now.

Story: Two new classmates

  • Mika
    わたしは ミカ です。こうこうせい です。
    Watashi wa Mika desu. Koukousei desu.
    I'm Mika. I'm a high school student.
  • Alex
    ぼくは アレックス です。だいがくせい です。
    Boku wa Arekkusu desu. Daigakusei desu.
    I'm Alex. I'm a university student.
  • Mika
    アレックスさんは アメリカじん ですか。
    Arekkusu-san wa Amerikajin desu ka.
    Are you American, Alex?
  • Alex
    はい、アメリカじん です。ミカさんは？
    Hai, Amerikajin desu. Mika-san wa?
    Yes, I'm American. And you, Mika?
  • Mika
    わたしは にほんじん です。
    Watashi wa nihonjin desu.
    I'm Japanese.
  • Alex
    にほんごの せんせいは？
    Nihongo no sensei wa?
    And the Japanese teacher?
  • Mika
    せんせいも にほんじん です。
    Sensei mo nihonjin desu.
    The teacher is Japanese too.

Vocabulary

  • わたし / ぼくwatashi / bokuI (ぼく is used by boys and men)
  • ～さん-sanMr, Ms
  • こうこうせいkoukouseihigh school student
  • だいがくせいdaigakuseiuniversity student
  • にほんじんnihonjinJapanese person
  • アメリカじんAmerikajinAmerican person
  • せんせいsenseiteacher

Remember

  • は is read “wa” only when it is a particle. The same character in はい is still “ha”.
  • Do not say さん about yourself. You are ミカ です, never ミカさん です.
