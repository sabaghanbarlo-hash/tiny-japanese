か: The Question Particle

Goal: By the end you can ask yes/no questions and answer them.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

Japanese questions are easy: take a normal sentence and put か at the end.

There is no question mark needed, but your voice goes up a little on か.

How it works, with examples

Sentence + か

アレックスさんは がくせい です。 is “Alex is a student”. Add か and アレックスさんは がくせい ですか。 is “Is Alex a student?”.

Answer with はい or いいえ

はい means “yes” and いいえ means “no”: がくせい ですか。 → はい、がくせい です。 or いいえ、がくせい じゃ ありません。 You will learn the negative later; for now, answer はい.

Question words

Words like なに (what), だれ (who) and どこ (where) already make the sentence a question, and you still put か at the end: これは なん ですか。 means “What is this?”. Before です, なに becomes なん.

Do not use か at the end of a statement

か only asks. If you want to say something, end with です. If you want to ask, end with ですか.

Story: At the school festival

  • Alex
    すみません。これは なん ですか。
    Sumimasen. Kore wa nan desu ka.
    Excuse me. What is this?
  • Mika
    それは たこやき です。
    Sore wa takoyaki desu.
    That's takoyaki.
  • Alex
    たこやきは にほんの りょうり ですか。
    Takoyaki wa nihon no ryouri desu ka.
    Is takoyaki a Japanese dish?
  • Mika
    はい、そうです。
    Hai, sou desu.
    Yes, it is.
  • Alex
    あの ひとは だれ ですか。
    Ano hito wa dare desu ka.
    Who is that person?
  • Mika
    あの ひとは わたしの せんせい です。
    Ano hito wa watashi no sensei desu.
    That person is my teacher.
  • Alex
    せんせいは たこやきの みせの ひと ですか。
    Sensei wa takoyaki no mise no hito desu ka.
    Is your teacher a person from the takoyaki shop?
  • Mika
    いいえ、ちがいます。おきゃくさん です。
    Iie, chigaimasu. Okyakusan desu.
    No, not at all. A customer.

Vocabulary

  • なに / なんnani / nanwhat (なん before です)
  • だれdarewho
  • たこやきtakoyakitakoyaki, octopus balls
  • りょうりryouridish, cooking
  • みせmiseshop
  • おきゃくさんokyakusancustomer
  • ちがいますchigaimasuthat's wrong, no

Remember

  • Say it with a slightly rising voice on か, and you will sound natural.
  • なん is used before です and before words starting with t, d or n; otherwise you say なに.
