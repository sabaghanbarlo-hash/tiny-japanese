Lesson 3 of 10 · 10 min

の: Linking Nouns

Goal: By the end you can join two nouns with の in the right order.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

の links two nouns. The first noun describes or owns the second one.

It is like English “'s” or “of”, but the order is reversed.

How it works, with examples

Owner の thing

わたしの かばん is “my bag” and ミカさんの かさ is “Mika's umbrella”. The owner comes first, then の, then the thing.

Describing with の

にほんごの ほん is “a Japanese book (a book about Japanese)”. だいがくの せんせい is “a university teacher”. The first noun says what kind.

Chain them

You can link more than two: わたしの ともだちの かばん is “my friend's bag”. Always read from the left: the last noun is the main thing.

Remember: の is not は

ミカさんは せんせい です。 uses は because Mika is the topic. ミカさんの せんせい uses の because it names Mika's teacher.

Story: Whose bag is it?

  • Alex
    あ、かばんが あります。
    A, kaban ga arimasu.
    Oh, there's a bag.
  • Mika
    これは だれの かばん ですか。
    Kore wa dare no kaban desu ka.
    Whose bag is this?
  • Alex
    わたしの かばん じゃ ありません。
    Watashi no kaban ja arimasen.
    It's not my bag.
  • Mika
    あ、これは ともだちの かばん です。
    A, kore wa tomodachi no kaban desu.
    Oh, this is my friend's bag.
  • Alex
    ともだちの なまえは？
    Tomodachi no namae wa?
    What's your friend's name?
  • Mika
    さくらさん です。さくらさんの ほんも ここに あります。
    Sakura-san desu. Sakura-san no hon mo koko ni arimasu.
    It's Sakura. Her book is here too.
  • Alex
    にほんごの ほん ですね。
    Nihongo no hon desu ne.
    It's a Japanese book, isn't it.

Vocabulary

  • かばんkabanbag
  • かさkasaumbrella
  • ほんhonbook
  • ともだちtomodachifriend
  • なまえnamaename
  • だれのdare nowhose
  • ここkokohere

Remember

  • Read のs from the left. わたしの ともだちの かばん is “my friend's bag”.
  • の links two nouns. It does not link a noun to a verb.
