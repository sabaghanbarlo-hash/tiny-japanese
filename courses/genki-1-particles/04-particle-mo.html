Lesson 4 of 10 · 10 min

も: Also, Too

Goal: By the end you can say “me too” and link two similar sentences.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

も means “also” or “too”. It replaces は when the new topic is the same as before.

It is a tiny word, but it keeps your Japanese from repeating itself.

How it works, with examples

は becomes も

わたしは がくせい です。 → わたしも がくせい です。 means “I'm a student too”. You do not use both: も takes the place of は.

Same comment, new topic

ミカさんは にほんじん です。 せんせいも にほんじん です。 The comment is the same, so the second sentence changes the topic and adds も.

Me too

A short way to say “me too” is わたしも です。 or just わたしも。 You can drop です in casual talk.

も and ですか

アレックスさんも ですか。 means “Is Alex too?”. も does not stop you from asking a question.

Story: Same class, same lunch

  • Mika
    わたしは こうこうせい です。
    Watashi wa koukousei desu.
    I'm a high school student.
  • Alex
    さくらさんも こうこうせい ですか。
    Sakura-san mo koukousei desu ka.
    Is Sakura a high school student too?
  • Mika
    はい、さくらさんも こうこうせい です。
    Hai, Sakura-san mo koukousei desu.
    Yes, Sakura is a high school student too.
  • Alex
    わたしの ともだちも こうこうせい です。
    Watashi no tomodachi mo koukousei desu.
    My friend is a high school student too.
  • Mika
    ともだちの なまえは？
    Tomodachi no namae wa?
    What's your friend's name?
  • Alex
    ケンです。ケンさんも にほんごの クラスの ひと です。
    Ken desu. Ken-san mo nihongo no kurasu no hito desu.
    It's Ken. Ken is also in the Japanese class.

Vocabulary

  • もmoalso, too
  • クラスkurasuclass
  • ひとhitoperson
  • おなじonajithe same
  • ともだちtomodachifriend

Remember

  • も takes the place of は. Do not write ミカさんは も.
  • も only works when the new sentence has the same comment as the one before.
