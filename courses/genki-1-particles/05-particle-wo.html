を: The Object Marker

Goal: By the end you can say what you eat, drink, read and watch.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

を marks the object of a verb: the thing the action happens to.

In 「パンを たべます」, bread is the thing being eaten.

How it works, with examples

Thing を verb

パンを たべます。 means “I eat bread”. ほんを よみます。 means “I read a book”. The verb always goes last.

Written を, read “o”

を is only used as a particle, and it is pronounced “o”. You write ほんを, you say “hon o”.

Mark one thing at a time

テレビを みます。 means “I watch TV”. Use を on the thing the verb acts on, not on a place or a person.

Combine with the topic

わたしは コーヒーを のみます。 has two jobs: は marks the topic (I) and を marks the object (coffee).

Story: What do you have in the morning?

  • Mika
    アレックスさん、あさ なにを たべますか。
    Arekkusu-san, asa nani o tabemasu ka.
    Alex, what do you eat in the morning?
  • Alex
    パンを たべます。そして コーヒーを のみます。
    Pan o tabemasu. Soshite koohii o nomimasu.
    I eat bread. And I drink coffee.
  • Mika
    わたしは ごはんを たべます。おちゃを のみます。
    Watashi wa gohan o tabemasu. Ocha o nomimasu.
    I eat rice. I drink tea.
  • Alex
    あさ テレビを みますか。
    Asa terebi o mimasu ka.
    Do you watch TV in the morning?
  • Mika
    はい、ニュースを みます。
    Hai, nyuusu o mimasu.
    Yes, I watch the news.
  • Alex
    わたしは しんぶんを よみます。
    Watashi wa shinbun o yomimasu.
    I read the newspaper.

Vocabulary

  • たべますtabemasuto eat
  • のみますnomimasuto drink
  • みますmimasuto see, to watch
  • よみますyomimasuto read
  • パンpanbread
  • ごはんgohanrice, meal
  • おちゃochatea
  • コーヒーkoohiicoffee
  • しんぶんshinbunnewspaper

Remember

  • を is read “o”, and it only ever appears as a particle.
  • The verb comes last. Thing を verb, never verb を thing.
