に: Time, Goal and Target

Goal: By the end you can say when you do something and where you go.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

に marks a point: a point in time, a destination, or the person an action reaches.

It is one small word with three jobs, and they feel alike once you see them.

How it works, with examples

Time に

しちじに おきます。 means “I wake up at seven”. Put に after a clock time or a day of the week: げつようびに いきます。

Destination に

がっこうに いきます。 means “I go to school”. に marks the place you head for.

Target に

ともだちに あいます。 means “I meet a friend”. に marks the person you meet.

Not every time word takes に

Words like きょう, あした and まいにち do not take に. Use に for specific times: しちじに, にちようびに.

Story: Mika's Monday

  • Alex
    ミカさんは なんじに おきますか。
    Mika-san wa nanji ni okimasu ka.
    What time do you wake up, Mika?
  • Mika
    しちじに おきます。
    Shichiji ni okimasu.
    I wake up at seven.
  • Alex
    がっこうに なんじに いきますか。
    Gakkou ni nanji ni ikimasu ka.
    What time do you go to school?
  • Mika
    しちじはんに がっこうに いきます。
    Shichiji han ni gakkou ni ikimasu.
    I go to school at seven thirty.
  • Alex
    ごごは なにを しますか。
    Gogo wa nani o shimasu ka.
    What do you do in the afternoon?
  • Mika
    ごごに ともだちに あいます。
    Gogo ni tomodachi ni aimasu.
    In the afternoon I meet a friend.

Vocabulary

  • なんじnanjiwhat time
  • しちじshichijiseven o'clock
  • ～はん-hanhalf past
  • ごごgogoafternoon, p.m.
  • おきますokimasuto wake up, to get up
  • いきますikimasuto go
  • あいますaimasuto meet
  • がっこうgakkouschool

Remember

  • Use に with clock times and days of the week, but not with きょう or あした.
  • あいます needs に for the person you meet: ともだちに あいます.
