Lesson 7 of 10 · 10 min

で: Place of Action and Means

Goal: By the end you can say where you study and what you go by.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

で has two jobs: it marks the place where an action happens, and the means you use.

Compare に and で: に is where you go, で is where you do it.

How it works, with examples

Place で action

としょかんで べんきょうします。 means “I study at the library”. The library is where the studying happens.

Means で

バスで いきます。 means “I go by bus”. じてんしゃで いきます。 means “I go by bicycle”.

Language で

にほんごで はなします。 means “I speak in Japanese”. で marks the tool, here the language.

に vs で

がっこうに いきます is “I go to school” (a destination). がっこうで べんきょうします is “I study at school” (where the action happens).

Story: How do you get there?

  • Alex
    ミカさんは どこで べんきょうしますか。
    Mika-san wa doko de benkyou shimasu ka.
    Where do you study, Mika?
  • Mika
    としょかんで べんきょうします。
    Toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
    I study at the library.
  • Alex
    としょかんに バスで いきますか。
    Toshokan ni basu de ikimasu ka.
    Do you go to the library by bus?
  • Mika
    いいえ、じてんしゃで いきます。
    Iie, jitensha de ikimasu.
    No, I go by bicycle.
  • Alex
    ともだちと にほんごで はなしますか。
    Tomodachi to nihongo de hanashimasu ka.
    Do you talk with friends in Japanese?
  • Mika
    はい。えいごでも はなします。
    Hai. Eigo demo hanashimasu.
    Yes. I talk in English too.

Vocabulary

  • どこdokowhere
  • としょかんtoshokanlibrary
  • バスbasubus
  • じてんしゃjitenshabicycle
  • べんきょうしますbenkyou shimasuto study
  • はなしますhanashimasuto speak, to talk
  • えいごeigoEnglish

Remember

  • で marks where an action happens, and に marks where you go.
  • で also marks the means: バスで, じてんしゃで, にほんごで.
