へ: Direction

Goal: By the end you can say where you go, come or return to.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

へ marks the direction you are heading. It is similar to に for going places.

As a particle, へ is read “e”, not “he”.

How it works, with examples

Place へ go

きょうとへ いきます。 means “I go to Kyoto”. へ points toward the place.

Read “e”

You write へ but say “e”. きょうとへ is “Kyouto e”. The same character in へや (room) is still “he”.

へ and に

With いきます, きます and かえります you can use either: がっこうへ いきます and がっこうに いきます mean the same. へ is a little more about direction, に about the goal.

へ does not mark time

しちじへ おきます is wrong. For times, always use に.

Story: A trip to Kyoto

  • Mika
    あした、どこへ いきますか。
    Ashita, doko e ikimasu ka.
    Where are you going tomorrow?
  • Alex
    きょうとへ いきます。
    Kyouto e ikimasu.
    I'm going to Kyoto.
  • Mika
    なにで いきますか。
    Nani de ikimasu ka.
    How are you going?
  • Alex
    でんしゃで いきます。
    Densha de ikimasu.
    I'm going by train.
  • Mika
    いつ うちへ かえりますか。
    Itsu uchi e kaerimasu ka.
    When will you come home?
  • Alex
    あさって うちへ かえります。
    Asatte uchi e kaerimasu.
    I'll come home the day after tomorrow.

Vocabulary

  • きょうとKyoutoKyoto
  • あしたashitatomorrow
  • あさってasattethe day after tomorrow
  • でんしゃdenshatrain
  • かえりますkaerimasuto return, to go home
  • うちuchihome, house
  • いつitsuwhen

Remember

  • へ is read “e” as a particle.
  • For times use に, and for directions use へ or に.
