Lesson 9 of 10 · 10 min

と: And, With

Goal: By the end you can list a complete set of nouns and say who you went with.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

と has two meanings: “and” between nouns, and “with” a person.

Both are easy, but と only joins nouns.

How it works, with examples

Noun と noun

パンと たまごを かいます。 means “I buy bread and eggs”. と lists nouns, and the list is complete.

With someone

ともだちと いきます。 means “I go with a friend”. と marks the partner in the action.

Not for verbs

You cannot use と to join two verbs or two adjectives. Genki teaches other ways for those.

Alone: ひとりで

To say “alone”, use ひとりで: ひとりで いきます。 means “I go alone”. It does not use と.

Story: Weekend plans

  • Mika
    しゅうまつ、だれと えいがを みますか。
    Shuumatsu, dare to eiga o mimasu ka.
    Who are you watching a movie with this weekend?
  • Alex
    ともだちと みます。
    Tomodachi to mimasu.
    I'm watching with a friend.
  • Mika
    ケンさんと さくらさんと ですか。
    Ken-san to Sakura-san to desu ka.
    With Ken and Sakura?
  • Alex
    いいえ、ケンさんと ふたりで みます。
    Iie, Ken-san to futari de mimasu.
    No, just the two of us, Ken and me.
  • Mika
    えいがの あとで パンと コーヒーを かいますか。
    Eiga no ato de pan to koohii o kaimasu ka.
    Will you buy bread and coffee after the movie?
  • Alex
    はい、かいます。
    Hai, kaimasu.
    Yes, I will.

Vocabulary

  • しゅうまつshuumatsuweekend
  • えいがeigamovie
  • だれとdare towith whom
  • ふたりでfutari detwo people together
  • ひとりでhitori dealone
  • かいますkaimasuto buy

Remember

  • と joins nouns only. It does not join verbs.
  • だれと means “with whom?”, and the answer is ともだちと, かぞくと, and so on.
