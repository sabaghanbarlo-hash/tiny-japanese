が: The Subject Marker

Goal: By the end you can answer who does something and say what exists.

Watch the lesson

Turn your sound on: listen to each sound, then say it out loud yourself. Tap any character or speaker button on this page to hear it again. Voices: Mika and Alex (neural text-to-speech).

What it does

が marks the subject: who or what does the action, or what exists.

は and が can look alike. は says what we are talking about, and が points at the answer.

How it works, with examples

Question word が

だれが コーヒーを いれますか。 means “Who makes the coffee?”. When the subject is a question word, use が, not は.

Answer with が

アレックスさんが いれます。 means “Alex makes it”. The new, important information gets が.

Things that exist

ほんが あります。 means “There is a book”. が marks the thing that exists.

は vs が

アレックスさんは いれます。 sounds like “As for Alex, he makes it”. が puts the focus on Alex: it is Alex who does it.

Story: Who makes the coffee?

  • Mika
    だれが コーヒーを いれますか。
    Dare ga koohii o iremasu ka.
    Who makes the coffee?
  • Alex
    わたしが いれます。
    Watashi ga iremasu.
    I do.
  • Mika
    ありがとう。じゃあ、わたしが おちゃを いれます。
    Arigatou. Jaa, watashi ga ocha o iremasu.
    Thanks. Then I'll make the tea.
  • Alex
    テーブルの うえに ケーキが あります。
    Teeburu no ue ni keeki ga arimasu.
    There's a cake on the table.
  • Mika
    だれが かいましたか。
    Dare ga kaimashita ka.
    Who bought it?
  • Alex
    さくらさんが かいました。
    Sakura-san ga kaimashita.
    Sakura bought it.

Vocabulary

  • いれますiremasuto make (tea, coffee)
  • ケーキkeekicake
  • テーブルteeburutable
  • うえuetop, above
  • ありますarimasuthere is (things)
  • かいましたkaimashitabought

Remember

  • Use が when the subject is a question word: だれが, なにが.
  • は is the topic and が is the doer. Use が to highlight who does it.
