Genki I Particles

One short video for each Japanese particle: は, か, の, も, を, に, で, へ, と and が.

  • 10 lessons
  • about 4 hours
  • Textbook companion

A pilot for a one-concept-per-lesson way of learning Genki I grammar. Each lesson takes one particle, explains it from the start, shows examples, then puts it in a short story with Mika and Alex. An independent companion, not affiliated with the Genki publisher.

Free while we launch

Everything in this course is open to read right now, no account needed.

Start lesson 1

What you'll learn

  • Know what each basic particle does and how it is read
  • Choose between は and が, に and で, に and へ
  • Build simple sentences about who, what, where and when

Who it's for: Beginners who can read hiragana and want each grammar point explained on its own.

Lessons

  1. 1は: The Topic MarkerLearn what は marks, why it is read “wa”, and how to talk about someone or something.
  2. 2か: The Question ParticleTurn a sentence into a yes/no question, and ask with question words.
  3. 3の: Linking NounsSay “my teacher”, “a Japanese teacher” and “the school's festival” with の.
  4. 4も: Also, TooUse も to say “too” and “also”.
  5. 5を: The Object MarkerMark what the action is done to: eat bread, read a book, watch TV.
  6. 6に: Time, Goal and TargetUse に for clock times, destinations and the person you meet.
  7. 7で: Place of Action and MeansSay where you do things and how you get there.
  8. 8へ: DirectionSay where you are heading with へ, read “e”.
  9. 9と: And, WithJoin nouns with と and say who you do things with.
  10. 10が: The Subject MarkerSee what が marks, and when it replaces は.
