Genki I Companion

Follow the 12 chapters of Genki I with Mika and Alex, one grammar topic at a time.

  • 12 lessons
  • about 8 hours
  • Textbook companion

An independent study companion for the Genki I textbook, not affiliated with its publisher. Each lesson follows the grammar topics of one Genki chapter, but the dialogues, examples and quizzes are all original: a short story with Mika and Alex, vocabulary, clear grammar notes and a quiz. Use it next to your own copy of the book, or on its own.

What you'll learn

  • Introduce yourself, point at things and ask whose they are
  • Use verbs, adjectives and particles, the て form and casual speech
  • Compare things, talk about plans and experiences
  • Give reasons and advice, and say what you must do

Who it's for: Beginners who can read hiragana (take Hiragana Starter first if not) and are studying Genki I or want to follow the same path.

Lessons

  1. 1は, です, か and の: Your First SentencesBuild your first sentences with the topic marker は, the polite です, the question marker か and the linking particle の.
  2. 2Pointing Words, も, じゃ ありません, ね and よPoint at things and places with これ, この and ここ, ask whose they are with だれの, say “also” with も, say “is not” and add ね and よ.
  3. 3Verbs and Particles: を, で, に and へLearn the three kinds of verbs, make the polite ます form, and use the particles を, で, に and へ, invitations and frequency words.
  4. 4ある and いる, Past Tense and とSay what exists and where things are, talk about the past with でした and ました, and use にも, でも, ～時間, たくさん and と.
  5. 5Adjectives, Past Tense and CountersDescribe things with い- and な-adjectives, in the present and the past, say what you like, suggest with ましょう and count things.
  6. 6て Form: Requests, Permission and ReasonsMake the て form of every verb, then use it for requests, chains of actions, permission, prohibition and reasons with から.
  7. 7ている, Describing People and Counting PeopleUse ている for actions in progress and states, describe people with は～が, join descriptions with the て form, say “go to do” with に いく and count people.
  8. 8Casual Form, と思います and がSpeak casually with friends in the plain form, say what you think and what others said, make negative requests, and use が for new information.
  9. 9Casual Past, Verbs as Adjectives and もう／まだTalk about the past in casual speech, use verbs to describe nouns, and say “already” and “not yet” with もう and まだ.
  10. 10Comparisons, つもり and になるCompare things with より and の ほうが, choose the best with の なかで, make plans with つもり, talk about change with になる and use どこか, だれか and なにか.
  11. 11たい, たり, たことがある and やSay what you want to do with ～たい, list example actions with ～たり ～たりする, talk about experiences with ～たことがある and list nouns with や.
  12. 12んです, すぎる, ほうがいい, ので and なければいけませんExplain and ask for explanations with んです, say “too much” with すぎる, give advice with ほうがいい, give reasons with ので, say what you must do and ask politely with でしょうか.
