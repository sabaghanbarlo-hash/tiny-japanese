What you'll learn

  • Make and understand simple “A is B” sentences with は and です
  • Use the particles の, も, と, を, に, で and へ correctly
  • Say what you do and did with polite ます and ました verbs
  • Describe things with い- and な-adjectives, in the present and the past
  • Say what you want and ask for things politely

Who it's for: Beginners who can read hiragana (take Hiragana Starter first if not).

Lessons

  1. 1です and は: A is BSay who and what things are with は and です, ask questions with か and say no with じゃ ありません.
  2. 2This, That and WherePoint at things and places with これ, それ, あれ, ここ, そこ, あそこ and ask what or where something is.
  3. 3の, も and とShow whose something is with の, say “also” with も and join nouns with と.
  4. 4Verbs with ますPremiumUse polite verbs like たべます and のみます, mark the object with を and say no with ません.
  5. 5に, で and へ: Time, Place and DirectionPremiumSay when you do things, where you do them and where you go with に, で and へ.
  6. 6い-AdjectivesPremiumDescribe things with おいしい, たかい, おおきい and say they are not so with くない.
  7. 7な-AdjectivesPremiumUse しずか, にぎやか, すき and other な-adjectives, and say what you like with が.
  8. 8The Past TensePremiumTalk about yesterday with ました, ませんでした, でした and かったです.
  9. 9Wanting and AskingPremiumSay what you want with ほしい and たい, and ask for things with ください.
