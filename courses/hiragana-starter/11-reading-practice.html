You can read hiragana

You now know the whole system. This lesson is for practice: words you'll see in real life, and simple sentences. Read each aloud first, then check the romaji and meaning.

Places and everyday words

  • とうきょうtoukyouTokyo
  • きょうとkyoutoKyoto
  • おおさかoosakaOsaka
  • ふじさんfujisanMt. Fuji
  • にほんごnihongothe Japanese language
  • おいしいoishiidelicious
  • すみませんsumimasenexcuse me, sorry
  • おはようございますohayou gozaimasugood morning (polite)
  • こんにちはkonnichiwahello
  • ごちそうさまでしたgochisousama deshitathank you for the meal

Sentences to read aloud

  • Sentence 1
    これは ほんです。
    Kore wa hon desu.
    This is a book.
  • Sentence 2
    きょうは あついです。
    Kyou wa atsui desu.
    It's hot today.
  • Sentence 3
    ねこが すきです。
    Neko ga suki desu.
    I like cats.
  • Sentence 4
    すしを たべます。
    Sushi o tabemasu.
    I eat sushi.
  • Sentence 5
    えきは どこですか。
    Eki wa doko desu ka.
    Where is the station?
  • Sentence 6
    ひらがなが よめます！
    Hiragana ga yomemasu!
    I can read hiragana!

Where to go next

Katakana is the second script, used for foreign words, names and many food and drink names. It follows exactly the same sound chart you have just learned, only with different shapes, so the hard part is already done.

To use your new reading skills straight away, start the First Conversations course. Its dialogues are written in hiragana and katakana, with romaji and English alongside.
