Hiragana Starter

Read every hiragana character in 11 short lessons.

  • 11 lessons
  • about 3.5 hours
  • Reading & writing

A step-by-step path through all 46 basic hiragana, plus the sound marks, combined sounds, small っ and long vowels. Every lesson has a chart, real words to read, tips and a quiz.

Free while we launch

Everything in this course is open to read right now, no account needed. Want to hear about new courses and extras? Email us.

Start lesson 1 Download the workbook (PDF)

What you'll learn

  • Read and say all 46 basic hiragana
  • Use dakuten and handakuten (two tiny marks) to read 25 more sounds
  • Read combined sounds (きゃ, しゅ, ちょ), small っ and long vowels
  • Read real words, place names and full sentences in hiragana

Who it's for: Complete beginners. No Japanese needed.

Lessons

  1. 1Meet Hiragana: The VowelsHow Japanese writing works, and your first five characters: あ い う え お.
  2. 2The K Row: か き く け こAdd five consonant-vowel characters and read your first words about places and colours.
  3. 3The S Row: さ し す せ そFive more characters, and your first food words: すし and さけ.
  4. 4The T Row: た ち つ て とMore irregular sounds (chi and tsu) and your first useful time words.
  5. 5The N Row: な に ぬ ね のAnimals, questions and one very important particle.
  6. 6The H Row: は ひ ふ へ ほThe H row, the sound “fu”, and the characters that change sound when they are particles.
  7. 7The M and Y RowsEight more characters: ま み む め も and や ゆ よ.
  8. 8The R Row, わ, を and んThe last of the basic 46 characters, including the particle を and the standalone ん.
  9. 9Dakuten and Handakuten: が ざ だ ば ぱTwo tiny marks that give you 25 more sounds.
  10. 10Combined Sounds, Small っ and Long VowelsThe final pronunciation rules that let you read nearly any hiragana word.
  11. 11Reading Practice and ReviewPut it all together: read real words, place names and complete sentences.
