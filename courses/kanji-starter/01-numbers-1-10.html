Numbers 一 to 十

Goal: By the end you can read 一 to 十 and words like 三人, 五月 and 九時.

Warm-up

Numbers are the friendliest first kanji: 一, 二 and 三 are just one, two and three lines. Each kanji has an “on” reading (from Chinese) used mostly in compounds and a “kun” reading (native Japanese) used for single words. For numbers, the on reading does most of the work.

The ten number kanji

  • 一ichi
  • 二ni
  • 三san
  • 四yon / shi
  • 五go
  • 六roku
  • 七nana / shichi
  • 八hachi
  • 九kyuu / ku
  • 十juu

Words to read

  • 一人 (ひとり)hitorione person
  • 二人 (ふたり)futaritwo people
  • 三人 (さんにん)sanninthree people
  • 五月 (ごがつ)gogatsuMay
  • 十月 (じゅうがつ)juugatsuOctober
  • 四時 (よじ)yoji4 o'clock
  • 七時 (しちじ)shichiji7 o'clock
  • 九時 (くじ)kuji9 o'clock
  • 二十 (にじゅう)nijuutwenty
  • 八つ (やっつ)yattsueight (things)

Dialogue: How many people?

  • Mika
    なん人 ですか。
    Nannin desu ka.
    How many people?
  • Alex
    三人 です。ミカさんと わたしと ともだち です。
    Sannin desu. Mika-san to watashi to tomodachi desu.
    Three. You, me and a friend.
  • Mika
    なん時に あいますか。
    Nanji ni aimasu ka.
    What time shall we meet?
  • Alex
    七時に あいます。
    Shichiji ni aimasu.
    We'll meet at seven.

Grammar and usage

Two readings for 4, 7 and 9

四 is よん or し, 七 is なな or しち, 九 is きゅう or く. Counting uses よん, なな and きゅう. But 4 o'clock, 7 o'clock and 9 o'clock use the older readings: よじ, しちじ, くじ.

Numbers combine by position

十 is ten, so 二十 is two tens (20), 三十 is thirty and 十一 is eleven (ten + one). The kanji add up like arithmetic: 十二 = 12.

People and months

Add 人 for people: 一人 and 二人 are special (ひとり, ふたり), then 三人 (さんにん). Add 月 for months: 五月 is the fifth month, May.

How to remember

  • 一, 二, 三 are one, two and three strokes. 四 looks like a box with legs. 十 is a simple cross, like a plus sign. Practise writing each kanji in the right stroke order: top to bottom, left to right.
