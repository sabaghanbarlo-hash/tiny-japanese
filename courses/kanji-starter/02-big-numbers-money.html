Hundreds, Thousands and Yen

Goal: By the end you can read prices like 三百円 and 一万円, and years like 二千二十六年.

Warm-up

Prices appear everywhere in Japan, and they are written with kanji and numbers. These six kanji unlock price tags, menus and dates.

The six kanji

Words to read

  • 百円 (ひゃくえん)hyaku en100 yen
  • 三百円 (さんびゃくえん)sanbyaku en300 yen
  • 千円 (せんえん)sen en1,000 yen
  • 五千円 (ごせんえん)gosen en5,000 yen
  • 一万円 (いちまんえん)ichiman en10,000 yen
  • 今年 (ことし)kotoshithis year
  • 来年 (らいねん)rainennext year
  • 何年 (なんねん)nannenwhat year? how many years?
  • 二千二十六年 (にせんにじゅうろくねん)nisen nijuuroku nenthe year 2026

Dialogue: How much is it?

  • Alex
    すみません、これは いくら ですか。
    Sumimasen, kore wa ikura desu ka.
    Excuse me, how much is this?
  • Mika
    三百円 です。
    Sanbyaku en desu.
    It's 300 yen.
  • Alex
    あれは いくら ですか。
    Are wa ikura desu ka.
    How much is that one?
  • Mika
    千二百円 です。
    Sen nihyaku en desu.
    It's 1,200 yen.

Grammar and usage

Sound changes in 百 and 千

百 is ひゃく, but 300 is さんびゃく, 600 is ろっぴゃく and 800 is はっぴゃく. 千 is せん, but 3,000 is さんぜん and 8,000 is はっせん. These changes make the words easier to say.

一 before 万

10,000 is 一万 (いちまん). Unlike 百 and 千, you must say いち before 万. One yen at the till is 一円 (いちえん).

円 and 年

円 (えん) is the yen. 年 (ねん) means “year” and also counts years: 今年 (this year), 来年 (next year), 二千二十六年 (2026).

何 is the question kanji

何 asks “what” or “how many”: 何年 (what year), 何人 (how many people), 何円 (how many yen). It is read なん before most counters and なに alone.

How to remember

  • 円 looks like a coin in a box, which matches its old meaning of “round”. 千 is like 十 with a slanted top, and 百 is white (白) with a line on top.
