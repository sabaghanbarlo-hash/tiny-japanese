What you'll learn

  • Read the kanji for numbers, prices, years and the days of the week
  • Read people, school and nature words such as 先生, 学生, 山 and 天気
  • Read signs like 出口, 入口, 右 and 左 and polite verbs like 食べます and 行きます
  • Read clock times such as 午後三時十五分

Who it's for: Learners who can read hiragana and katakana (take Hiragana Starter first if not).

Lessons

  1. 1Numbers 一 to 十Read and recognise the kanji for one to ten and use them in people, months and clock times.
  2. 2Hundreds, Thousands and YenRead 百, 千, 万, 円, 年 and 何 and use them for prices and years.
  3. 3Days of the WeekRead the seven day-of-the-week kanji and the word 曜日.
  4. 4People and SchoolPremiumRead 人, 男, 女, 子, 先, 生, 学 and 友 in words like 先生, 学生 and 友だち.
  5. 5Nature and WeatherPremiumRead 山, 川, 田, 雨, 空, 花, 天 and 気 and talk about the weather.
  6. 6Size and PositionPremiumRead 上, 下, 中, 大, 小, 左, 右 and 外 and use them for size and directions.
  7. 7Everyday ActionsPremiumRead the kanji for see, eat, drink, go, come, exit, enter and rest in verbs and signs.
  8. 8Time, Places and ThingsPremiumRead 時, 分, 午, 前, 後, 本, 車 and 駅 for clock times, books, cars and stations.
