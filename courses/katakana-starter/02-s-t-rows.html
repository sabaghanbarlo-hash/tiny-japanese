The S and T Rows

Goal: Read サ シ ス セ ソ and タ チ ツ テ ト and read words like タクシー and スカート.

The S row

The T row

The famous look-alikes: シ and ツ, ソ and ン

シ vs ツ

Both are two short strokes and a long curve. In シ (shi) the two short strokes lie almost flat, side by side, and the long stroke sweeps upward. In ツ (tsu) the short strokes stand more upright, and the long stroke sweeps downward. Check the direction of the long stroke first.

ソ vs ン

You will meet ン (n) in lesson 5. Learn ソ (so) now: both of its strokes are written downward. In ン, the long stroke is written upward from the lower left.

Do not worry about perfect memory

Almost every learner confuses these for a while. Context helps: if you read シ in a word and it makes no sense, try ツ.

Words you can read now

  • スキーsukiiskiing
  • タクシーtakushiitaxi
  • テストtesutotest, exam
  • セーターseetaasweater
  • スーツsuutsusuit
  • アイスaisuice, ice cream
  • サイトsaitowebsite
  • ソースsoosusauce
  • スカートsukaatoskirt
  • キスkisukiss

How to practise

  • Say each sound out loud as you read it. You already know the sounds from hiragana, so this is only about new shapes.
  • Write each new character about ten times, following a reliable stroke-order chart. Writing helps you notice the small differences between look-alike characters.
  • Read the words aloud and guess what they are before checking the meaning. Most katakana words come from English, so you will often get them right.
