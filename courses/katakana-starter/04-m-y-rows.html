The M and Y Rows

Goal: Read マ ミ ム メ モ and ヤ ユ ヨ and read words like マスク and ホーム.

The M row

  • マma
  • ミmi
  • ムmu
  • メme
  • モmo

The Y row

  • ヤya
  • ユyu
  • ヨyo

Notes

The Y row has three characters

There is no “yi” or “ye” in standard Japanese, so the row has only ヤ (ya), ユ (yu) and ヨ (yo). The empty cells in the chart are on purpose.

ミ is three strokes

ミ (mi) is three short slanted strokes stacked one above the other.

ム and メ

ム (mu) is a corner with a small mark. メ (me) is a simple cross, a bit like a multiplication sign. Not to be confused with ス (su) and ナ (na).

Words you can read now

  • マスクmasukumask
  • マイクmaikumicrophone
  • メモmemomemo, note
  • ミスmisumistake
  • ムースmuusumousse
  • ユーモアyuumoahumour
  • ヨーヨーyooyooyo-yo
  • ハムhamuham
  • ホームhoomuhome, station platform
  • モーターmootaamotor

How to practise

  • Say each sound out loud as you read it. You already know the sounds from hiragana, so this is only about new shapes.
  • Write each new character about ten times, following a reliable stroke-order chart. Writing helps you notice the small differences between look-alike characters.
  • Read the words aloud and guess what they are before checking the meaning. Most katakana words come from English, so you will often get them right.
