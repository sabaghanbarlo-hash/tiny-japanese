Katakana Starter

Read every katakana character in 11 short lessons.

  • 11 lessons
  • about 4 hours
  • Reading & writing

The second Japanese script, built on the sounds you already know from hiragana. Learn all 46 basic characters, the sound marks, combined sounds, small ッ and the extra sounds for foreign words, then practise on menus, countries, names and real sentences.

Free while we launch

Everything in this course is open to read right now, no account needed. Want to hear about new courses and extras? Email us.

Start lesson 1

What you'll learn

  • Read and say all 46 basic katakana
  • Read voiced sounds, combined sounds, small ッ and the long vowel mark ー
  • Read the extra sounds used for foreign words (ティ, ファ, チェ)
  • Read menus, country names, places and your own name in katakana

Who it's for: Learners who can read hiragana (take Hiragana Starter first if not).

Lessons

  1. 1Meet Katakana: Vowels and the K RowWhat katakana is for, your first ten characters, and the long vowel mark ー.
  2. 2The S and T RowsTen more characters, including two that are easy to mix up: シ and ツ.
  3. 3The N and H RowsTen more characters, with a coffee-shop word list.
  4. 4The M and Y RowsEight characters, since the Y row only has three.
  5. 5The R Row, ワ, ヲ and ンThe last of the 46 basic characters, plus the words that restaurants and hotels use.
  6. 6Dakuten and Handakuten: ガ ザ ダ バ パTwo small marks give you 25 more sounds, exactly as in hiragana.
  7. 7Combined Sounds: キャ シュ チョSmall ャ ュ ョ blend into the character before them, as in hiragana.
  8. 8Small ッ and Sounds for Foreign WordsThe double-consonant pause, and extra combinations like ティ and ファ that katakana has for borrowed words.
  9. 9Katakana in the Wild: Food and DrinkRead menus and shop signs, where katakana appears most.
  10. 10Countries, Places and NamesRead country names, everyday places and your own name.
  11. 11Reading Practice and ReviewRead real sentences that mix hiragana and katakana.
