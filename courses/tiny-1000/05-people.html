People

Goal: By the end you can recognize, say and use these 10 words: 友達, 先生, 学生, 家族, 母, 父, 男, 女, 子供, 名前.

Warm-up

This lesson teaches 10 of the most useful words in Japanese, grouped by theme: people. Listen to Mika and Alex use them, study each word with its example sentence, then try the quiz. Say every word and sentence out loud.

Grammar and usage

Topic は and polite です

Use は to mark the topic and です to make a polite statement. Add か at the end to make a polite yes-or-no question. わたしの 名前は アレックスです。 (My name is Alex.) あの 男の人は 先生ですか。 (Is that man a teacher?)
