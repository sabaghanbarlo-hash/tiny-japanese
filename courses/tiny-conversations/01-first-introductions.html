First Introductions

Goal: By the end you can follow and use this everyday conversation: two new classmates introduce themselves in a classroom.

Useful patterns

Introducing yourself

Say 私は plus your name plus です to introduce yourself. Example: 私は Mika です。

Asking a yes-or-no question

Add ですか to make a polite question. Example: 学生ですか。

Real-life tip

  • 初めまして is commonly used when meeting someone for the first time, and よろしくお願いします is a polite way to close the introduction.
