Names and Countries

Goal: By the end you can follow and use this everyday conversation: two guests share their names and countries at a party.

Useful patterns

Asking where someone is from

Use 「どこの 国から 来ましたか」 to ask what country someone came from. Example: 「Alexは どこの 国から 来ましたか。」

Saying where you are from

Use 「〜から 来ました」 to say where you came from. Example: 「私は 日本から 来ました。」

Real-life tip

  • In everyday Japanese, people often omit pronouns. Using someone’s name, as Mika does here, can make a question clear and friendly.
