A New Neighbor

Goal: By the end you can follow and use this everyday conversation: two neighbors meet in their apartment hallway.

Useful patterns

Ask whether something happened

Add か to a polite past-tense question. Example: 「最近 引っ越しましたか。」 means “Did you move recently?”

Say where you live

Use a place or relationship word with に住んでいます to say where you live. Example: 「隣に 住んでいます。」 means “I live next door.”

Give simple directions

Use ここから for “from here,” then a direction. Example: 「ここから まっすぐ 行くと 店が あります。」 means “Go straight from here and there’s a store.”

Real-life tip

  • When meeting a neighbor in Japan, a brief greeting and よろしくお願いします are friendly ways to start getting acquainted.
