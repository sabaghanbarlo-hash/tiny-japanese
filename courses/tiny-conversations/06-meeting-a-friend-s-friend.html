Lesson 6 of 100 · 10 min

Meeting a Friend's Friend

Goal: By the end you can follow and use this everyday conversation: two people introduce themselves at a café with a mutual friend.

The situation

Two people introduce themselves at a café with a mutual friend. Read the dialogue once for the meaning, then read it aloud. Cover the English and try to understand each line from the Japanese. Every word comes from the Tiny 1000 list.

Conversation

  • Mika
    こんにちは。 私は Mika です。
    Konnichiwa. Watashi wa Mika desu.
    Hello. I'm Mika.
  • Alex
    初めまして。 Alex です。 よろしくお願いします。
    Hajimemashite. Alex desu. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu.
    Nice to meet you. I'm Alex. Pleased to meet you.
  • Mika
    友達から 聞きました。
    Tomodachi kara kikimashita.
    I heard about you from our friend.
  • Alex
    私も 友達から 聞きました。
    Watashi mo tomodachi kara kikimashita.
    I heard about you from our friend too.
  • Mika
    この 店の コーヒーは おいしいですか。
    Kono mise no koohii wa oishii desu ka.
    Is the coffee at this shop good?
  • Alex
    はい、とても おいしいです。 お茶も あります。
    Hai, totemo oishii desu. Ocha mo arimasu.
    Yes, it's very good. They have tea too.
  • Mika
    じゃあ、何か 飲みませんか。
    Jaa, nanika nomimasen ka.
    Well then, would you like something to drink?
  • Alex
    いいですね。 私は お茶を 飲みます。
    Ii desu ne. Watashi wa ocha o nomimasu.
    Sounds good. I'll have tea.
  • Mika
    お茶が 好きですか。
    Ocha ga suki desu ka.
    Do you like tea?
  • Alex
    はい、好きです。 コーヒーも 好きです。
    Hai, suki desu. Koohii mo suki desu.
    Yes, I do. I like coffee too.
  • Mika
    そうですか。 一緒に 飲みましょう。
    Sou desu ka. Issho ni nomimashou.
    I see. Let's have a drink together.
  • Alex
    はい。 ありがとう。
    Hai. Arigatou.
    Sure. Thanks.

Useful patterns

Asking about something

Add ですか to make a polite question. Example: お茶が 好きですか。 means “Do you like tea?”

Inviting someone

A verb in the ませんか form makes a polite invitation. Example: コーヒーを 飲みませんか。 means “Would you like to have coffee?”

Suggesting an activity together

Use 一緒に with a verb in the ましょう form to suggest doing something together. Example: 一緒に 飲みましょう。 means “Let’s have a drink together.”

Real-life tip

  • 初めまして is a common greeting when meeting someone for the first time. よろしくお願いします is a polite way to close an introduction.
