Useful patterns

Asking what someone does

仕事は 何 ですか asks what someone’s job is. The topic marker は follows 仕事.

Saying where you work

Use a place plus で and a verb to describe an action there: 会社で 働いています means “I work at a company.”

Asking if something is busy

Add ですか to make a polite yes-or-no question: 先生の 仕事は 忙しい ですか means “Is a teacher’s job busy?”

Real-life tip

  • 仕事は 何 ですか is a direct, simple way to ask about someone’s job. In a more formal setting, use a more polite expression.
