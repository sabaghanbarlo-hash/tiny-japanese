See You Again

Goal: By the end you can follow and use this everyday conversation: two acquaintances say goodbye after meeting at a station.

The situation

Two acquaintances say goodbye after meeting at a station. Read the dialogue once for the meaning, then read it aloud. Cover the English and try to understand each line from the Japanese. Every word comes from the Tiny 1000 list.

Useful patterns

Asking where someone is going

Use どこへ 行きますか to ask where someone is going. Example: どこへ 行きますか。

Suggesting a future meeting

Use a time expression with 会いませんか to invite someone to meet. Example: 来週、駅で 会いませんか。

Real-life tip

  • 駅で means “at the station”; で marks the place where an action happens.
