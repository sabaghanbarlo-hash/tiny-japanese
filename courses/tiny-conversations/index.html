Tiny Conversations

100 everyday Japanese conversations, built only from the 1,000 words you already know.

  • 100 lessons
  • about 16.5 hours
  • Conversation

The second half of the Tiny 1000 method. After you learn the 1,000 most useful words, put them to work in 100 short everyday conversations with Mika and Alex: meeting people, getting around town, eating out, shopping, travel, home, work, health, free time and small talk. Every conversation uses only words from Tiny 1000, with useful patterns, a real-life tip and a quick comprehension quiz.

What you'll learn

  • Follow and take part in 100 everyday situations in Japanese
  • Reuse the same 1,000 words in new combinations until they feel natural
  • Learn the sentence patterns Japanese speakers actually use
  • Get comfortable reading and saying full conversations out loud

Who it's for: Learners who have finished Tiny 1000, or know most of its 1,000 words.
