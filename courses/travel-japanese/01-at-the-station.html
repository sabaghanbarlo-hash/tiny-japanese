At the Station

Goal: By the end you can buy a ticket, ask for a platform number and ask when a train leaves.

Watch the lesson

Watch the story, learn the words and grammar, then try the quiz.

Warm-up

Trains are the best way to travel in Japan, and station staff are used to helpful questions from visitors. Three questions solve most situations: how much, which platform and what time. Mika plays the station staff member.

Dialogue: Buying a ticket

  • Alex
    すみません、とうきょうまで いくら ですか。
    Sumimasen, Toukyou made ikura desu ka.
    Excuse me, how much is it to Tokyo?
  • Mika
    せん にひゃく えん です。
    Sen nihyaku en desu.
    It's 1,200 yen.
  • Alex
    なんばんせん ですか。
    Nanbansen desu ka.
    Which platform is it?
  • Mika
    さんばんせん です。
    Sanbansen desu.
    Platform 3.
  • Alex
    なんじに でますか。
    Nanji ni demasu ka.
    What time does it leave?
  • Mika
    じゅうじ じゅうごふんに でます。
    Juuji juugofun ni demasu.
    It leaves at 10:15.
  • Alex
    ありがとう ございます。
    Arigatou gozaimasu.
    Thank you very much.

Vocabulary

  • きっぷkipputicket
  • 〜まで-madeto, as far as, until
  • いくらikurahow much
  • 〜ばんせん-bansenplatform number ~
  • でますdemasuleave, depart
  • つきますtsukimasuarrive
  • かたみちkatamichione-way
  • おうふくoufukuround trip
  • しんかんせんshinkansenbullet train

Grammar and usage

〜まで: as far as

Put the destination before まで: とうきょうまで (to Tokyo). Add いくら ですか for the price: とうきょうまで いくら ですか。 Ask the time with なんじに でますか。

Platform numbers

Add ばんせん after a number: いちばんせん (1), にばんせん (2), さんばんせん (3). Ask with なんばんせん ですか。

IC cards save time

Most visitors use a rechargeable IC card (like Suica or Pasmo) instead of buying paper tickets for local trains. You tap it at the gate and at the exit. For long trips such as the shinkansen you still buy a きっぷ.

Tip

  • Train times in Japan are very precise. If the timetable says 10:15, the train will leave at 10:15, so arrive a few minutes early.
