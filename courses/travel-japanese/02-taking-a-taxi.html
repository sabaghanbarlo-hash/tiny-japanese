Taking a Taxi

Goal: By the end you can ask a taxi to take you somewhere and ask how long it will take.

Warm-up

Taxis in Japan are clean, safe and very polite. The back door opens and closes automatically, so do not touch it. Tipping is not expected. Alex shows the driver an address on his phone and Mika plays the driver.

Dialogue: To the hotel

  • Alex
    すみません、この じゅうしょまで おねがいします。
    Sumimasen, kono juusho made onegaishimasu.
    Excuse me, to this address please.
  • Mika
    はい、わかりました。
    Hai, wakarimashita.
    Yes, understood.
  • Alex
    どのくらい かかりますか。
    Dono kurai kakarimasu ka.
    About how long will it take?
  • Mika
    じゅうごふん ぐらい です。
    Juugofun gurai desu.
    About fifteen minutes.
  • Alex
    いくら ぐらい ですか。
    Ikura gurai desu ka.
    About how much is it?
  • Mika
    せん ごひゃく えん ぐらい です。
    Sen gohyaku en gurai desu.
    About 1,500 yen.
  • Alex
    ここで とめて ください。
    Koko de tomete kudasai.
    Please stop here.

Vocabulary

  • タクシーtakushiitaxi
  • じゅうしょjuushoaddress
  • おねがいしますonegaishimasuplease (a request)
  • わかりましたwakarimashitaI understand, understood
  • どのくらいdono kuraiabout how long / how much
  • かかりますkakarimasutake (time or money)
  • 〜ぐらい-guraiabout, approximately
  • ここで とめて くださいkoko de tomete kudasaiplease stop here
  • まっすぐmassugustraight ahead

Grammar and usage

Place + まで + おねがいします

The simplest taxi sentence is destination + まで おねがいします: えきまで おねがいします (To the station, please). Show an address on your phone to be safe.

かかります and ぐらい

かかります means that time or money is needed: どのくらい かかりますか (How long does it take?). Add ぐらい after a number for “about”: じゅうごふん ぐらい (about fifteen minutes).

Set phrases with ください

ここで とめて ください (please stop here) and まっすぐ いって ください (please go straight) are useful set phrases. Learn them whole for now; the て-form comes in a later course.

Culture note

  • Wait for the driver to open the door. Do not slam it. Paying by IC card or card is common, but ask first: カードで はらえますか。 (Can I pay by card?).
