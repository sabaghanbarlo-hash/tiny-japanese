What you'll learn

  • Buy tickets, ask about platforms and take a taxi
  • Check in to a hotel and ask about breakfast and Wi-Fi
  • Handle a convenience store checkout and order in a restaurant
  • Shop tax-free, ask for sizes and ask for directions
  • Ask for help and describe simple health problems

Who it's for: Beginners who can read hiragana and have finished First Conversations (or know the basics).

Lessons

  1. 1At the StationAsk the price, the platform and the departure time, and understand the answers.
  2. 2Taking a TaxiGive an address, ask how long it takes and ask the driver to stop.
  3. 3Hotel Check-InCheck in, ask about breakfast times and get the Wi-Fi password and your room number.
  4. 4At the Convenience StorePremiumAnswer the cashier's usual questions and pay for your snacks, drinks and lunch.
  5. 5At the RestaurantPremiumGet a table, ask for the menu and recommendations, order, and ask for the bill.
  6. 6Shopping and Tax-FreePremiumAsk for sizes, try things on and ask whether something is tax-free.
  7. 7When You're LostPremiumAsk for directions, understand left, right and straight, and ask someone to repeat.
  8. 8Emergencies and HealthPremiumAsk for help, say where it hurts, mention allergies and know the emergency numbers.
