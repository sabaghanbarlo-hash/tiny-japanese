How it works

Every lesson follows the same three small steps. No account needed.

  1. 1

    Read a short conversation

    Follow a short Japanese dialogue with romaji and an English translation.

  2. 2

    Learn the words and grammar

    Go through the vocabulary and simple explanations, written for complete beginners.

  3. 3

    Try a quick quiz

    Check what you learned and get instant feedback after every answer.
