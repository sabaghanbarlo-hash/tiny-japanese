Lesson 12 · Asking for directions

Finding Your Way

Goal: Ask where a place is and understand basic directions.

Alex asks Mika how to find the station.

Watch this lesson

Watch Mika and Alex act out the dialogue, then practise the phrases below.

Dialogue

  • Alex
    すみません。えきはどこですか？
    Sumimasen. Eki wa doko desu ka?
    Excuse me. Where is the station?
  • Mika
    まっすぐいってください。
    Massugu itte kudasai.
    Please go straight.
  • Mika
    みぎです。
    Migi desu.
    It is on the right.
  • Alex
    ありがとうございます！
    Arigatou gozaimasu!
    Thank you very much!

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • えき (えき)ekistation
  • どこ (どこ)dokowhere
  • まっすぐ (まっすぐ)massugustraight ahead
  • みぎ (みぎ)migiright

Grammar

Place + はどこですか

Use place + はどこですか to ask where a place is. Verb + てください is a polite request or instruction.

Notes

  • ひだり (hidari) means left.
