Lesson 8 · Classroom objects

At School

Goal: Ask what something is and name simple objects.

Alex and Mika practise classroom vocabulary.

Watch this lesson

Watch Mika and Alex act out the dialogue, then practise the phrases below.

Dialogue

  • Alex
    これはなんですか？
    Kore wa nan desu ka?
    What is this?
  • Mika
    ほんです。
    Hon desu.
    It is a book.
  • Alex
    これはえんぴつですか？
    Kore wa enpitsu desu ka?
    Is this a pencil?
  • Mika
    はい、そうです。
    Hai, sou desu.
    Yes, that’s right.

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • これ (これ)korethis
  • なん (なん)nanwhat
  • ほん (ほん)honbook
  • えんぴつ (えんぴつ)enpitsupencil

Grammar

Questions with か

これはなんですか means “What is this?” Add か to make a polite yes/no question.

Notes

  • Try naming objects around you in Japanese.
