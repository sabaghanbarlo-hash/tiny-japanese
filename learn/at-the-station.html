Lesson 3 · Asking where something is

At the Station

Goal: By the end you can say “Good morning”, ask “Where is the station?”, and understand “It's over there.”

Alex is looking for the station and asks Mika.

Dialogue

  • Mika
    おはようございます！
    Ohayou gozaimasu!
    Good morning!
  • Alex
    おはようございます。えきは どこですか？
    Ohayou gozaimasu. Eki wa doko desu ka?
    Good morning. Where is the station?
  • Mika
    あそこです。
    Asoko desu.
    It's over there.
  • Alex
    ありがとうございます！
    Arigatou gozaimasu!
    Thank you!

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • おはようございますohayou gozaimasugood morning (polite)
  • 駅 (えき)ekistation
  • どこdokowhere
  • あそこasokoover there
  • ありがとうございますarigatou gozaimasuthank you (polite)

Grammar

X は どこですか？ — “Where is X?”

Put the thing you're looking for before は, then add どこですか. えきは どこですか？ = “Where is the station?”

Answer with the place + です

あそこです = “It's over there.” The answer simply swaps どこ for a place word.

ここ / そこ / あそこ

These three place words work as a set: ここ is near the speaker, そこ is near the listener, and あそこ is away from both of you.

Notes

  • おはようございます is a polite morning greeting. The shorter おはよう is the casual version you'd use with friends.
  • In the published video, えき is written in hiragana. The kanji 駅 is shown in the vocabulary list.

Extra phrases (not in the video)

Optional. These lines are not part of the video dialogue.

  • まっすぐ いって、みぎです。
    Massugu itte, migi desu.
    Go straight, then it's on the right.
  • ひだりです。
    Hidari desu.
    It's on the left.
