Episode 17 · Emergency phrases · Food allergies

I'm Allergic To…

Goal: By the end you can state an egg allergy and ask staff to check whether a dish contains eggs.

Mika and Alex ask about eggs in a restaurant dish. This is a learning aid, not a guarantee of allergy safety.

Content status: Safety-sensitive Japanese: AI-written and not native-speaker-verified. Confirm wording with a qualified native speaker before relying on it for an actual allergy.

Watch this lesson

Watch Mika and Alex act out the dialogue, then practise the phrases below.

Dialogue

  • Mika
    すみません！たまごの アレルギーが あります。
    Sumimasen! Tamago no arerugii ga arimasu.
    Excuse me! I'm allergic to eggs.
  • Alex
    この りょうりに たまごが はいっていますか？
    Kono ryouri ni tamago ga haitte imasu ka?
    Does this dish contain eggs?
  • Mika
    かくにんして ください。
    Kakunin shite kudasai.
    Please check.
  • Alex
    はい、かくにんします。
    Hai, kakunin shimasu.
    Yes, I'll check.

Vocabulary

  • すみませんsumimasenexcuse me / sorry; polite way to get attention
  • 卵 (たまご)tamagoegg
  • のnolinks nouns; here, “egg allergy”
  • アレルギーarerugiiallergy
  • がgasubject marker
  • ありますarimasuthere is / have; used in this expression for having an allergy
  • このkonothis (comes before a noun)
  • 料理 (りょうり)ryouridish / food / cooking
  • にniparticle marking where something is present in this sentence
  • 入っています (はいっています)haitte imasuis contained / has inside
  • かkaquestion particle
  • 確認して (かくにんして)kakunin shitecheck / confirm (te-form of 確認する)
  • くださいkudasaiplease do … / please give me …
  • はいhaiyes
  • 確認します (かくにんします)kakunin shimasuwill check / confirm (polite)

Grammar

Noun + の + allergy

たまごの アレルギーがあります literally means “there is an egg allergy,” used to communicate “I have an egg allergy.” A native speaker should confirm the most natural wording for a serious allergy.

この 料理に たまごが 入っていますか？

This asks whether eggs are in this dish. に marks the dish as the place where the ingredient may be present, and が marks たまご as the subject of 入っています.

Te-form + ください for a request

確認して ください means “please check.” 確認して is the te-form of 確認する (to check/confirm), followed by ください for a polite request.

か at the end of a sentence

はいっていますか turns the statement into a polite yes/no question: “Does it contain eggs?”

Notes

  • IMPORTANT: This phrase and the dialogue have not been verified by a native speaker and must not be treated as a guarantee that food is safe. For a real allergy, use a professionally reviewed allergy card, state the allergy clearly, ask staff to check ingredients and cross-contact with the kitchen, and do not eat if the answer is uncertain.
  • The source script uses たまご, アレルギー, and かくにん in hiragana/katakana exactly as shown. The website preserves the dialogue; kanji forms are provided only in the vocabulary list.
  • This content is AI-written and has not been reviewed by a native speaker. Native-speaker review is required before presenting it as reliable emergency language.
