Lesson 7 · Introducing family

Talking About Family

Goal: Say who is in your family.

Mika introduces her family to Alex.

Dialogue

  • Mika
    これはわたしのかぞくです。
    Kore wa watashi no kazoku desu.
    This is my family.
  • Alex
    そうですか。
    Sou desu ka.
    I see.
  • Mika
    こちらはあねです。
    Kochira wa ane desu.
    This is my older sister.
  • Mika
    こちらはおとうとです。
    Kochira wa otouto desu.
    This is my younger brother.

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • かぞく (かぞく)kazokufamily
  • わたし (わたし)watashiI / me
  • あね (あね)aneolder sister (your own)
  • おとうと (おとうと)otoutoyounger brother

Grammar

The particle の

の connects nouns and can show possession: わたしのかぞく means my family.

Notes

  • Japanese family terms can change when speaking about your own family versus someone else’s.
