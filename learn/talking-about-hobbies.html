Lesson 11 · Likes and interests

Talking About Hobbies

Goal: Say what you like and ask about another person’s interests.

Mika and Alex compare their interests.

Dialogue

  • Mika
    おんがくがすきです。
    Ongaku ga suki desu.
    I like music.
  • Alex
    わたしもすきです！
    Watashi mo suki desu!
    I like it too!
  • Mika
    アニメがすきですか？
    Anime ga suki desu ka?
    Do you like anime?
  • Alex
    はい、だいすきです！
    Hai, daisuki desu!
    Yes, I love it!

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • おんがく (おんがく)ongakumusic
  • すき (すき)sukilike
  • だいすき (だいすき)daisukilove / really like
  • アニメ (アニメ)animeanime

Grammar

X がすきです

Use X がすきです to say you like X. Use も instead of が to mean also or too.

Notes

  • You can replace アニメ with another hobby or interest.
