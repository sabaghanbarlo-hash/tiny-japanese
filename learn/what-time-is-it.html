Lesson 9 · Asking the time

What Time Is It?

Goal: Ask what time it is and say a simple hour.

Alex asks Mika the time.

Watch this lesson

Watch Mika and Alex act out the dialogue, then practise the phrases below.

Dialogue

  • Alex
    いま、なんじですか？
    Ima, nanji desu ka?
    What time is it now?
  • Mika
    さんじです。
    Sanji desu.
    It is three o’clock.
  • Alex
    ありがとうございます。
    Arigatou gozaimasu.
    Thank you very much.
  • Mika
    どういたしまして。
    Dou itashimashite.
    You’re welcome.

Tip: untick Romaji and English to test yourself, then tick them to check.

Vocabulary

  • いま (いま)imanow
  • なんじ (なんじ)nanjiwhat time
  • さんじ (さんじ)sanjithree o’clock

Grammar

Numbers + じ

Put a number before じ to say the hour; さんじ means three o’clock.

Notes

  • Some Japanese hour readings are irregular; learn them one at a time.
